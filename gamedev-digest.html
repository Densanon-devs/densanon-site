Draft of new EU law proposes sweeping restrictions on online games

GamesIndustry.biz
  • The EU Commission published a draft of the proposed EU KIDS Act targeting online platforms, including games.
  • The most controversial provisions — a ban for under-13s and restrictions for under-15s — primarily target social networks and video-sharing platforms.
  • Other provisions in the draft would still apply to online games, meaning studios operating in the EU could face new compliance requirements if it passes.
