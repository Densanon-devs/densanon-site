Entire Quixel Megascans Library Goes Free ...Again

Game From Scratch
  • Epic says the full Quixel Megascans library, over 23,000 assets, is free on Fab from November 5 to November 12; listings update during the window, so some may not show as free right away.
  • Epic also updated the terms so AI models can train on Megascans and Megaplants; buyers from January 1, 2025 onward are refunded to their Epic balance and keep the license with the new AI rights.
  • The author found no updated asset license or Fab EULA yet, so the new terms may appear later.
