Electronic Arts completes $55bn acquisition; CEO announces senior leadership changes

GamesIndustry.biz
  • EA has officially completed its $55 billion leveraged buyout, with Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund now the majority owner alongside Silver Lake and Jared Kushner's Affinity Partners.
  • The close comes with senior leadership changes announced by the CEO, signaling an executive shake-up as EA shifts from a public company to a PIF-controlled entity.

CNA - It's C++ XNA

Game From Scratch
  • CNA is a new C++ port of the XNA API, extending the life of a framework Microsoft abandoned years ago but that MonoGame and FNA have kept alive in C#.
  • Bringing XNA's API to C++ gives developers XNA's simplicity without depending on the .NET/Mono runtime.
