Godot adoption is rising: what are devs getting out of the engine?

Game Developer
  • 12% of developers now name Godot their primary engine, an 8-point year-over-year jump, and Godot took 47% of entries in a recent GMTK game jam survey, ending Unity's 9-year run as the most-used jam engine.
  • Developers cite the debugger as a standout: pausing on a breakpoint doesn't suspend the whole engine, which keeps iteration fast compared to competitors.
  • The composition-focused node model and a readable, well-documented open-source codebase are repeatedly cited as reasons teams -- including Slay the Spire's developers -- are shipping commercial titles on it.

Free Blender Tool For Procedural Spider Walks

80 Level
  • The free 'Spider Walker' Blender add-on (Blender 5.2 LTS, on GitHub) procedurally generates multi-leg locomotion: it tracks the body bone's velocity, predicts footfall targets ahead of travel direction, raycasts to the ground for placement, and steps legs sequentially to avoid unnatural synchronized movement.
  • Includes a bake operator to convert the live procedural animation into keyframed actions for manual refinement -- a rigging starting point rather than a final-pass solution.
  • The author notes parts of the code were developed with AI assistance, a small but telling sign of AI-assisted tool development becoming normal among solo tool authors.

Unity's Stylized Water 3 Is Getting Spline-Based River Modeler Tool

80 Level
  • The rebuilt River Modeler tool for Stylized Water 3 uses Unity's native Spline system and runs on the Job System plus Burst compiler for fast rebuilds and interactive editing.
  • Rivers are divided into segments for performance at scale, with automatic blending at intersections for branching rivers and automatic VFX/SFX integration including 3D audio for streams and cascades.
  • It's a full rebuild, not an in-place upgrade -- existing Stylized Water 3 users will need to migrate rather than patch, though a discount is planned.

How Dark and Darker's Devs Used Aura to Build a Major Feature in Half the Time

80 Level
  • IRONMACE used 'Aura,' an AI coding assistant, to let a non-technical designer (president Terence Park) build Dark and Darker's first proper tutorial system directly against the codebase.
