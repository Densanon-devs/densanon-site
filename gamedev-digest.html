Unreal Engine 6 will merge UE5 and UEFN into 'a single, unified engine'

Game Developer
  • Epic is unifying Unreal Engine 5 and the Fortnite-focused UEFN into one engine, converging AAA and user-generated-content tooling under a single programming model.
  • The new version ships cross-game interoperability tools — assets and logic built in one project can be shared or migrated across games.
  • Generative AI pipeline features are baked in at the engine level, meaning developers will need to form a deliberate adoption stance early rather than opt in later.
