The Future of Unreal Engine 6

Game From Scratch
  • Details from Unreal Fest Tokyo 2026 say Blueprints and Actors will be removed entirely in UE6 rather than deprecated, and the C++ Gameplay Framework is being abolished.
  • Gameplay logic moves fully to Verse and Scene Graph, while C++ stays for core systems such as rendering and physics; AI-powered tools are planned to convert Blueprint and C++ projects to Verse.
  • UE6 Early Access is targeted for the end of 2027, and projects in development can stay on the UE5 branch, including UE5.9 and later 5.x releases.

How Ares Interactive runs live ops without waiting on client updates

Unity Blog
  • Baseball Hits 26 uses a custom remote-config system with 50+ configuration domains; changes are versioned, logged, rollback-capable and applied by servers within seconds without a restart.
  • The Walking Dead: Aftermath publishes spreadsheet-authored content as remote bundles that carry a hash, a checksum and the oldest compatible client version, so live fixes can ship in about 15 minutes.
  • Weekly-changing content goes in bundles, while art and code still require a build.

Valve warns of slower Steam approvals, possibly due to AI game surge

Game Developer
  • Valve normally aims to test builds in 3-5 days but says it is currently taking up to 12 business days, citing a significant increase in store page and build review submissions.
  • Valve says much of the delay comes from re-reviewing games; per Kotaku, some developers waited 2-3 weeks and missed planned launch dates.
  • Valve does not attribute the surge to AI games; the article links it to Steam Next Fest submissions and Itch.io data on AI-driven games.

Ubisoft among the bidders for Arkane as Xbox looks to sell the studio

Game Developer
  • Bloomberg reported on November 3, 2026 that Arkane Lyon is taking multiple bids and no deal is close; Ubisoft's terms were not reported.
  • Options include a sale to Ubisoft, a management buyout similar to Double Fine and Undead Labs, or closure, which Microsoft has not ruled out.
  • French law requires consultation with the Social and Economic Committee before large-scale layoffs, which affects timing.
