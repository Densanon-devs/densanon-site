Optimizing Deep Rock Galactic Survivor for mobile

Unity Blog
  • Piktiv's mobile port replaced pathfinding with flow field navigation to handle large numbers of enemies efficiently on mobile hardware.

Interview: How Cyberpunk 2077's Night City Was Built Almost Entirely by Hand

80 Level
  • CD Projekt Red's art directors say Night City was built almost entirely by hand rather than through heavy proceduralization.
  • The first-person camera perspective directly shaped decisions about environment scale and level of detail, since players view the city at eye level.
