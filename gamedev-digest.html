What Rami Ismail learned with his return to game development

Game Developer
  • After about six years away, Ismail is back with a turn-based strategy game, Australia Did It: End-of-the-World Railway Defense Business, backed by publisher Mystic Forge.
  • He used a loose, gig-like team of freelancers and Aesthetician Labs that will disband after launch, which he sees as a way to take creative risks in a risk-averse industry.
  • The core mechanics took four to five months to feel right, and the team built a 'progression checker' to help players gauge how they are doing.
