Huion Kamvas 22 (Gen 3): Affordable Entry-Level Pen Display for 2D, 3D & Video Editing

80 Level
  • A 22-inch entry-level pen display aimed at artists who want one panel that handles sketching, 3D work and timeline scrubbing rather than a dedicated tablet plus a separate monitor.
  • The framing in the review is the interesting part — it is sold as a second monitor first and a drawing surface second, which is how most small studios actually end up using these.
  • For solo devs and small teams, a 22-inch class display is the size where a pen surface stops being a peripheral and starts replacing a monitor, changing the cost comparison entirely.
  • Worth a look if you are speccing an art workstation on a budget; the Gen 3 refresh mostly competes on price rather than on new capability.
