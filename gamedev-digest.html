Splinter Cell designer says modern lighting has made stealth games harder to read

Game Developer
  • Clint Hocking identifies ambient occlusion and physically-based lighting as double-edged: they look better but destroy the high-contrast silhouette reads that stealth gameplay depends on.
  • Classic stealth games used deliberately stylized, low-realism lighting to communicate safe shadow vs exposed at a glance — modern PBR pipelines make that binary harder to maintain.
  • Practical implication: stealth games may need explicit post-process or material overrides to preserve gameplay-legible darkness zones independent of the art lighting pass.
