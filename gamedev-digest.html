Nvidia Acquires Open-Source AI Platform Hugging Face for $12.9bn

GamesIndustry.biz
  • Nvidia is acquiring Hugging Face for $12.93 billion, its second-largest purchase ever after the $20 billion Groq asset buy, putting the dominant hub for open model and dataset distribution (18M+ developers, 3M+ models, 500K+ datasets) under a single hardware vendor's control.
  • Jensen Huang has publicly committed that Hugging Face will "remain an open platform for the entire AI ecosystem," but the deal is expected to close in H1 2027 pending regulatory approval, so terms could still shift.
  • With 200,000+ companies already using Hugging Face to discover and deploy models, consolidation under Nvidia is worth tracking for anyone leaning on HF-hosted GGUF or safetensors checkpoints for local-first inference tooling.

Xbox Puts Monthly Limits on Cloud Gaming for Game Pass Subscribers

80 Level
  • Starting November 2026, Xbox Game Pass caps cloud gaming hours by tier: 15 hours/month on Ultimate, 10 on Premium, and 5 on Essential, turning a previously unlimited perk into a metered one.
  • Extra hours become purchasable a la carte through the Xbox Store, and Microsoft is also opening cloud gaming to non-Game Pass subscribers as a standalone paid purchase, a monetization expansion framed as a cost-control measure.
  • Microsoft says only about 4% of subscribers will actually hit the new caps, citing rising cloud infrastructure costs, while Sony still offers unlimited cloud streaming and Nvidia's GeForce NOW caps at a much higher 100 hours/month.

Steam Wishlists by the Numbers: What Actually Gets Wishlisted

80 Level
  • GEM Capital's analysis of Steam's 100 most-wishlisted games found indie titles holding their own against AAA: 50 indies pull 46M total wishlists (910k average) versus 50 AAA games at 48M (955k average), a near dead heat.
  • Survival/Adventure is the strongest genre by per-game average (1.17M wishlists across 9 games, 23% of all indie wishlists), and older announcements dominate: 31 of the top 50 indies were revealed before 2025 and hold roughly 70% of indie wishlist share, averaging 1.2M versus just 704k for 2025 releases.
  • A strong announcement trailer averages 322k wishlists in its first 30 days versus just 81k for a weak one, a 4x difference, while Steam Next Fest participation (190k average) and direct YouTube uploads (175k average) both outperform most paid showcases for wishlist conversion.

Interview: How War for Westeros Turns Game of Thrones Into an Epic RTS

80 Level
  • PlaySide built Game of Thrones: War for Westeros on Unreal Engine 5 with a full DCC pipeline (Maya modeling, ZBrush sculpting, Substance 3D Painter texturing, Houdini procedural generation) plus a custom VAMP vertex-animation material pipeline built specifically to render large unit counts efficiently in an RTS.
  • Director Ryan McMahon deliberately keeps politics out of skirmish matches, since 10-40 minute sessions are too short for meaningful intrigue, and confines alliance-building and betrayal mechanics to the single-player sandbox campaign instead.
  • Factions (Starks, Lannisters, Targaryens) diverge in unit types, building structures, economy, and progression rather than being cosmetic reskins of the same kit, the kind of asymmetric-faction discipline directly relevant to a tactics game distinguishing its own factions or decks.

Blender Studio Opens OVERGROWN's Free Behind-the-Scenes Archive

80 Level
  • Blender Studio opened its normally subscriber-only OVERGROWN production archive for free during a limited-time "Open Doors Week," releasing six full production logs covering rigging and fur setup, shading, animation breakdowns, water effects, and final compositing for a stylized, illustrated look.

Book Character to a 3D Piece: Sculpting Human-Tree Character in ZBrush and Maya

80 Level
  • Andrea Buitrago's ZBrush-to-Maya pipeline leans on ZRemesher to hit target polygon counts without manual retopology, then does a quick auto-unwrap before export, a fast path from sculpt to a render-ready mesh worth borrowing for character work.
  • UVs get organized into separate UDIMs by required texture definition level, and Substance 3D Painter handles all texturing, including strategic ambient-occlusion painting (lighter and rougher at part seams) to sell a stone or marble material.
  • The full piece, from sculpt through final Arnold render with two separate light rigs (one for the hero shot, one for a 360-degree turnaround), took about two months of part-time work, a concrete benchmark for scoping a single stylized character asset.

This Web Tool Lets You Create 360-Degree Illustrations

80 Level
  • JUNOTA's free web tool Gururi Paint lets an artist sketch directly inside a 360-degree panoramic canvas (hold Space and drag to look around, hold Z and drag to zoom) rather than painting a flat equirectangular image and hoping it wraps correctly.
  • It ships the basics needed for real background work, including brush, fill, eyedropper, layers, and adjustable canvas and grid settings, making it a lightweight option for blocking out environment concepts or skybox-style backdrops before committing to a full 3D scene.
  • A companion gallery site, Gururi Watch, lets creators publish finished 360-degree pieces for others to view in-browser, a fast environment-concept sharing loop for a small team.
