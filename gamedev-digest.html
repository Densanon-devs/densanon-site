The hangover kicks in after a decade of games-as-a-service obsession

GamesIndustry.biz
  • Q1 financial results are surfacing the true cost of GaaS over-investment: studios that went all-in on live service pipelines are now booking expensive, public failures.

Mobile gaming has a new, and growing, measurement problem

GamesIndustry.biz
  • Google cut its Play Store fee to 20% — or 15% if developers integrate its AI assistant, cloud saves, and achievements — tying the best discount directly to platform lock-in via Google AI adoption.
  • The deeper issue the piece exposes: the industry lacks reliable cross-store attribution, so developers often don't know which storefront actually drives revenue.
  • The 15% tier is a trojan horse — studios trading short-term margin gains for deep Google AI integration will find it structurally harder to stay portable across platforms later.
