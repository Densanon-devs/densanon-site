In This First-Person Horror, Eye Exam Pulls You Into Backrooms-Style House

80 Level
  • Farsight from Studio Noori is an upcoming Unity-powered first-person horror game in which you play a 12-year-old pulled into a Backrooms-style house inside an eye-exam machine.
  • Gameplay leans on perception tools: peeking through keyholes, binoculars, an unreliable walkie-talkie, and a 90s-style handheld camera that plays mini cartridge games and takes low-res photos revealing hidden things.
  • A Steam wishlist page is linked, but no release date or confirmed platform list has been announced.
