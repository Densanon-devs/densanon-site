Microsoft raises price of Xbox consoles once again, sunsets 2TB model

Game Developer
  • The entry-level Xbox will now cost $499.99, with the 512 GB model rising $100 and 1 TB rising $150 effective August 2.
  • Microsoft cites a 'components crisis' — specifically increased costs for RAM and NAND storage — as the driver behind the hikes.
  • The 2TB SKU is being discontinued entirely, narrowing the console lineup and potentially impacting game install-size expectations for devs targeting Xbox.

Micron says chip shortage will "improve gradually" in 2028

GamesIndustry.biz
  • Micron CEO Sanjay Mehrotra confirmed the AI-driven global RAM and NAND shortage will persist through 2027, with only gradual improvement in 2028.
  • This supply constraint is the root cause behind the Xbox price hikes and will continue to pressure console and PC hardware costs for developers and consumers.
  • Studios planning hardware-dependent features (large install sizes, high VRAM demands) should factor prolonged component scarcity into their 2026-2028 roadmaps.
