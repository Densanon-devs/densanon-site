Game Dev Morning Digest

Layoffs dominate today's roundup: Double Fine cut 23 roles (25% of its workforce) right after splitting from Xbox, and a new industry tracker now projects 14,259 total 2026 job losses, up 78% from its original forecast — even as EA's CEO collected a $38.6M pay package in the same year his studios cut staff and leaned harder into GenAI. Financials tell a mixed story elsewhere: Krafton's Q2 revenue jumped 94.9% to $889.1M on Subnautica and PUBG, Resident Evil Requiem crossed 8M units sold, and the UK Games Fund handed out its largest-ever indie grant cohort. On the technical side, Unity makes the business case for D2C webshops, EverQuest Legends' team discusses recreating a classic MMORPG's visual identity, and two Blender tutorials cover procedural fur/feathers and constraint-based rigging.
