Report: Silver Lake and the Saudi Arabia PIF hold talks with Unity to explore acquisition

Game Developer
  • Reuters reports that Unity is in talks with Silver Lake and Saudi Arabia's Private Investment Fund about a possible acquisition; Unity has not confirmed it and no deal has been announced.
  • According to Reuters, Silver Lake already owns about 8% of Unity, and sources say any deal would be a leveraged buyout similar to the Electronic Arts takeover, though the figures are unverified.

How SEED renders a world players never stop building

Unity Blog
  • Klang Games' SEED is a server-authoritative MMO sandbox, and its largest player society has 172 buildings and about 190,000 modules, rendered with BatchRendererGroup, the C# Job System and Burst.
  • On PC the game was CPU-bound, and disabling the animation portion of LOD cross-fading restored instanced draw calls by letting objects use the GPU Resident Drawer path.
  • On mobile, memory was the main constraint, and switching to Mesh LOD, introduced in Unity 6.2, was described as a crucial step in making the world renderable.

Godot Showcase - Sovereign Tower

Godot Engine
  • Sovereign Tower is a narrative round table management RPG from Wild Wits Games, released on Steam for Windows and Linux.
  • The team says its time-rewind mechanic was the biggest technical challenge because it had to track many narrative variables and characters.
  • Lead programmer Paul Mozet says he would like Godot to support multiple lockable file system and inspector tabs, as Unity does.

SAG-AFTRA introduces new process for hiring union actors in game pitches

Game Developer
  • SAG-AFTRA released the Pre-Funding Pitch Interactive Media Agreement, a framework for using union actors in vertical slices, demos and other content needed to pitch games.
  • Projects are ineligible if more than 30% of their initial production funding is already secured or if they will be commercially released.
  • The article suggests the rules may be meant to discourage generative AI voices in early development.
