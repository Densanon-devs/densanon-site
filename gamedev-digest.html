The EU KIDS Act: What game developers need to know

GamesIndustry.biz
  • The EU Commission published a draft of the KIDS Act (Keeping Internet Digital Spaces Accountable and Trustworthy) on September 17.
  • Its toughest provisions — a ban on under-13 access and added restrictions — are aimed at social networks and video-sharing platforms rather than games directly.
  • Developers with social or user-generated-content sharing features should still watch how "digital space" gets defined as the draft moves forward.

Try This Film Emulation Tool For Unreal Engine 5 Cinematics

80 Level
  • True Lens, a Blueprint tool by Scott Jardine, packs 14 film-photography effects — anamorphic bokeh, halation, grain, chromatic aberration, lens dirt/flares, gate weave, and more — into UE5.
  • Fully keyframable in Sequencer and works with both Deferred and Path Traced rendering.
  • Runs through a single Blueprint-managed Post Process Volume with saveable presets, cutting out the need for external post software.

Desirable Patina: How to Make Realistic Rust in 3D

80 Level
  • Loic Anquetil builds procedural rust materials in Substance 3D Designer, starting from studying rust's real physical structure rather than jumping straight into node graphs.
  • Custom reference photography captures subtle tonal variation that stock references miss.
  • The approach layers grayscale noise for streaks and erosion, reverse-engineers Designer's built-in noise nodes, and stacks corrosion stages like peeling paint and grime to tell a material's age.

Recreating the Interior of a Cafe in Hong Kong Using UE5 & ZBrush

80 Level
  • Soyun Kang's Mido Cafe project mixes hard-surface (3ds Max/TurboSmooth) and organic (ZBrush decimation) prop workflows, unwrapped separately in 3ds Max and RizomUV.
  • Substance 3D Painter texturing layers metal bases with grunge and gradient dirt maps, as seen on the project's vintage fridge prop.
  • Lighting uses directional light as the key source plus window rectangle lights and spotlights, rendered with path tracing; the whole project took about 18 months.
