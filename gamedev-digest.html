Game Dev Morning Digest

A single-item day: at Meta Connect 2026 Meta announced Horizon Create and Horizon Studio, two prompt-driven AI "game engines" that turn text descriptions into 2D and 3D games for Facebook, Instagram and Horizon. GameFromScratch is openly scornful.

Engines & Tools

Meta Horizon Create and Horizon Studio “Game Engines”

Game From Scratch
  • Users describe a game in text and the tools generate a playable 2D or 3D game: environments, characters, mechanics and multiplayer support, which users can refine before publishing.
  • Published games go out through Facebook, Instagram and Meta's Horizon platform. Pricing and release date were not announced beyond "upcoming."
  • GameFromScratch calls it "AI-slop game generation" and questions whether it deserves the "game engine" label at all. The consolation it offers: the output stays inside Meta's feeds.
