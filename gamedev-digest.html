Roblox's reported user numbers will make it subject to additional EU legislation for regulating social networks

GamesIndustry.biz
  • Roblox's user base has reportedly grown large enough to trigger "very large online platform" (VLOP) classification under EU law, a designation historically reserved for social media giants.
  • This would make Roblox the first game to fall under the EU's stricter content-moderation and transparency obligations built for large social networks.
  • It signals regulators are starting to treat large-scale UGC game platforms the same as traditional social networks — a precedent other UGC-driven platforms should watch.
