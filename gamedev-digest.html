Experimental Stylized 3D Tribute To Spider-Man: Brand New Day

80 Level
  • Dan Eder, formerly Art Director on Invincible VS, sculpted the stylized 3D Spider-Man piece using ZBrush, Substance 3D Painter, and Marmoset Toolbag.
  • The piece blends cel-shaded comic rendering with photoreal lighting and textures, including outlines that pick up the colors of their surroundings instead of solid black.
  • Eder wove in Japanese text as language practice — notable given his track record of fan art crossing into official work, including Netflix licensing his Queen's Gambit piece.

Bring Me Hope [$20.00] [Role Playing] [Windows]

itch.io New Games
  • Bring Me Hope is a GameMaker-built, pixel-art souls-like RPG about love, loss, and family, following protagonist Alium through a world of echoes and memories.
  • Combat is built around parrying and dodging plus an environmental interaction system — fire spreads through grass, electricity conducts through wet enemies, and oil chains reactions.
  • Priced at $20 with a free demo and Steam key included, running on Windows (and Steam Deck via Proton); no generative AI was used in development.
