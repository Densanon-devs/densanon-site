Epic Games Store launches on iPhone in Japan

GamesIndustry.biz
  • Japan's Mobile Software Competition Act enabled this — it's the first major market where Epic can distribute iOS games outside the App Store, offering developers an alternative path with no Apple commission.
  • This is a live stress test for alternative app store viability: if Epic gains meaningful traction in Japan, expect more markets to legislate similar access and more storefronts to follow.
  • For iOS developers, this is the opening of a genuine alternative distribution channel within 2-3 years — the economics of mobile game publishing could shift materially.
