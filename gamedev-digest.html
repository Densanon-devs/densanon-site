'Do not': Rockstar outlines modding rules before GTA VI touches down

Game Developer
  • Rockstar's new modding guidelines, covering GTA V, Red Dead Redemption II, and the upcoming GTA VI, bar modders from mixing maps, characters, or storylines across different Rockstar titles or altering official canon.
  • The rules also prohibit real-money gambling mechanics, loot boxes, gacha systems, cryptocurrency, NFTs, and any in-game currency sales made outside Rockstar's own Creator Platform.
  • Modders cannot reuse Rockstar's voiceover recordings or use a real person's voice, image, or likeness without written permission.
  • Legitimate mod monetization must go through storefront providers Rockstar has specifically approved as part of the Creator Platform.

Breakdown: How to Create a Detailed ICA Dresden Folding Plate Camera

80 Level
  • 3D artist Rajan Verma detailed his workflow for modeling an ICA Dresden antique folding plate camera using Blender, Maya, ZBrush, Substance 3D Painter, and Marmoset Toolbag 5.
  • The pipeline moved from a blockout focused on proportions and silhouette to a high-poly pass with subtle imperfections, then an optimized low-poly model of roughly 25,000 triangles on a single 2K texture sheet.
  • Texturing emphasized material-specific wear: the leather got color variation and creasing while painted metal received layered damage concentrated at contact points and edges rather than random wear.
  • The project won first place in Sierra Division Academy's 'Back in Time: Antique Props Challenge,' a five-week contest capped at 25K triangles, one texture set, and no third-party assets.

OZARK: Creating a Dark Story-Driven 2.5D Action-Horror Game

80 Level
  • Indie studio Alter-Boy detailed development of OZARK, a 3-6 hour story-driven 2.5D action-horror game built in Unity, upgraded from a 2018-2019 build to Unity 2022 LTS on the Built-in Render Pipeline.
  • The fixed 2.5D camera forced the team to build separate aiming systems for controller versus mouse-and-keyboard once depth movement was allowed, and to design three distinct co-op camera modes.
  • The game is set in a Canadian location influenced by real sites like SNOLAB in Ontario, with environmental storytelling handled through grime, wear, and props rather than exposition.
  • The team used Blender and Cinema 4D for modeling, Substance Painter for texturing, and a mix of Mixamo, hand animation, and custom Vicon motion capture for animation.
