Industry & News

GTA 6 will launch at $79.99 and will be download only

GamesIndustry.biz
  • Grand Theft Auto 6 will retail at $79.99 — a $10 jump over the previous AAA standard and the highest launch price point for a mainstream console release to date.
  • Physical copies will be a download code in a box only, effectively ending disc-based distribution for this title and eliminating the used-game market.
  • The pricing move is likely to set a new industry benchmark, putting pressure on other major publishers to follow Rockstar's lead on both price and format.
  • Digital-only physical packaging reflects a broader industry push to standardize on account-bound licenses and reduce reliance on retail supply chains.

Valve admits Steam Machine is "more expensive" than it hoped

GamesIndustry.biz
  • Valve has publicly acknowledged that its Steam Machine hardware carries a higher cost than originally targeted — a recurring challenge for dedicated gaming hardware entrants.
  • The admission signals that SteamOS-based living room PCs are still struggling to compete on price against dedicated consoles like PlayStation and Xbox.
  • NVIDIA GPU support is reportedly coming, which may expand compatibility but is unlikely to reduce component costs.
  • Valve is allowing existing PCs to run Steam Machine mode, which could blunt consumer demand for the dedicated hardware unit.

Appcharge: Direct-to-consumer market worth around $17bn today

GamesIndustry.biz
  • New research from Appcharge estimates direct-to-consumer mobile game spending reached approximately $17 billion in 2025.

  • Regulatory pressure on app stores post-Epic v. Apple has accelerated developer investment in first-party DTC storefronts.

Resident Evil Creator Says Games Aren't Good If Players Just Watch Streams

80 Level
  • Shinji Mikami argues that a game has failed at its core design goal if players are satisfied watching a stream rather than playing it themselves.
  • The quote highlights a growing design tension: streaming boosts discovery but can reduce purchase motivation if the experience is fully consumable passively.
  • Mikami's framing implies that genuine interactivity and player agency — not just narrative — are the irreplaceable value proposition of games.
  • The comment arrives as many studios report declining sales for single-player games with high streaming viewership despite broad audience awareness.

Build Entire River Environments From Curves With This Blender Tool

80 Level
  • The Stylized River Generator for Blender lets artists define entire river paths using spline curves, dramatically speeding up environment layout vs. hand-modeled water assets.
  • Dynamic obstacle interactions allow the river mesh to react procedurally to rocks, banks, and other scene geometry placed in the environment.
  • Curve-driven procedural generation reduces the manual cleanup pass typically needed when assembling large water-based environments.

New Free Toolkit Released For Maya Animators

80 Level
  • Animo is a free Maya toolkit covering space switching automation, fast arc tracking, fast baking, and animation layer management in a single package.
  • Space switching automation alone eliminates a tedious multi-step manual process that animators repeat hundreds of times per shot.
  • Real-time arc tracking provides visual feedback on limb and prop motion paths, catching drift issues early in blocking rather than late in spline polish.
  • Being free lowers the barrier for small studios and freelancers who cannot afford commercial animation plugins with comparable feature sets.
