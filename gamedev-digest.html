Total consumer spending on games topped $60B in the US in 2025

Game Developer
  • US game market hit $60B+ in 2025, with over half of surveyed players making in-game purchases — a direct indicator that live-service and IAP models remain structurally dominant.
  • ESA data covers ages 5–90 with 212 million active players, meaning the addressable market is now effectively the entire US population with internet access.
  • Studios targeting niche demographics face a tide where the median consumer already expects to spend money post-launch, making premium-only pricing increasingly a niche choice.

ESA: 67% of Americans played video games for more than an hour in 2025

GamesIndustry.biz
  • 67% of Americans — roughly 212 million people ages 5 to 90 — played games for more than an hour in 2025, confirming gaming is now a mainstream daily habit rather than a niche hobby.
  • The breadth of the age range (5–90) strengthens the case for accessibility-first design and age-spanning UI/UX as table-stakes rather than optional features.
  • This penetration level means market growth must come from spend-per-player or new platform categories, not from expanding the player headcount alone.

PlayStation first-party sales see modest rebound after half-decade decline

GamesIndustry.biz
  • PlayStation first-party unit sales climbed from 28.9M (FY24) to 32.1M (FY25) — a partial recovery from the 58.4M peak in FY20, but still less than 55% of that high.
  • The persistent gap between PS5 install base and first-party software attach rates suggests players are shifting toward third-party and live-service titles over Sony exclusives.
  • For indie and mid-tier studios, declining first-party volume means less marketing noise from Sony, but also weaker platform-holder investment in ecosystem expansion.

Mina the Hollower sells 300,000 copies in three days

Game Developer
  • Yacht Club Games moved 300K units of Mina the Hollower in 72 hours, validating that a well-branded retro action title from a proven studio can still open at Shovel Knight-tier velocity.
  • The studio publicly stated the game's performance would 'make or break' them — demonstrating that even established indie studios operate with thin commercial margins on any single title.
  • At $20–$25 launch price this implies roughly $6–7.5M gross in three days before platform cuts, a concrete commercial target for similar-scope projects.

GamesIndustry.biz
  • Brendan Greene's PlayerUnknown Productions is halting Go Wayback development and reducing headcount after self-funding hit its limit — a case study in the risk of founder-financed experimental studios without external investment.

"Nobody's making games for the retired people" – The growing yet underserved market for grey gamers

GamesIndustry.biz
  • The first generation that grew up with games (now in their 50s–60s) is entering retirement with disposable income and established gaming habits — a segment most studios are not explicitly designing for.
  • Design adjustments for older players — larger UI text, reduced reaction-time demands, flexible session lengths — are parameter changes, not fundamental redesigns.
  • Indie developers building for older demographics face less competition and a potentially loyal, less price-sensitive customer base compared to the saturated 18–35 core market.

How to direct unconventional games like Control Resonant

Game Developer
  • Keeping a team aligned on a game with a novel or hybrid genre requires more explicit shared documentation of the 'feel' goal, not just feature specs — verbal alignment breaks down when there are no reference titles.
  • Directors working outside established genre templates must define the player experience in concrete, testable terms so QA and design can evaluate against intent rather than precedent.
  • The production challenge of unconventional games is less about technical implementation and more about maintaining creative coherence across departments when no comparable shipped title exists.

GamesIndustry.biz
  • European mobile studios generated €7.53B globally in 2025, with forecasts projecting €8B+ by 2028 — signaling continued headroom in mobile even as PC/console markets plateau.
