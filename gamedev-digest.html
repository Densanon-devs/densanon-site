Get This Free Blender Add-On That Makes Hair Meshes Thinner at the Ends

80 Level
  • MT Hair Taper, a free add-on by MOTOMODEL, narrows the tips of hair-strand meshes while keeping their length and shape.
  • It needs grid-like quad meshes (flat plane bundles or closed cylinders). Triangle-heavy meshes and radial or irregular vertex flow are not supported.
  • It requires Blender 4.0+ and is free to download from MOTOMODEL's Booth page.

Check Out This Relaxing Brutalist City Builder Inspired by Tiny Glade

80 Level
  • Tiny Brutalism, from Jimjum Studios and built in Unity, is a cozy builder where 20+ blocks combine into procedurally generated concrete structures.
  • There are no goals. Players can decorate interiors, explore their builds in first person with parkour, and watch rain, fog and a day/night cycle.
  • It is still in development with a Steam wishlist page and no release date.
