Roblox will allow devs to release games as standalone apps on other stores

Game Developer
  • Announced at the Roblox Developers Conference (September 2026), select developers will be able to publish standalone builds of their games on Steam, the Apple App Store, Google Play, and browsers outside the main Roblox client.
  • Roblox says these standalone releases keep 'the same revenue-share economics' as the Roblox app, including a higher DevEx payout rate (standard rate is $0.0038 per Robux) for age-verified 18+ users in the US.
  • No launch date was given for external submissions; the same event added offline single-player support, 2D tooling (orthographic camera, sprite sheets), and a 'Roblox Wallet' for direct currency payouts.

Valve's Steam Frame pricing starts at $1,059

Game Developer
  • Steam Frame launches at $1,059 for the 256GB standalone SteamOS headset and $1,299 for the 1TB model, with a $29 power supply and $59 accessory kit sold separately.
  • Valve is opening developer kits for the headset through the Steam Partner dashboard for studios building native VR titles.
  • The price sits well above Meta's Quest ($349 for 512GB); Valve points to its policy of not subsidizing hardware plus a memory-chip shortage driven by AI-industry supply deals with Samsung and SK Hynix.

Valve Explains Why Steam Frame Uses Half-Life: Alyx Instead of a New VR Game

80 Level
  • SteamOS developer Pierre-Loup Griffais said Valve bundled 2020's Half-Life: Alyx rather than build a new VR game because most buyers 'maybe haven't played it,' and the priority was making the whole Steam catalog run well on the headset, not shipping one showcase title.
  • Programmer Robin Walker and engineer Autumn Aston said porting Alyx surfaced driver bugs and performance fixes that benefit every other title on Steam Frame, calling it 'a great way to get everything up to scratch.'
  • Lottery-style pre-order sign-ups closed September 17 at 10am PT, with the first purchase invites going out September 18.

Bungie is overhauling Marathon from its original extraction shooter vision

Game Developer
  • Bungie is adding a permanent PvE mode called 'Symbiosis,' launching December 8, 2026, and dropping Marathon's strict seasonal content schedule for more flexible delivery.
  • The studio says fixing the extraction shooter 'requires us to change both what we're building and how we deliver it,' aiming for a 'more alien and dynamic' game based on player feedback for more depth and progression.
  • The overhaul follows Sony's June 2026 layoff of 292 Bungie employees and the halt of Destiny 2 live development; a fuller roadmap is due in a blog later this month, with changes continuing through March 2027.

Bohemia Interactive acquires minority stake in Everwind developer Enjoy Studio

Game Developer
  • Bohemia Interactive took an undisclosed minority stake in Polish developer Enjoy Studio - its first investment in a Polish studio - after the relationship began through Bohemia's Incubator program in 2024.
  • Enjoy's Everwind, a sandbox survival RPG published by Bohemia, sold over 300,000 copies within six months of entering early access.
  • Bohemia CEO Marek Spanel framed it as a bet on the studio's talent generally rather than a single title: 'We want to make the best games we can together and bring them to as many players as possible.'

Moss developer Polyarc has shut down

Game Developer
  • The Seattle-based VR specialist, maker of the Moss franchise, closed after nearly 12 years, citing financing challenges that first forced a significant downsizing in March 2026 before the full shutdown in September.
  • Roughly 30 people lost their jobs across the two rounds.
  • The studio's farewell statement: 'After nearly 12 years riding the joyous rollercoaster of emotions that is making video games, our time together has come to an end.'

Worker-run studio KO_OP confirms layoffs

Game Developer
  • The worker-owned studio behind Goodbye Volcano High and Young Suns cut 13 roles, including team leads and elected executives, in a decision made democratically by its members.
  • KO_OP said the funding landscape has 'changed radically' and it has been unable to secure new-project financing while still delivering on current engagements.
  • Affected staff keep profit shares from Young Suns, which recently launched on Xbox and is headed to more platforms; the studio says it will rehire when funding allows.

Wardogs lead says the studio won't hire people who decry crunch on social media

Game Developer
  • Bulkhead Interactive CEO Joe Brammer said the studio screens candidates' social media and steers away from hiring people who publicly criticize crunch: 'If you're constantly tweeting anti-crunch messaging... we know that and go, "Look, you're not going to be happy here."'
  • The comments follow Wardogs' Early Access launch on September 10, which sold 2 million copies in its first five days; Brammer frames crunch as offset by stock ownership and bonuses for some staff.
  • Bulkhead has run roughly 20% annual layoffs for six years, and equity isn't extended to all employees.
