Engines & Tools

Maintenance release: Godot 3.6.3

Godot Engine
  • Driven by platform requirement changes, not new features: Google Play now requires apps to target Android 16 (API level 36), and Xcode 27 raises the effective iOS minimum to 15.0, which also drops support for the long-obsolete 32-bit iOS export (unsupported since iOS 11).
  • Also fixes real cross-platform bugs -- Windows WASAPI audio crackling, a GDScript function-signature-hint bug, a Linux/BSD text-to-speech deadlock, and a broken iOS x86_64 simulator build -- plus updates bundled mbedTLS to 3.6.7 and libpng to 1.6.58.
  • 20 fixes from 16 contributors; the Godot team calls it "safe and recommended" with minimal impact for projects already on 3.6.2, available now via the archive downloads page and the online editor at editor.godotengine.org/3.6.3.stable.
