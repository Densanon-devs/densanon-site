Modeler Creates a Blender-Style Modeling Plugin for Roblox Studio

80 Level
  • The plugin, called Bloxender, was built by creator Dinonotdilo to bring Blender-style modeling tools directly into Roblox Studio, avoiding the export/import round trip to a separate 3D app.
  • It supports UV mapping, texture painting, and vertex color/alpha editing, and includes an AI agent that can read model state and propose modifications for user approval.
  • It ships as a free Lite edition and a full beta edition, available through the Roblox plugin store.
