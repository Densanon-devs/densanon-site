Don't Nod considering laying off 90 workers in France

Game Developer
  • Don't Nod is planning to cut 90 jobs in France after H1 2026 operating revenue fell 56% year-on-year and EBITDA posted a loss of EUR 4.3 million.
  • The studio warns it may lack runway to operate past January 31, 2027 without new external financing, after Tencent withdrew planned investment earlier this year.
  • The restructuring consolidates French operations around a single production line instead of parallel teams; the board approved it and management is now negotiating with unions.

FuturLab cuts staff after cancelling non-Powerwash Simulator title

GamesIndustry.biz
  • FuturLab laid off 7 employees after cancelling an unannounced project that had been in pre-production for 2-3 years, once projected costs exceeded projected revenue.
  • CEO Kirsty Rigden confirmed the cut is unrelated to PowerWash Simulator 2; the affected staff were not working on that game.
  • Internal messaging reportedly avoided the word 'redundancy', referring to affected staff as 'the leavers' during the restructuring meeting.

Capcom says it will focus on reviving dormant IPs after Onimusha: Way of the Sword's breakout launch

GamesIndustry.biz
  • Onimusha: Way of the Sword sold over 1 million copies on day one, pushing the franchise past 10 million lifetime units after a 20-year gap since its last mainline entry.
  • Capcom named seven dormant franchises as revival candidates at a May 2026 investor meeting: Mega Man, Devil May Cry, Onimusha, Dead Rising, Ace Attorney, Dragon's Dogma, and Okami.
  • Mega Man: Dual Override is already confirmed for 2027 and a new Okami sequel is in development, so the strategy has moved from statement to shipped roadmap.

Sega cancelled its "risky" Super Game project as it "would have to grow enormously to match the scale of the service"

GamesIndustry.biz
  • Sega scrapped Super Game, the live-service AAA initiative announced in 2021, after budgeting close to $1 billion combined for internal development and acquisitions.
  • President/COO Shuji Utsumi said analysis showed the required operational scale, content throughput, and live-ops commitment was far bigger than the studio could responsibly commit to right now.
  • Sega isn't exiting live-service entirely: Total War: Warhammer 40,000, Phantasy Star Online 2: New Genesis, and Sega Football Club Champions remain in active development.

"It's not sexy, but it's one of the most successful genres on Steam" - Stronghold developer Firefly launches publishing label to serve the vast strategy market

GamesIndustry.biz
  • Firefly Studios (Stronghold) launched WuselFaktory, a publishing label for external teams making strategy games, after 25 years of self-publishing only its own titles.
  • The first release under the label is Fool King from first-time studio Loot Donkey, a tactical autobattler with asynchronous PvP heading to Steam Early Access.
  • The label's name borrows 'Wuselfaktor', a German strategy/city-sim term for the charm of watching tiny characters scurry through their tasks.

Niko Partners: Chinese games market surpasses $50bn for the first time in 2025

GamesIndustry.biz
  • China's PC, mobile, and console games revenue hit $51.8 billion in 2025, up 5.4% year-on-year, with 2026 projected at $53.9 billion and $59.8 billion by 2030.
  • Annual ARPU crossed $70 for the first time in 2025 and is forecast to reach $77.68 by 2030, with the player base expected to hit 769 million by 2030.
  • 41% of Chinese players now discover new games through short-form video apps, making it the single most common discovery channel Niko tracked.

Arm explains its mobile-first AI-reconstruction technology, which takes a different tack from the "black box" approach of DLSS 5

GamesIndustry.biz
  • Arm unveiled its Neural Technology stack at Arm Everywhere China, built on Neural Super Sampling, Neural Super Sampling and Denoising, and Neural Frame Rate Upscaling.
  • Claimed numbers: Neural Super Sampling upscales 540p to 1080p, and Neural Frame Rate Upscaling takes 30 FPS to 60 FPS via on-device frame generation.
  • The stack ships alongside the Mali G2-Ultra NX, Arm's first AI-native Mali GPU, paired with CSS for Mobile 2; Arm is pitching it as a more transparent alternative to DLSS 5's closed pipeline.

80 Level

Simple & Low-Cost Hair Simulation & Deformation System

80 Level
  • Character artist Gerardo Castellanos built Simple Hair, part of the GRM Groom Toolkit for Houdini, using tetrahedron-based 3D structures instead of strand-level simulation.
  • It targets game-dev pain points directly: long sim times, collision failures, and grooms losing their authored style after simulation, aimed at secondary or background characters seen from a distance.
  • Still unreleased with no launch date; the existing GRM Groom Toolkit (GRM_hairGenClump, GRM_radiusOfInfluence) is already purchasable and hints at the toolset it will join.

Tiny Glade-Inspired Diorama Builder With Interactive Water

80 Level
  • Waterfalls Dream, from solo dev KingCakeTheFruity, drives its interactive water with a PB-MPM (particle-in-cell material point method) simulation for both water and aerated foam on a shared velocity grid.
  • Performance target: a 350K-particle simulation plus rendering from any camera angle at 120 FPS on a Mac M5 Pro with 24GB, using under 20MB of memory.
  • Players carve terrain in a 3D voxel world and watch water route itself through the result in real time; the dev plans to open-source the water sim once it's finished.
