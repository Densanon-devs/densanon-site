How Fireshine Games backed a winner with Far Far West

GamesIndustry.biz
  • Far Far West (chaotic cyberpunk cowboy roguelite) sold 250,000 units in its first 48 hours on Steam Early Access and crossed 1 million units within two weeks — a rare EA breakout.
  • Publisher Fireshine paired a small French studio (Evil Raptor) with focused marketing around a clear genre identity; the 'chaotic' positioning resonated strongly with the Vampire Survivors crowd.
  • Early Access is still viable as a launch strategy when the core loop is demonstrably fun and the genre positioning is specific rather than broad.

How Heathen Reimagines Diablo's Classic Dungeon-Crawling Formula in First Person

80 Level
  • The Heathen dev translated isometric ARPG design (loot density, room cadence, elite spawn budgets) to first-person by keeping the same underlying dungeon graph while changing the camera contract — a useful separation of genre rules from perspective.
  • Semi-procedural dungeon generation uses handcrafted room prefabs assembled by a procedural connector system, giving art direction control over feel while keeping layouts fresh across runs.
  • Built in Unity; the dev notes that first-person ARPGs must solve spatial audio and hitbox legibility problems that isometric games get for free from the top-down camera.

80 Level
