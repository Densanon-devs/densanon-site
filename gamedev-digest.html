Xbox to cap Cloud Gaming hours for Game Pass subscribers

Game Developer
  • Starting November 2026, monthly cloud gaming hours are capped by tier: 15 hrs for Ultimate, 10 hrs for Premium, 5 hrs for Essential.
  • Microsoft estimates only about 4% of Game Pass subscribers will actually hit the new limits.
  • Anyone who exceeds their cap (or isn't subscribed) can buy extra "playtime hours" a la carte through the Xbox Store.
  • Microsoft blames rising cloud infrastructure costs, while admitting the change is effectively a price increase for heavy cloud users.

Nexus Mods is acquiring database and analytics site SteamDB

Game Developer
  • Financial terms were undisclosed; SteamDB keeps its name, identity, and independent operation under Nexus Mods.

Apple faces £2bn lawsuit over implementation of App Tracking Transparency framework

GamesIndustry.biz
  • ATT Collective Action Limited filed a £2 billion claim with the UK Competition Appeal Tribunal on behalf of UK developers who earned ad revenue or spent on user acquisition via iOS.
  • The claim alleges Apple holds itself to looser consent standards for its own ads/data collection than the rules it imposes on third-party apps under ATT (launched with iOS 14.5 in 2021).
  • An Ampere Analysis researcher notes ATT hit mature, ad-dependent franchises like Angry Birds especially hard.
  • France and Italy have already fined Apple over ATT (combined north of £200m) and Germany settled with Apple in August 2026, giving this UK case regulatory precedent to lean on.

GTA 6 Netflix preview causes PlayStation and Xbox hardware sales to jump in the UK

GamesIndustry.biz
  • Nielsen IQ data shows UK PS5 sales rose 33% and Xbox Series X|S sales rose 34% in the week after the GTA 6 Netflix reveal; PS5 Pro is selling out nationwide.
  • Overall UK hardware sales are still down 14.4% year-on-year (PS5 down 32%), so analysts read this as a short-term bump rather than a trend reversal.

Sony and Microsoft request dismissal of consumer lawsuits over tariff refunds

GamesIndustry.biz
  • Both companies are asking courts to toss suits claiming refunds are owed now that the Supreme Court ruled the 2025 IEEPA tariffs illegal; Sony's motion is in California's Northern District, Microsoft's in Washington State federal court.
  • Sony argues its 2025 price hikes reflected multiple cost factors, not just tariffs, and notes it raised prices again even after the tariffs were struck down.
  • Sony separately told investors in July it expects a $508 million tariff refund from the government, which boosted Q1 operating income 37% — money it argues doesn't have to pass through to consumers.
  • Nintendo filed a similar dismissal motion in July; so far only Playdate maker Panic has voluntarily passed a tariff refund on to customers.

"This is the worst crash we've seen since the 1980s," says Tim Sweeney

GamesIndustry.biz
  • In Edge magazine issue 428, Sweeney says AAA costs plus a hardware component shortage (RAM/storage prices "quadrupling") are driving the worst downturn since the 1980s crash, with the supply crunch expected to last "the next three years."
  • He blames AI/data-center investment for outbidding the games industry for components.
  • Playable Worlds CEO Raph Koster's 30-year dataset (250 releases) shows AAA console/PC dev costs rising roughly 10x per decade — about $1M in the mid-90s, $10M in 2005, $100M in 2015; Sweeney says budgets now run $250-400M.
  • Former Tencent exec Amir Satvat says AI is already compressing team sizes, e.g. a would-be 50-60 person team shrinking to around 20.

Developing Tools for Large-Scale 3D Game Environments

80 Level
  • Jolan Huijskes built a custom Blender + UE5 toolset — a Geometry Nodes-based Fracture Generation tool (soon a free release), an Array Tool, a fracture-reconstruction tool, and PCG-based scattering with artist override — to build a full desert-biome environment for a fan project called Saros.
  • The fracture tool runs a three-stage pipeline: base fracturing via noise-displaced planes and booleans, interior detail via further noise displacement, then edge damage via remeshing and inflation.
  • The scattering tool deliberately mixes procedural placement with manual artist control via tagged Actor Arrays rather than going fully automated — a practical pattern for large open-world set dressing.

80 Level
  • Dwarf Fortress sold 160,000 copies in its first 24 hours on Steam in 2022, hit 500,000 within about a month, and has now passed 800,000 total, a milestone reached roughly two years ago.
