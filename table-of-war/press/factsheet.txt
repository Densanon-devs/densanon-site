SHORT DESCRIPTION
A tactics roguelike deckbuilder where you command an army, not a hero. Units move and fight on
their own action points; your deck calls in reinforcements, ordnance and orders. Every soldier has
a name, a rank, and exactly one life. Build the army. Read the board. Lose them anyway.

AT A GLANCE
- 3 commanders, a 4-act branching expedition, a permadeath roster
- 176 cards, 38 relics, 56 events, 9 bosses, 57 achievements, 12 ascension rungs
- Procedural non-rectangular battlefields, fog of war, zones of control, hazards, friendly fire
- Seeded runs and a Daily Commission everyone plays on the same seed
