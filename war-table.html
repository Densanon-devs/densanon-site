Medals Rewrite the Whole Run

A medal is not a stat bump on one soldier. Win one and it applies to every battle for the rest of the run — and if you take the same medal twice, it stacks.

Relic · Rank I
Close Air Support

Your Ordnance cards deal +3 damage. Every Air Strike, every Barrage, every shell you drop for the rest of the run.

Take a second one and it is +6. Build a run around ordnance and the medals compound into something the enemy has no answer to.
Cursed Relic
Necrosis

Your units cannot be healed. No medics, no repair bays, no mending of any kind — for the rest of the run.

Curses are run-wide too, and the numeric ones stack the same way: two Deserters is −2 troop cap, permanently. Some events hand you a relic and a curse in the same breath, and you have to decide whether the power is worth what it costs you.

Relics come from bosses, elites, shops and the events you gamble on. So do the curses.

Act I Ends With One of These

The demo runs the whole of Act I, and which commander waits at the end of it is fixed by your seed — so the two of them are two different fights, not two skins.

🎖 The Quartermaster
Defensive · Attrition

“You will break on my supply lines, Commander. I have more men than you have bullets.”

ENDLESS REINFORCEMENTS. Fresh troops muster every couple of turns. Race the clock and end it fast.
70 hp6 atk3 def range 2move 12x2 footprint
Its edge — Full Depots

Every unit it fields musters with +4 HP. It never charges you; it digs in behind an Artillery Emplacement and buries you in bodies.

It plays

Reinforce · Rifle Squad · Ambush x2 · Barrage · Incendiary · Build Turret — it holds the ground it has and shells you off yours.

🪓 The Berserker
Aggressive · Ramp

“The longer you live, the harder I hit. Tick. Tick. Tick.”

IT GROWS ANGRIER. Its attack climbs every single turn. Put it down FAST, before it starts one-shotting your line.
82 hp7 atk1 def range 1move 42 actions
Its edge — Blood Frenzy

Its whole warband deals +2 when attacking from an adjacent tile. Thin armour and a short reach, but it moves 4 and swings twice — it will be on you immediately.

It plays

Reinforce · Rifle Squad · Overrun x2 · War Cry · Blitz — every card it holds buys another attack, and it opens with two Attack Dogs.
