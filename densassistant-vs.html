Why we built it this way

Three deliberate choices — each one a trade we made on purpose, in your favor.

Local-first by default

Your memory never leaves your machine

Capture, transcription, indexing, and recall all run on your own device with small local models. There are no DensAssistant servers to breach and no analytics watching you. Your memory is a single file on your disk — export it or erase it anytime. The only network calls are the ones you explicitly turn on.

Text, not screenshots

A deliberate privacy choice

When a major screenshot-everything memory feature launched, the privacy backlash was immediate — a rolling gallery of everything on your screen is a lot to trust to any index. We follow the text you actually work with instead. It's lighter, it's searchable, and it never quietly banks a picture of your bank statement.

Proactive, not just passive

It preps you — it doesn't just remember

Most memory tools are a timeline you dig through after the fact. DensAssistant goes further: before a meeting it pulls what you discussed last time, open action items, and related notes, then reminds you in time to walk in ready. That proactive layer — meeting prep, routines, commitments — is our wedge over passive-recall tools (Pro).

Keep your memory yours.

Free core, no account, nothing in the cloud unless you say so. Pro adds proactive meeting prep, routines, and a bring-your-own cloud brain — one time, $59.99.
