The Potential of Haptic Foundation Models

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Identifies the lack of generalized touch sensing as the key bottleneck keeping foundation models from expanding into embodied AI, unlike language and vision.
  • Notes relevance beyond robots -- smartphones, wearables, and VR controllers all need better tactile foundation models.
  • Frames haptics as the next modality foundation-model research needs after vision-language.

RedLight-VLA: Models for traffic-rule grounding and behavioral emphasis in driving policies

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Targets a specific failure mode: behavior-cloned VLA driving policies underperform on rare, rule-governed maneuvers like stopping at red lights.
  • Braking and launching examples contribute little to averaged trajectory loss, so the paper proposes explicit rule-grounding rather than relying on it alone.
  • Points to a broader theme -- safety-critical rare events need explicit rule supervision layered on top of imitation learning.

Cognitively-Grounded On-Device Runtime Learning for Ground Robots in Unknown Physical Environments

ArXiv cs.RO
  • CogRun lets safety-critical ground robots learn at runtime entirely on edge-AI hardware, with no prior maps or perceptual knowledge.
  • Targets unknown physical environments -- the robot builds cognitive grounding on the fly instead of relying on pre-built world models.
  • On-device-only learning matters for deployments where cloud dependency or offline training isn't acceptable.

The Missing Layer in Robot Safety Assurance

The Robot Report
  • Argues current robot safety assurance frameworks don't account for adversarial attacks that alter perception, decision-making, or actuation.
  • Calls for security to be treated as a first-class layer of the safety case, not bolted on after functional safety sign-off.

The Robot Report
  • Argues gripper hardware, not just foundation models, is the bottleneck for physical AI generalization in real-world manipulation.
