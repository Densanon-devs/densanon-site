Research & Papers

EndoLIFT: Language-Disambiguated Latent-Conditioned Rectified Flow for Bidirectional Endoscopic Control

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Targets bidirectional control of endoscopes -- advancing toward target anatomy and later withdrawing or retroflexing for inspection -- as a single controllable behavior rather than two separate motion modes.
  • Uses a language-disambiguated, latent-conditioned rectified-flow model to resolve which direction the instrument should move when an external cue requests an earlier-than-expected phase change.
  • A less-covered but high-stakes application of language-conditioned robot control: surgical/medical manipulation rather than tabletop pick-and-place.

Koala Gripper: Co-designing Robotic Grippers and Data-Capture Devices for Scaling Dexterous Manipulation Learning

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Co-designs a handheld data-capture gripper alongside the robotic gripper it is meant to train, aimed at scaling manipulation-dataset collection.
  • Pushes back on the common practice of matching handheld data-capture devices to the morphology of an existing robot gripper, arguing that decoupling the two designs improves data quality.
  • Directly targets the imitation-learning data bottleneck behind large-scale dexterous manipulation policies.

Logic-VLA: A Temporal Logic Conditioned Vision-Language-Action Model

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Addresses a real gap in vision-language-action (VLA) policies: natural-language instructions often under-specify safety-critical or spatiotemporal requirements on the resulting behavior.
  • Introduces Logic-VLA, which conditions the model on formal (temporal-logic) requirements instead of relying on natural-language instructions alone.
  • Aimed at making learned manipulation/navigation policies verifiably compliant with hard constraints, not just plausible-looking.

Nonlinear Model Predictive Control for Trajectory Tracking of Differentially Flat Fixed-Wing Aerial Systems

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Tackles trajectory tracking for fixed-wing UAVs, which are harder to plan and control than multirotors due to nonlinear dynamics, aerodynamic limits, and disturbance sensitivity.
  • Combines differential flatness -- a principled way to generate fast, dynamically feasible trajectories -- with nonlinear MPC for tracking.
  • Core locomotion/control research relevant to any fixed-wing drone platform operating outdoors under wind disturbance.

These tiny drones are powered by sound

Robohub
  • EPFL's MICROBS Lab built microfliers that exploit Helmholtz resonance -- the same acoustic effect as blowing across a bottle neck -- to generate lift/thrust instead of conventional propellers or flapping wings.
  • A genuinely new actuation mechanism for insect-scale drones, an area where every gram of motor and battery weight is a hard constraint.
  • Real hardware research out of an academic robotics lab, not a vendor product announcement.

Running Next-Generation LeRobot Policies using lerobot-robot-ros2-zenoh SDK

ROS Discourse
  • Open-source lerobot-robot-ros2-zenoh SDK bridges Hugging Face's LeRobot policy-learning ecosystem directly into ROS2 via Zenoh, so trained policies can run on real hardware -- demoed on the OMY manipulator.
  • Closes the practical integration gap between a policy trained in LeRobot and a policy actually running on a ROS2-controlled robot, which is what determines whether research policies leave the sim.
  • Latest entry (#13) in an ongoing hands-on series testing LeRobot models on real manipulators.

A Robot Dog Trained Entirely on Dog's Video

r/robotics
  • Trains a Unitree GO2 quadruped to imitate a real dog's gait using only monocular phone video as the reference motion -- no motion-capture rig involved.
  • Pipeline goes from video straight to an RL locomotion policy, trained and validated in both Isaac Sim and MuJoCo, with open-source code released alongside the writeup.
  • A concrete demonstration that video-based motion imitation, increasingly common in humanoid work, scales down to consumer quadruped hardware too.
