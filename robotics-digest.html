ST-WAM: Semantic-Temporal World Action Model for Robust Manipulation under Visual Distribution Shifts

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Targets a known weakness in World Action Models: pixel-generative future prediction entangles action-relevant dynamics with task-irrelevant visual detail.
  • Proposes semantic-temporal modeling to keep manipulation policies robust when visual conditions shift from the training distribution.
  • Directly relevant to world-model-based manipulation policy learning, a core research theme in embodied AI.

D-VLC: Decentralized Vision-Language Collaboration for Heterogeneous Embodied Multi-Robot Systems in Unknown Environments

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Replaces rule-based multi-robot coordination (predefined task models) with decentralized vision-language collaboration for heterogeneous swarms.
  • Targets task execution in unknown environments where predefined rule-based models break down.

DART: Dual-Axis Airborne Reachability-Gated Torque-Reaction for Off-Road Vehicle Jumps

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Studies mid-air control of a 1,383 kg off-road vehicle during jump landings from crests, ledges, and ditches.
  • Finds the airborne phase is barely controllable via wheel angular momentum alone, motivating a torque-reaction control scheme.
  • Aims to reduce crash risk from mishandled landings at high speed on rugged terrain platforms.

Simulated zebrafish and a vision-equipped robotic fish reveal how the body shapes brain circuits

Robohub
  • Combines a simulated zebrafish model with a vision-equipped robotic fish (EPFL Biorobotics Lab) to study how body morphology shapes neural control circuits.
