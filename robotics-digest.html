Research & Papers

Light-Loco-Parkour: Versatile Perceptive Whole-Body Locomotion via Multi-Skill Distillation

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Proposes a whole-body humanoid locomotion system that distills multiple specialized skills into one versatile policy for moving through cluttered terrain.
  • Targets the gap between systems that track expressive whole-body motion without adapting to terrain, and systems that react to terrain but leave the arms and torso disconnected from the response.
  • Aims for humanoids that coordinate the whole body across obstacles rather than treating legs as the only terrain-aware part of the system.

Semantic Haptic Feedback Enhances Dexterous Robotic Teleoperation

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Introduces a 'semantic' haptic feedback approach for teleoperation that departs from trying to replicate high-fidelity real-world touch sensations.
  • Targets dexterous manipulation tasks, where existing high-fidelity haptic replication methods run into practical hardware and bandwidth limits.
  • Suggests conveying the meaning of a contact event, rather than its raw sensory fidelity, may be a more effective feedback channel for remote operators.

Toward Certified Functional Safety for Industrial Humanoid Robots: The Fail-Passive Gap and a Feasibility Study

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Identifies a 'fail-passive gap': legged humanoid robots have no true passive-safe state the way a wheeled robot can simply stop, because balance itself requires active control.
  • Argues functional-safety certification, not locomotion or manipulation skill, is now the binding constraint on deploying humanoids in industrial settings.
  • Presents a feasibility study aimed at closing that certification gap, directly relevant as regulators increasingly scrutinize humanoid robots.
