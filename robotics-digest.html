Research & Papers

Too Much of a Good Thing: When sim2real Efforts Impede Policy Learning (And What to Do About It)

ArXiv cs.RO
  • The paper argues that heavy sim2real engineering (domain randomization, fidelity tuning, adaptation layers) creates simulator lock-in that misaligns incentives with learning robust policies.
  • Excessive sim2real investment can mask poor policy architecture choices that would be caught immediately on real hardware, slowing genuine progress.
  • The authors propose a principled framework for deciding when to invest in simulation fidelity versus designing policies that are robust to the sim-to-real gap by construction.

See Less, Specify More: Visual Evidence Budgets for Generalizable VLAs

ArXiv cs.RO
  • The paper introduces a visual evidence budget that forces VLA models to commit to a small set of task-relevant visual tokens rather than attending broadly over the full image.
  • Restricting visual evidence improves generalization under distractors and appearance shifts by preventing the policy from over-fitting to irrelevant scene features.
  • The approach is architecture-agnostic and improves success rates on semantically similar tasks where standard VLAs fail to distinguish execution details.

Fixed-Time Dynamic Landing of Quadrotors using Adaptive Unscented Kalman Filtering and Nonlinear Model Predictive Control

ArXiv cs.RO
  • The framework pairs adaptive unscented Kalman filtering with nonlinear MPC to land a quadrotor on a moving platform with guaranteed fixed-time convergence bounds.
  • A real-time minimum-jerk trajectory planner is embedded inside the MPC loop, enabling smooth re-planning as the target platform moves unpredictably.
  • Target state estimation is handled entirely by the adaptive UKF — no direct velocity measurements of the moving platform are required.

Motion Planning in Dynamic Environments: A Survey from Classical to Modern Methods

ArXiv cs.RO
  • The survey maps the evolution from classical reactive planners (potential fields, velocity obstacles) through sampling-based methods to modern learned planners for dynamic environments.
  • Most existing benchmarks under-represent highly dynamic multi-agent scenarios, leaving a documented gap between reported performance and real-world results.
  • The paper provides a taxonomy linking method classes to environment dynamics categories, useful as a design reference when selecting planners for specific deployment contexts.

RoboChem Flex: democratisation of the autonomous synthesis robot

Robohub
  • Published in Nature Synthesis, RoboChem Flex is a modular, reconfigurable autonomous flow-chemistry robot designed so non-specialist labs can assemble and operate it without custom engineering.
  • The platform couples automated synthesis with closed-loop Bayesian optimization over reaction conditions, running experiments without human intervention between iterations.
  • Swappable reaction modules for different chemistries lower the barrier to automated synthesis research beyond well-funded specialist labs.

Festo launches lightweight pneumatic gripper and tests GripperAI

The Robot Report
  • Festo's new compact cobot gripper is robot-agnostic and significantly lighter than previous pneumatic grippers, designed for direct cobot end-of-arm mounting.
  • GripperAI enables zero-shot grasping of novel objects without any prior training — grasp strategy is inferred from object geometry at runtime.

This DIY Bipedal Robot Used Pneumatic "Air-Muscles" Instead of Motors

IEEE Spectrum Robotics
  • The Shadow Walker project (started 1987) used McKibben-style pneumatic artificial muscles (PAMs) rather than electric motors to drive a full-size humanoid biped.
  • PAMs contract under air pressure to approximate biological muscle geometry, providing inherently compliant actuation that rigid motor drives cannot match.
  • The project predates most modern compliant-actuator research and serves as an early practical proof-of-concept for biologically-inspired bipedal locomotion.

The Robot Report
  • Mapless AI specializes in navigation in unstructured environments without pre-built maps — a capability gap distinct from FORT's existing industrial-environment focus.
