Research & Papers

FLORA: An Open Model Suite for Scaling Robotics Data Engines

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Argues the life-cycle of robotics foundation models depends on data labels.
  • Names the label types needed: outcome rewards for monitoring real-world deployments, process rewards for RL post-training, and timestamped captions for filtering data.
  • Presented as an open model suite for scaling robotics data engines.

See What I Need, Dream What I Want: A Navigation Foundation Model on a Self-Constructed Representation Space

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Asks what visual representation a navigation policy should act on, noting current policies built on vision foundation models mostly inherit whatever the encoders' pretraining objectives provide.
  • Introduces RepresentNav, a navigation foundation model built around a self-constructed representation space.
Read full article

KiRoSe: Using Kinematic Data for Efficient Segmentation of Robot Demonstrations

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Targets segmenting demonstration data into temporal and semantic subsegments, useful for learning, cleaning, retrieval and analysis.
  • Notes this is often done manually, which is tedious and error prone.
  • Uses kinematic data to make the segmentation efficient, in contrast to approaches leaning on multimodal LLMs.
Read full article

PrefO2O: Zero-Shot Preference-Based Reinforcement Learning via Offline-to-Online Latent Preference Transfer

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Preference-based RL learns robot behaviors aligned with human intent, but scaling is limited by the cost of collecting human preference labels.
  • Proposes zero-shot offline-to-online transfer of latent preferences to reduce that labeling need.
