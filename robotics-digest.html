Google DeepMind's Gemini Robotics 2 and FedEx's expanded Dexterity trailer-loading rollout show physical AI moving from demos into production on real humanoid and warehouse hardware. Research & Papers

Cross-Embodiment Transfer via Behavior-Aligned Representations

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Uses embodiment-invariant representations (end-effector traces, object boxes, language motion descriptions) instead of embodiment-specific features to transfer manipulation skills across different robots.
  • End-effector traces give the largest transfer benefit and get more valuable as dataset size grows, including when learning from action-free data.
  • Reports a 28% improvement in real-robot task completion when policies are pretrained on simulation data before transfer.

It's Not Just More Demos: Counterfactual Action Sensitivity Coverage for Data-Efficient Robust Robot Imitation

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Introduces Counterfactual Nuisance Behaviour Cloning (CFNBC): generates paired observations with visual nuisances (lighting, distractions) while keeping expert actions fixed, then measures 'action drift' to find the most informative repair examples.
  • Selecting just 20-30 strategically chosen examples approaches the robustness gains of much larger randomly-collected repair datasets.
  • Supports a data-centric fix for policy brittleness: target examples that expose fragile responses rather than collecting more random diverse data.

Failure Detection for Surgical Robot Imitation Policies via Flow-Matching World Modeling

ArXiv cs.RO
  • FoMo-FD learns normal short-horizon visual dynamics with an action-conditioned flow-matching world model, then flags failures via inverse-transport nonconformity -- no failure demonstrations needed for training.
  • Achieves a 96.6% failure detection rate at a 1.3% false alarm rate on the da Vinci Research Kit, validated across four surgical tasks and twenty failure modes.
  • Uses conformal calibration on successful executions only, so detection thresholds generalize to unseen failure types.

Researchers develop modular nanorobot

Robohub
  • Built entirely from biomolecules and nanoparticles (no electronics): a magnetic propulsion module plus a payload capsule of four enzyme-loaded vesicles, joined by a DNA 'Velcro fastener' of complementary DNA strands.
  • At 150x smaller than a human hair, it navigates via magnetic propulsion, docks to target cells, and releases enzymatic payloads; the team reports it can be retrieved and reused.
  • Tested against HeLa cancer cells producing an anticancer compound in situ, reducing cell viability to 16% within 72 hours; also proposed for industrial catalysis and environmental remediation.

Exploring Runtime Trust and Authorization Patterns for ROS2-Based Autonomous Systems

ROS Discourse
  • Flags a governance gap as ROS2 systems move from dev to production: no built-in runtime authorization, behavioral monitoring, or decision audit trail for autonomous actions.
  • Proposed VYRA project layers add runtime visibility, execution-time action authorization policies, trust scoring, and anomaly detection on top of baseline ROS2.
  • Targets fleet-scale deployments (AMRs, industrial robots, warehouse automation, drones) where accountability for autonomous decisions is currently unaddressed by core ROS2 infrastructure.
