The Secret to Marathon-Winning Humanoid Robots

IEEE Spectrum Robotics
  • Honor Lightning ran a half-marathon in 50:26, beating the 2025 best robot time by nearly two hours and the human world record by 7 minutes.
  • The performance gap closed through gait optimization, thermal management, and joint-level control tuning rather than a single breakthrough technology.
  • Sets a concrete benchmark for locomotion researchers evaluating endurance vs. speed trade-offs in bipedal platforms.

New research enables a robot to chart a better course

Robohub
  • The open-source MIGHTY system generates smooth, obstacle-avoiding path plans rapidly enough for real-time deployment in logistics and disaster recovery scenarios.

IEEE Spectrum Robotics
  • The lead UX sound designer on Astro describes how non-verbal audio cues — not voice — were the primary tool for creating a sense of presence and personality in a home robot.
