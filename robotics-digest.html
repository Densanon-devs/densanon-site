Research & Papers

Spatial-UMI-Dream: Scaling World Action Models with Metric Geometry for UMI Manipulation

ArXiv cs.RO
  • A 5B-parameter world action model pretrained on 605 hours of RGB-D UMI data that jointly predicts future RGB frames, metric depth and actions.
  • On the new 20-task RoboTwin-UMI benchmark it reaches 79.10% in-domain and 72.15% under combined embodiment and environment shifts, 4.65 and 10.30 points above the strongest baseline.
  • Reports 85.0% average success on six real-world tasks, and RGB-only pretraining cost 5.75 points in-domain versus RGB-D.

CRISP: Constraint-Resolved Interactions between Subsystems for Predictive Whole-Body Cooperation

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Treats a humanoid as coupled subsystems (rigid torso, floating-base arms and lower body) with arm-torso and hand-to-hand links modeled as holonomic constraints whose multipliers are interaction wrenches.
  • A high-level nonlinear MPC jointly optimizes states, arm torques, ground reaction forces and wrenches online, for dynamic dual-arm manipulation.
  • Validated in simulation and on a G1 humanoid; the abstract gives qualitative results only, no numbers.

Curvature by Design: A Support Structure for Continuum Robots

ArXiv cs.RO
  • A single-piece, scissor-based compliant structure mechanically forces a tendon-driven continuum robot to follow a prescribed curvature profile.
  • It resists other deformations, such as S-shapes under opposing loads, and shrinks the number of shape variables that must be estimated.
  • The authors say shape precision improves significantly over classical flexible-backbone designs; the abstract does not quantify it.

How did Agility Robotics perform on its first day on the stock market?

The Robot Report
  • Trading as AGLT on Nasdaq after a SPAC deal priced at $10 a share; the company calls itself the first pure-play legged humanoid company to go public.
  • The stock opened at $11.26, ranged from $8.40 to $12.15 and closed at $11.49.
  • The offering was expected to bring in about $465 million in gross proceeds before expenses.

Chinese regulators block SoftBank's $5.4B acquisition of ABB Robotics

The Robot Report
  • China's foreign investment security review office prohibited SoftBank's roughly $5.375B purchase of ABB's robotics division; ABB confirmed the block and the Financial Times reported it first.
  • Authorities reportedly worried that robotics data could be sent overseas and used to train AI models.
  • ABB says it and SoftBank are in close dialogue and working constructively on the transaction; no timeline was given.

How Ambi Robotics helps its customers prepare for peak season

The Robot Report
  • Ambi's COO says logistics throughput can rise 25% to 40% in a typical peak season, and Ambi expects customer volume up 9% to 10% this year.
  • It is installing systems at eight facilities before peak, mostly the new AmbiSort B Series, and stocking spares at East Coast hubs and customer sites.
  • Its systems do not self-improve; models are retrained on new data and A/B tested in a controlled setting before wider rollout.

Robotiq_description and robotiq_controllers releases moving from ros2_robotiq_gripper to robotiq-ros

ROS Discourse
  • The packages on Humble, Jazzy, Lyrical and Rolling move from PickNik's repo to Robotiq's robotiq-ros, going from 0.0.1 to 1.x; Kilted stays on 0.0.1 until end of life.
  • Package names, macro arguments and joint names are unchanged, but old names like sim_ignition, sim_isaac and connected_to now warn as deprecated, and the Gazebo and Isaac plugin names change.
