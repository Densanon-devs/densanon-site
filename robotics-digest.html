Research & Papers

GHOST: Hierarchical Sub-Goal Policies for Generalizing Robot Manipulation

ArXiv cs.RO
  • GHOST factorizes manipulation control into a high-level policy that predicts 3D end-effector sub-goals and a low-level policy that executes them, enabling generalization beyond the training distribution.
  • By decoupling goal prediction from motor execution, the framework can adapt to novel object configurations without full retraining.
  • This hierarchical approach directly addresses one of the core brittleness issues in visuomotor policies: overfitting to specific scene layouts seen during training.

Efficient-WAM: A 1B-Parameter World-Action Model with Low-Cost Future Imagination

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Efficient-WAM couples future visual prediction with action generation in a 1B-parameter model, dramatically reducing inference latency compared to photorealistic world-model approaches.
  • The model replaces high-fidelity pixel prediction with a lower-cost 'imagination' mechanism, making real-time embodied control more practical on edge hardware.
  • Smaller, faster world-action models are a critical stepping stone toward deploying VLA-style systems outside of cloud-connected, high-compute environments.

Flow Control: Steering Vision-Language-Action Models with Simple Real-Time Inputs

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Flow control allows operators to steer VLA model actions in real-time using generic inputs (e.g., a keyboard) without any retraining or fine-tuning of the underlying model.
  • The method works out-of-the-box and is model-agnostic, making it immediately applicable to a wide range of deployed VLA systems.
  • Real-time human-in-the-loop steering is a practical safety and correction mechanism for deploying robot policies in environments where full autonomy is not yet reliable.
