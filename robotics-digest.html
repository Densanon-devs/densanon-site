Research & Papers

GRACE: Gradient-Free Robot Action Generation via Combined Diffusion-MPPI Posterior Mean Estimation

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Diffusion policies generate multimodal robot action sequences from demonstrations, but steering them toward deployment-time constraints has typically relied on differentiable guidance costs.
  • That reliance excludes many practical safety constraints, such as binary/non-differentiable ones.
  • GRACE combines diffusion with MPPI (a gradient-free method) to estimate the posterior mean, avoiding the need for differentiable guidance.

Ordered Action Tokens for Visuomotor Policy Learning

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Action tokenization maps continuous robot action chunks to discrete tokens and has become an important interface for modern visuomotor policies.
  • Existing approaches rely on analytical discretization methods that produce prohibitively long token sequences.

  • General-purpose robots need to reason about their actions by combining perception, world knowledge, planning, success detection, recovery, and low-level control.
