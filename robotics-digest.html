Tinca: a phone control panel for ROS 2 robots, built from 160+ survey answers, plus its open-source bridge library

ROS Discourse
  • Tinca is an Android and iOS app that turns a phone into a control panel for a ROS 2 robot.
  • Users build dashboards from 30 widgets for topics, services and actions, including joysticks, a D-Pad, Map 2D with nav goals, camera and time series.
  • It was shaped by 160+ survey answers and ships with an open-source bridge library.
