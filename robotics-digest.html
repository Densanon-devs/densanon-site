Bimo's walking model now runs natively on a Raspberry Pi Pico at 5ms inference time!

r/robotics
  • Bimo's locomotion policy — trained on thousands of Isaac Lab parallel environments — was distilled down to run on a bare RP2040 microcontroller with no external compute.
  • 5.2ms inference time on the Pico means the full walking control loop runs at well over 100Hz on a $4 chip.
  • Policy distillation from Isaac Lab sim to embedded edge hardware is a repeatable workflow for low-cost legged robot platforms.
  • Demonstrates that simulation-trained locomotion policies are now compact enough for microcontroller deployment, not just GPU-class inference.
