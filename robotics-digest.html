Research & Papers

GigaBrain-WBC-0.5: A Behavior World Model for Robust Whole-Body Control with Environment Interaction

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Positions itself as the first Behavior World Model (BWM) for humanoid whole-body control: a causal Transformer jointly predicts the next action, next state, and a distribution over feasible behavioral commands instead of just reactively tracking motion.
  • An automated terrain-annotation pipeline extracts 3D contact geometry from motion data at scale, teaching the model how terrain reshapes what commands are actually achievable; at deployment it flags implausible commands and adjusts them to feasible "best-effort" behavior rather than failing outright.
  • Reports 81.3% success on terrain interaction (4.3x the strongest baseline), 83.1% success under implausible commands, and 99.3% fall recovery, with policy transfer demonstrated across different robot hardware.

Revisiting the "Push-T" Robot Manipulation Task with Agentic Robotics

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Instead of training a diffusion policy on human demonstrations, the authors pointed an LLM coding agent (Claude Code) at the classic Push-T benchmark and had it write and iteratively refine an algorithmic solution from scratch, with zero demonstration data.
  • The agent-written solution hit a 100% success rate using 46% fewer steps than the best diffusion policy trained on 200 human demonstrations.
  • The agent then extended the benchmark on its own initiative, generating a self-authored "Push-A to Push-Z" curriculum and new 3D simulation setups for Franka and UR5 arms with visual feedback.

GAPL: Grounded Action-effect Policy Learning for LLM-Based Trajectory Planning

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Targets a core failure mode of using LLMs for autonomous-driving trajectory planning: hallucinated reasoning and poor numerical precision when the model reasons in free text instead of grounded dynamics.
  • Combines an LLM-based Effect Evaluator (structured multi-dimensional action-effect estimation) with a simulation-based Effect Grounder, then distills both into an Effect-Aware Decision Maker that guides PPO policy learning.
  • Across four Highway-env scenarios, reports lower collision rate and displacement error alongside higher average reward versus baselines -- pairing LLM semantic reasoning with simulator-grounded precision.

Scheduling and Routing with Degradation-Triggered Job Arrivals: An Application to Forest Firefighting with an Unmanned Aerial Vehicle Fleet

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Models wildfire response as a joint scheduling/routing problem where new "jobs" are triggered by the degradation of existing ones: each ignited area has a time-bound action window, and delay lets it grow, spread, and lose value.
  • Proposes two solution methods -- a mixed-integer program for value optimization and a hybrid dynamic-constraint-generation model built for scalability -- validated with computational experiments and case studies for a UAV firefighting fleet.
  • Published in the European Journal of Operational Research with publicly released code, aimed at time-critical wildfire suppression rather than generic drone routing.

What does Unitree Robotics’ IPO mean for the humanoid industry?

The Robot Report
  • Unitree's IPO on Shanghai's STAR Market closed up 460% on day one, raising roughly 6.1 billion yuan (~$905M); the company swung from a $1.6M loss in 2023 to $14M profit in 2024 and $41M profit in 2025, on unit sales that grew from 5 humanoids in 2023 to 5,215 in 2025 (about 70% to universities/research, under 10% commercial).
  • Rival UBTech (Hong Kong-listed since 2023) sold 1,079 humanoids in 2025 but posted a roughly $104M loss, leaning on consumer products like lawn mowers and pool cleaners for revenue; Agility Robotics is separately pursuing a SPAC merger at a $2.5B pre-money valuation.
  • Lands as the FCC moved in July 2026 to restrict U.S. imports of certain foreign-made humanoids and quadrupeds, adding a regulatory wrinkle to how far Unitree's momentum can travel.

Open-source, Accessible, and Customizable 3D-printed Humanoid Robot

Hacker News Robotics
  • UC Berkeley's "Humanoid Lite" costs under $5,000 in parts sourced from ordinary e-commerce sites and printed on standard desktop 3D printers, using cycloidal gearbox actuators to work around the durability limits of plastic parts.
  • Hardware design, embedded code, and the RL training/deployment framework are fully open-source, with zero-shot sim-to-real policy transfer already demonstrated on the physical robot.
  • The team introduces a "performance factor per dollar" benchmark and reports the platform holds its own against far pricier humanoids, while also handling fine motor tasks like solving a Rubik's cube and writing.

Unitree's humanoid robot jumps 2 meters, hits 12.66M/s to break human records

Hacker News Robotics
  • A new Unitree humanoid (successor to April 2026's H1) posted a 2-meter standing vertical jump and a 12.66 m/s top speed, landing steadily after the jump rather than just hitting a peak number momentarily.
  • Both figures top human records and build on the H1's prior 10 m/s milestone, which had been closing in on Usain Bolt's peak sprint speed.
  • Unitree says the robot was developed in just over three months and frames the runs as hardware/software stress tests -- balance control and stability limits -- rather than a finished product, with "significant room for improvement" still ahead.
