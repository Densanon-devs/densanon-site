Research & Papers

GigaBrain-WBC-0.5: A Behavior World Model for Robust Whole-Body Control with Environment Interaction

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Introduces the first Behavior World Model for humanoid whole-body control: a causal Transformer that jointly predicts its next action, next state, and a distribution over its next latent behavior command, so the network that acts also models how the environment shapes what it can do next.
  • An automatic terrain-annotation pipeline recovers full 3D contact geometry from retargeted motion data, letting the policy learn how contact with terrain and objects reshapes dynamics instead of training only on flat, empty scenes.
  • Beats three large-scale tracker baselines across all four test regimes: 81.3% success on terrain interaction (4.3x the strongest baseline), 83.1% under implausible commands, and 99.3% fall recovery (16.8x the strongest baseline); the Unitree G1 checkpoint transfers to a Maker L01 robot with simple fine-tuning.

Revisiting the "Push-T" Robot Manipulation Task with Agentic Robotics

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Instead of training a visuomotor imitation-learning policy on human demonstrations, the researchers prompted an LLM coding agent -- Claude Code with Fable 5 -- to write an algorithmic solution to the iconic Push-T manipulation benchmark from scratch, with zero demonstration data.
  • The agent found a 2D gym simulation online and ran its own simulated experiments to learn push mechanics, iteratively optimizing until it hit a 100% success rate using 46% fewer steps than the best diffusion policy trained on 200 human demonstrations.
  • Extended itself from Push-T to a full self-generated Push-A through Push-Z curriculum, and generated 3D cross-embodiment simulation code with visual feedback for both Franka and UR5 robot arms.

GAPL: Grounded Action-effect Policy Learning for LLM-Based Trajectory Planning

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Targets a known failure mode of LLM-based autonomous-driving planners -- hallucinated reasoning and poor grounding in environment dynamics -- with a three-module closed loop: an LLM-based Effect Evaluator, a simulation-based Effect Grounder, and a PPO-based Effect-Aware Decision Maker.
  • The Effect Grounder uses simulator rollouts to check that the LLM's predicted action outcomes are actually dynamics-consistent before a distiller lets them shape the policy, rather than trusting the LLM's reasoning directly.
  • Across four Highway-env scenarios, reports average reductions of 0.76, 0.86, and 2.00 in collision rate, average displacement error, and final displacement error respectively, plus a 1.44 average reward gain over baselines.

Scheduling and Routing with Degradation-Triggered Job Arrivals: An Application to Forest Firefighting with an Unmanned Aerial Vehicle Fleet

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Models a scheduling/routing problem where new 'jobs' are triggered by the degradation of existing ones: each ignited area has an action window, and the longer it goes unaddressed the more it spreads and loses value -- turning 'a stitch in time saves nine' into an optimization objective for a UAV firefighting fleet.
  • Formulates a mixed-integer program to maximize total value retention across wildfire-threatened areas, plus a hybrid dynamic-constraint-generation variant to keep the model scalable.
  • Validated with computational experiments and a case study; the team released the full codebase for reproducibility, with the work published in the European Journal of Operational Research.

What does Unitree Robotics’ IPO mean for the humanoid industry?

The Robot Report
  • Unitree debuted on Shanghai's STAR Market, raising roughly $905M (6.1 billion yuan) and closing its first trading day up 460% -- billed as the first major legged-robotics IPO.
  • Growth looks steep on paper (5 humanoid units sold in 2023 to 5,215 in 2025), but rivals are skeptical of real commercial traction: Ghost Robotics' CEO put Unitree's commercial-deployment share under 10%, and roughly 70% of units reportedly went to universities and research labs.
  • A recent FCC restriction on imports of certain foreign-made mobile robots (including humanoids and quadrupeds) threatens Unitree's U.S. sales, even as rivals eye their own exits -- UBTech's 2023 Hong Kong IPO lost $104M last year, and Agility Robotics is reportedly weighing a $2.5B SPAC merger.

Serve Robotics to deploy its autonomous delivery robots with Grubhub

The Robot Report

Pudu Robotics launches new MP2000 autonomous forklift

The Robot Report
  • The AI-native MP2000 moves loads up to 2,000 kg with pallet pickup and fork-in cycles as fast as 20 seconds, using 3D lidar and depth cameras to locate pallets and correct its approach path in real time.
  • Handles three-runner and perimeter-base pallets plus customized non-standard load carriers, validating pallet dimensions on the fly to compensate for misalignment.
  • Coordinates with other Pudu AMRs via distributed fleet intelligence -- local decision-making plus fleet-level coordination that keeps working through network outages -- and includes manual operator override for use as a powered forklift.

Open-source, Accessible, and Customizable 3D-printed Humanoid Robot

Hacker News Robotics
  • Berkeley Humanoid Lite is a fully open-source humanoid platform -- hardware design, embedded code, and training framework all public -- built from parts you 3D-print on a standard desktop printer, for under $5,000 total.
  • Uses modular cycloidal gearboxes specifically engineered to hold up in 3D-printed plastic, addressing the durability problem that normally rules out printed actuators.
  • Demonstrated bipedal walking, fine manipulation (marker writing, solving a Rubik's cube), and zero-shot sim-to-hardware policy transfer via reinforcement learning; validated in an RSS 2025 publication with full code, docs, and Discord/WeChat community support.

China's humanoid robot boom is fueled by government-backed training centres

Hacker News Robotics
  • Local governments across China have funded roughly 40 dedicated humanoid-robot training centers, where human workers demonstrate everyday tasks -- folding laundry, wiping surfaces -- that robots imitate to build up scarce real-world training data.
  • Shanghai's Pudong district runs one of the flagship centers, already training over 100 robots with a stated plan to scale to 1,000 by 2027, as part of a broader national push to make humanoid robotics a strategic industry through 2030.
  • Highlights a structural China-vs-West gap: state-subsidized data-collection infrastructure at this scale is something few Western humanoid startups can match on their own budget.

Hacker News Robotics
  • Unitree's newest humanoid (successor to the H1) posted a 2-meter standing vertical jump and a 12.66 m/s top sprint speed on legs just 0.85 meters long -- both said to exceed human athletic records, after roughly three months of development.
