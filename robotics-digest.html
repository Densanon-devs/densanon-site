Confidence-Modulated Safety Filtering for End-to-End Policies

ArXiv cs.RO
  • The paper addresses end-to-end policies, from task-specific controllers to robot foundation models, that are not guaranteed to remain safe.

CaPtain: A Pipeline for Post-Training Code-as-Policy Robot Agents

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Code-as-policy casts robot control as code written by a language-model agent.

WorldBridge: Bridging Video and Action for Task Generalization in World Action Models

ArXiv cs.RO

ArXiv cs.RO
