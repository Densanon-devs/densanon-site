Support-Constrained RL Enables Real-World Policy Improvement without Real-World Experience

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Real-world trained robot policies are often imprecise and brittle; this work uses support-constrained RL to improve them using only simulation, skipping expensive real-world retraining.
  • The approach constrains policy updates to the support of existing real-world data, preventing the sim-to-real gap from degrading performance.
  • If validated broadly, this could significantly reduce the cost of policy iteration for deployed robots.

AO-ARC: Almost-Surely Asymptotically Optimal Multi-Robot Motion Planning with ARC

ArXiv cs.RO
  • AO-ARC is an anytime multi-robot motion planning method that achieves fast initial solutions on par with state-of-the-art feasibility solvers.
  • The method converges to near-optimal solutions faster and more reliably than existing anytime MRMP approaches as robot count grows.
  • Anytime planning with strong optimality guarantees is a practical requirement for warehouse, swarm, and infrastructure robotics.

Spacecraft Fiducial Marker for Autonomous Rendezvous, Proximity Operations, and Docking

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Existing fiducial markers fail in space due to harsh lighting, radiation, and extreme distances; this work introduces a marker designed specifically for those conditions.
  • The new marker aids autonomous rendezvous, proximity operations, and docking (RPOD) without relying on GPS or active beacons.
  • Reliable autonomous docking is a prerequisite for on-orbit servicing, debris removal, and deep-space assembly missions.

SceneBot: Contact-Prompted General Humanoid Whole Body Tracking with Scene-Interaction

ArXiv cs.RO
  • SceneBot addresses a key weakness in humanoid RL: current policies handle free-space motion well but fail at contact-rich tasks like pushing objects or navigating uneven terrain.
  • The system uses contact prompts to resolve physical ambiguities during whole-body tracking, enabling richer scene interaction.
  • This is directly relevant to humanoid robot deployment in real environments where contact with the world is unavoidable.

Learning to Throw: Agile and Accurate Cable-Suspended Payload Delivery with a Quadrotor

ArXiv cs.RO
  • This work teaches quadrotors to release cable-suspended payloads mid-flight with high accuracy, enabling delivery to targets unreachable by hovering.
  • The learned policy handles the dynamic coupling between the drone and swinging payload, a notoriously difficult control problem.
  • Applications include search-and-rescue drops and medical supply delivery in GPS-limited or physically inaccessible environments.

RUBI (Robot Utility Board Interface) - v2

ROS Discourse
  • RUBI v2 is a single-window ROS 2 control board that replaces the need to juggle multiple terminals and monitoring tools during robot development.
  • It provides live topic rates, bandwidth monitoring, QoS mismatch detection, and a health watchdog in one unified interface.
