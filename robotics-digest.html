Being-H0.7: A Latent World-Action Model from Egocentric Videos

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Proposes a VLA architecture that learns latent world representations from egocentric video rather than relying on sparse action supervision alone, reducing shortcut mappings.
  • Directly addresses the core failure mode of standard VLAs: models that regress actions without grounding in how the world actually changes under those actions.
  • Egocentric training signal is particularly applicable to humanoid and mobile manipulation policies where first-person visual streams are the primary sensor input.

Predictive Spatio-Temporal Scene Graphs for Semi-Static Scenes

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Extends spatio-semantic scene graphs with temporal prediction, enabling robots to reason about where objects will be — not just where they currently are.
  • Targets semi-static scenes (the common real-world case where most objects stay put but a subset changes), rather than assuming fully static or fully dynamic environments.
  • Directly applicable to manipulation and navigation agents that need to anticipate state changes before executing long-horizon plans.

Do Open-Loop Metrics Predict Closed-Loop Driving? A Cross-Benchmark Correlation Study of NAVSIM and Bench2Drive

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Empirically tests whether fast open-loop benchmark scores (displacement error, etc.) actually predict how a planner behaves when it is in the loop controlling the vehicle.
  • Finds that traditional open-loop metrics have questionable predictive validity — a critical signal for simulation pipeline design and benchmark selection.
  • Provides a reproducible cross-benchmark correlation methodology teams can apply to validate their own evaluation setups before committing to a metric.
