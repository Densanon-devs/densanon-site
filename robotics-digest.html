ArXiv cs.RO
  • Targets the problem of improving a pretrained vision-language-action (VLA) model with demonstrations that address its specific weaknesses.

Racing the Clock: Teaching VLNs Budget-Conditioned Object Search

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Argues a rescue robot should search differently with thirty seconds than with ten minutes, sprinting to the most promising room versus touring broadly.
