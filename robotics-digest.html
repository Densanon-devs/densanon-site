Research & Papers

Egocentric Station Holding of Robotic Fish in Unknown Turbulent Background Flow

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Addresses 'station holding' (maintaining position against a current) for robotic fish in unknown, turbulent background flow using an egocentric, robot-relative sensing/control formulation rather than assuming a known flow field
  • Targets a real gap: most swimming-robot research has optimized swimming efficiency and maneuverability in calm or known-flow conditions, not station-keeping under unmodeled turbulence
  • Relevant control pattern for any project handling disturbance rejection under unknown dynamics, e.g. densanon-core's physics/vehicle_sim engines

Steeringless Drifting: Differential-Torque Control of a Four-Wheel Independently Driven Vehicle

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Demonstrates sustained drift (oversteer at the handling limit) on a four-wheel-independently-driven vehicle using only differential torque across the four wheels -- no mechanical steering input at all
  • Departs from conventional drift control, which relies on mechanical steering geometry plus rear-tire saturation, making it specific to emerging steer-by-wire/torque-vectoring EV chassis architectures
  • Useful reference for extreme-handling vehicle control work, e.g. densanon-core's vehicle_sim engine

FIRMGrasp: A Friction-Informed Risk Margin for Robust Grasp Synthesis

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Points out that classical grasp-quality metrics assume a single fixed friction coefficient, so they can't predict whether a grasp actually retains force closure across the real range of friction a surface may exhibit
  • Proposes a friction-informed risk margin that scores grasp candidates by robustness to friction uncertainty rather than by quality under one nominal friction value
  • Directly relevant to dexterous-hand manipulation pipelines (see today's TactaBot announcement) where friction mis-estimation is a common real-world grasp-failure mode

Amortising Trajectory Optimisation for Residual MPC via Implicit Contact Differentiation

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Tackles contact-rich trajectory optimization by using differentiable simulation to expose control sensitivities, instead of expensive, step-size-sensitive finite-difference gradients
  • Amortizes the optimization into a residual MPC formulation, aiming to make contact-rich trajectory optimization fast enough for online control rather than offline planning only
  • Addresses a known bottleneck -- contact discontinuities breaking gradient-based optimization -- relevant to any physics-based contact handling, including densanon-core's tactics/agent_sim engines

Robot Finger Feels in Color

IEEE Spectrum Robotics
  • Describes an optical tactile sensor that reads contact deformation in color to capture fine surface texture -- e.g. resolving the raised lettering, portrait relief, and architectural detail on a U.S. coin -- detail that force-only touch sensors miss
  • Positions color-based sensing as a way to close a longstanding gap in robotic fingertip perception between geometric texture-reading and simple pressure/binary contact detection
  • Relevant benchmark for tactile/haptic feedback work in manipulation pipelines, alongside today's FIRMGrasp friction paper and TactaBot hand announcement
