Research & Papers

Track-Guided Hierarchical Reinforcement Learning for Autonomous Vehicle Drifting with Minimum-Lap-Time Planning

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Uses a hierarchical reinforcement learning approach guided by the track layout to plan minimum-lap-time trajectories for autonomous vehicles.
  • Targets drift maneuvers (intentionally breaking traction on loose surfaces) rather than the grip-limited racing-line optimization used in circuit racing.
  • Borrows a rally-driving technique -- drifting to rapidly reorient for corner exits -- and applies it to autonomous vehicle control.

Motion Planning for Mobile Manipulators Navigating Doorways via Model Predictive Control

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Presents an MPC-based motion planning framework that coordinates a mobile robot's base and arm together rather than planning them separately.
  • Targets doorway navigation specifically, framed as a fundamental capability for mobile manipulators operating in human environments.
  • Generates dynamically feasible motion plans, addressing the tight-clearance coordination problem doorways pose for combined base-and-arm systems.

Developing Combined Manipulation and Locomotion Skills with Interaction Representation and Skill Composition

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Proposes a developmental-principles-based approach for humanoid robots to learn whole-body reactive motion policies.
  • Focuses on composing multiple learned skills together to build more sophisticated combined manipulation-and-locomotion behaviors.
  • Frames skill composition as the path to more capable humanoid behavior rather than training one policy per task.

Walden Robotics Partners With Toyota on Practical Humanoids

IEEE Spectrum Robotics
  • Walden Robotics and Toyota are partnering on humanoid robots explicitly framed around practical, near-term goals rather than hype-driven development.
  • The coverage frames this as a course correction from an earlier industry-wide rush to build humanoids without realistic near-term use cases.

HEBI Robotics earns NASA SBIR grant to fast track miniaturized actuators

The Robot Report
  • HEBI Robotics received a NASA Phase I SBIR grant specifically to accelerate development of miniaturized actuators.
  • The grant is described as only one piece of a broader roadmap -- HEBI is also continuing to develop its existing actuation hardware line.

Former DeepMind team launches AI robotics startup with robots that learn from workers

Robotics & Automation News
  • Reimagine Robotics, founded by former leaders of Google DeepMind's Applied Robotics team, has emerged from stealth.
  • Core technology lets robots learn on the job from workers rather than requiring specialized robotics or ML expertise to program.
  • Pitch centers on end-user trainability -- 'anyone can train and use' the robots -- as the key differentiator from traditional industrial robot programming.

Researchers unveil open-source robotic guide dog designed to assist blind and visually impaired people

Robotics & Automation News
  • 'Milo' is described by its creators as the world's first fully autonomous robotic guide dog, targeting both indoor and outdoor navigation for blind and visually impaired users.
  • The project is open-source and explicitly positioned as a lower-cost alternative to existing guide solutions.
  • AI-powered mobile robot design implies onboard autonomy for navigation assistance rather than remote-operated or scripted movement.

Robotics & Automation News
