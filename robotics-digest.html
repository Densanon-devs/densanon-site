AI searched 100 million possibilities and found a cheaper way to 3D-print a NASA rocket alloy

ScienceDaily Robotics
  • An AI search explored over 100 million possible parameter settings for 3D-printing the NASA rocket alloy GRCop-42.
  • After only 40 physical experiments, the system identified six successful print configurations, including one running at a record-low 500 watts.
  • Lower laser power could make GRCop-42 substantially cheaper and easier to produce, since the alloy is currently difficult and costly to print.
