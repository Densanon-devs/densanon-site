Research & Papers

SUGAR: A Scalable Human-Video-Driven Generalizable Humanoid Loco-Manipulation Learning Framework

ArXiv cs.RO
  • SUGAR learns whole-body loco-manipulation from human video rather than task-specific reward engineering, making it far more generalizable across environments.
  • The framework avoids rigid reference-motion replay - a common failure mode - by allowing flexible adaptation during execution.
  • A meaningful step toward training humanoids on internet-scale human demonstration data rather than purpose-built robot datasets.

Proximal State Nudging: Reducing Skill Atrophy from AI Assistance

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Skill atrophy - humans losing capability when AI handles corrections - is a real safety risk in shared-control systems where sudden handoff to manual must remain safe.
  • Proximal State Nudging keeps human skills engaged by nudging system state toward outcomes the operator could achieve themselves.
  • Directly applicable to surgical robotics, aviation, and industrial teleoperation where operators may need to take over with little warning.

VBT-MPC: Vision-Based Tactile MPC for Contour Following

ArXiv cs.RO
  • VBT-MPC fuses vision-based tactile sensing with Model Predictive Control to enable real-time contour following without losing contact.
  • The MPC framing gives the system a principled way to recover from slip or misalignment - a long-standing brittleness in tactile-based inspection.
  • Applicable to quality control, assembly verification, and any manipulation task requiring maintained contact with complex or irregular geometries.
