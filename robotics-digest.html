Boston Dynamics built Stretch Gen 2 for reliable, consistent performance

The Robot Report
  • Boston Dynamics says Stretch Gen 2 took about two and a half years to develop, and that the first generation has logged over 100,000 operating hours.
  • The company says the robot can unload up to 1,000 cases per hour, depending on case weight and stacking, and that it cut 81% of internal cabling and switched to automotive-grade connectors.
  • A full swerve drive swap now takes about an hour instead of up to a day, and the company says its latest software update raised unload rate 24% across the fielded fleet.

MABEL: an open-source 56-DOF bimanual mobile robot from NYU and the University of Toronto

ROS Discourse
  • The poster shares MABEL, an open-source robot with 56 degrees of freedom, a three-wheel swerve base, a vertical lift, a pitching torso and a stereo-camera head.
  • It uses two 7-DOF arms adapted from OpenArm and two 17-DOF hands adapted from ORCA Hand, and is teleoperated with a single Apple Vision Pro.
  • Hardware is CERN-OHL-S-2.0 and software Apache-2.0; the post lists parts cost at about $8,056 (Essential) to $14,721 (Maximum) at July 2026 prices, with some parts not yet costed.

ZED ROS 2 Wrapper 5.6: CUDA zero-copy with rosidl::Buffer for ROS 2 Lyrical and Isaac ROS 5

ROS Discourse
  • ZED ROS 2 Wrapper v5.6 can publish images, depth, confidence maps and point clouds from GPU memory using the new rosidl::Buffer CUDA backend in ROS 2 Lyrical, avoiding a GPU-to-CPU copy.
  • Subscribers that cannot take CUDA buffers, such as RViz2 or ros2 bag, automatically receive standard CPU data.
  • On a Jetson AGX Thor with a ZED 2i, the poster reports latency reductions of 10% to 61% depending on point cloud size.

Ros2_control in Lyrical Luth: what's new

ROS Discourse
  • Lyrical Luth is marked stable in ros2_control, ros2_controllers, control_toolbox and realtime_tools.
  • Behaviour changes include strict switch_controller by default, a failing controller deactivating its whole chain, and removal of deprecated Jazzy handle and export APIs.
  • New additions include trajectory replacement in joint_trajectory_controller and battery_state, state_interfaces and magnetometer broadcasters.

Early end of support of Gazebo Harmonic, Fortress on macOS

ROS Discourse
  • The Gazebo PMC announced it will drop macOS support for Gazebo Harmonic and Fortress on 2027-01-10 because Homebrew is disabling the freeimage and qt@5 formulae.
  • Homebrew cited open CVEs in freeimage and Qt5 reaching end-of-life in 2025; freeimage is disabled on 2027-01-10 and qt@5 on 2027-05-19.
  • Harmonic and Fortress remain supported on Ubuntu until their documented end-of-life dates.
