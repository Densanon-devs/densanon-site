Learning Low-Latency Action Verification for Frozen Vision-Language-Action Models

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Vision-language-action (VLA) models have made significant progress in general-purpose robotic manipulation.
  • Sampled action sequences can be ineffective, and evaluating alternatives with computationally expensive verifiers adds latency at each step.
  • The paper targets low-latency action verification for frozen VLA models.

SunLightBots: Turning sunlight into self-sustained, high-power motion in soft robots

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Soft robots built from active materials can convert environmental stimuli directly into motion, offering a route to energy autonomy and embodied control.
  • Environmental energy is generally harvested at low power, whereas rapid, mechanically demanding motion needs high power; the work aims to bridge that gap with sunlight.
