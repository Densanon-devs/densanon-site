Research & Papers

AIRoA-0: Long-Horizon, Precise, Cross-Embodiment Manipulation from Handheld Data

ArXiv cs.RO
  • A bimanual manipulation system learned from 16,151 hours of handheld YUBI gripper demonstrations, with no robot data collected at any stage.
  • Averaged 78% success across ten tasks, versus 27% for the strongest of four other VLA models post-trained on the same demonstrations.
  • Dataset, pre-trained weights and post-training recipe are being released; it runs on several robots including Franka, OpenArm and UR.

Robot-Matched Soft Fingers for UMI Data Collection: Lessons from 100 Hours of Pick and Place

ArXiv cs.RO
  • A battery-powered handheld interface built on YUBI uses soft fingers matched to the robot's grippers, whose visible deformation acts as a visual proxy for grip force.
  • A 99.7-hour dataset over 53 objects trained pi0.5 policies; mid-training raised success on unseen objects from 35% to 57%.
  • Finger design, dataset and processing code are released.

RoboScope Kit: Unified Interfaces for Fast and Scalable Embodied-AI Evaluation

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Profiling shows simulation benchmark evaluation is CPU-bound, with GPU utilization averaging only 14.3%.
  • Separating simulator workers from policy replicas gave 1.5x to 4.5x speedups on LIBERO-Long over existing evaluators, with equivalent success rates.
  • On RoboTwin Easy, four GPUs cut a 360-minute 500-episode run to 14.4 minutes.

New ISO interoperability standard covers collaboration among mobile robots from multiple vendors

The Robot Report
  • ISO 21423:2026 sets communication and interoperability requirements for AGVs, AMRs and similar robots from different vendors in one facility.
  • Robots share position, status, tasks and availability, and the standard covers links to ERP systems, lifts, automated doors and workers' mobile devices.
  • A3 calls it its first non-safety robot standard; it costs $373 ($320 for A3 members).

From human experience to robot action: LiberAI launches Liber-0 world-action model

Robotics & Automation News
  • Liber-0 is pretrained on over 2.2 million hours of mostly human activity video, then refined with glove-recorded hand motions and robot demonstrations.
  • In an eight-task test, glove data added to 25% of robot demos scored 75.6 versus 72.8 for the full robot dataset.
  • Zero-shot success across 60 tasks was 36%.

Humanoid Robots in Warehouses vs AMRs: Which Automation Makes Sense in 2026?

Robotics & Automation News
  • AMRs automate movement and are the proven first investment, with payback of roughly two years per the article.
  • Humanoids suit targeted pilots on awkward, hard-to-staff tasks such as moving totes onto a conveyor.
  • Humanoid economics improve with multi-shift operation and rental pricing; both types must integrate with the warehouse management system.

Ben Waters on the latest wireless charging strategies

The Robot Report
  • Opportunity charging at natural pauses keeps fleets working, and wireless avoids wear on contact connectors.
  • Teams should plan charging power infrastructure early and avoid overbuilding batteries; fast charging trades off against battery lifetime.
  • WiBotic launched an IP65-rated 1 kW charger and plans to push products to 3 kW, with demand growing in AMRs, outdoor inspection and humanoids.
