GaussVLA: Geometry-Aware Spatial Reasoning for Vision-Language-Action Model

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Identifies a core limitation of current VLA models: they encode visual input as flat 2D patch tokens with no intrinsic geometric structure.
  • Shows that simply adding monocular depth only injects per-pixel scalar values without surface orientation, so it does not close the geometry gap.

ROS2 Connect: A new ROS2 over WAN Solution

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Targets a practical ROS2 limitation: its DDS/RTPS transport relies on multicast discovery that typically does not work across wide-area networks.
  • Proposes a solution letting distributed robotic systems communicate reliably over WAN links instead of being confined to local networks.

Extending Ground-Constraint LiDAR-IMU Calibration to Tilted Surfaces in a Continuous-Time Framework

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Extends targetless LiDAR-IMU calibration for ground vehicles from flat terrain to non-flat, tilted environments.
  • Addresses the practical constraint that standard calibration needs full sensor-rig excitation, which ground vehicles on flat surfaces cannot provide.

SkyDrive: Learning to Drive in a New City from Aerial Traffic Monitoring

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Tackles the domain-shift problem where imitation-learned driving planners degrade severely when deployed zero-shot in a new city.
  • Uses aerial traffic-monitoring footage of the new city to adapt the driving policy without collecting new ground-level demonstrations.

Sequential Object Placement Optimization with Convex Decomposition

ArXiv cs.RO

  • Applies convex decomposition to make sequential placement optimization tractable for logistics and industrial packing tasks.
