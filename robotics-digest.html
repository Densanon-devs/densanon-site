DUET: Decoupled Upper- and Lower-Body Execution for Task-Agnostic Humanoid Loco-Manipulation

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Argues that changing a humanoid's manipulation policy should not require retraining the controller that maintains balance.
  • DUET is a decoupled execution framework that lets locomotion and manipulation controllers operate separately.
  • Aims at task-agnostic loco-manipulation for humanoid robots.

Context-Aware RGB-Thermal-Radar Obstacle Perception for Resource-Constrained ASVs

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Targets small autonomous surface vessels, where reflections and glare confound visible-light cameras.

  • Combines RGB, thermal and radar sensing for obstacle perception on resource-constrained platforms.
