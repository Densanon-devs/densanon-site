Research & Papers

CS-Pi: Constraint-Satisfying Flow Policy Sampling

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Draw, project to the nearest safe action, re-add noise in proportion to how far the projection moved, and rerun the unmodified policy until a draw is safe.
  • Avoids two known failures: gradient guidance averaging left and right paths into the obstacle, and projection producing actions the policy would rarely take.
  • 81% safe success against 55% for projection on a 7-DoF shelf task, and 55% against 40% zero-shot on Franka hardware.

Meituan-Robotics-0: A Vision-Language-Action Foundation Model for Desktop Manipulation

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Trained on 147M vision-language samples plus 15,060 hours of robot trajectories across 12 embodiments.
  • Three stages: discrete action-token pre-training, then flow-matching mid-training, then real-world post-training.
  • 93.6% on RoboTwin 2.0 and beats π0.5 on three real tasks under matched HG-DAgger. The code repo is public; weights are not confirmed.

StrataSLAM: open-source multi-floor SLAM for ROS 2 (stairs, elevators, ramps, identical floors)

ROS Discourse
  • Builds one submap per floor, detects stairs, ramp and elevator transitions, and blocks loop closures between floors so identical floors are not merged.
  • Works with RGB-D and/or 3D LiDAR; IMU, barometer and wheel odometry are optional. Apache-2.0, ROS 2 Jazzy, github.com/EasyNavigation/StrataSLAM.
