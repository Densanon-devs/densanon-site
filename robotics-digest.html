Know Your Body: A Harness for Direct and Self-Improving Robot Control with VLMs

ArXiv cs.RO
  • The paper notes general-purpose vision-language models can understand a task goal without knowing how a specific robot's motion and functional parts actually produce that effect.
  • The authors introduce KnowBody, a harness that makes these action-relevant body relations explicit to the model.
  • The goal is more direct, self-improving robot control driven by VLMs.

Morphometric Imitation: From Morphology and Contact Aware Hand Retargeting to Sim-to-Real Visuomotor Policy

ArXiv cs.RO
  • The work uses human hand-object interaction demonstrations as a data source for dexterous manipulation learning.
  • It targets three challenges: bridging morphology gaps between human and robot hands, ensuring dynamical feasibility, and sim-to-real deployment.
  • The approach combines morphology- and contact-aware hand retargeting with a sim-to-real visuomotor policy.

OA-MPPI: Occlusion-Aware Model Predictive Path Integral Control for UAV Flight

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Targets autonomous UAV flight through cluttered, partially unknown environments.
  • Argues that safe flight requires reasoning about occluded regions the sensor cannot observe, not just observed obstacles.
  • Presents OA-MPPI, an obstacle- and occlusion-aware extension to Model Predictive Path Integral control.
