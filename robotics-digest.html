Research & Papers

DexGraspFlow: Topology-Aware SE(3) Flow Matching for Cross-Embodiment Dexterous Grasp Generation

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Targets dexterous grasp generation across robot hands that differ in geometry, joint parameterization and kinematic topology.
  • Uses topology-aware SE(3) flow matching, noting that spatial relations among links alone do not fully capture a hand's kinematic topology.

Replan for Error Recovery: Training Embodied VLMs through Verifiable Interaction with Action-Grouped Turn-GSPO

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Addresses embodied VLMs that need to replan after execution errors leave the environment in a state requiring recovery.
  • Trains them through verifiable interaction using an Action-Grouped Turn-GSPO approach, as an alternative to inference-time mechanisms with fixed policies.

Embodied Playground: A Long-Horizon Agentic Robotics Benchmark for Physical, Strategic, and Spatial Reasoning

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Introduces Embodied Playground, a benchmark for long-horizon agentic robotics tasks.
  • Evaluates foundation models on sustaining coherent reasoning and reliable physical execution across interdependent stages (physical, strategic and spatial reasoning).

Towards Interaction State Recognition in Sensorized Gecko-Inspired Adhesive Interfaces for Orbital Servicing

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Tackles reliable contact establishment for autonomous on-orbit servicing, especially with non-cooperative targets.
