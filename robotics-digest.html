Robotics Morning Digest

Today's research slate leans heavily into the sim-to-real and human-in-the-loop problem: five new ArXiv papers cover LLM-guided robot programming, tactile teleoperation, learned reduced-dynamics simulators, sim-augmented policy evaluation, and sampling-based MPC for humanoids. On the industry side, Waymo detailed the custom 5nm compute stack behind its robotaxis, Amazon is scaling Prime Air drone delivery sixfold to nearly 500 cities, Agtonomy shipped autonomous multi-point turning for ag equipment, and BrainChip brought neuromorphic inference into IBM's workload scheduler. Rounding it out, a community builder shared an early speech demo from a reflex-driven mechanical-skeleton robot.

Research & Papers

APPROVE: Visual End-User-in-the-Loop Robot Programming with LLMs

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Lets non-experts describe tasks in natural language; an LLM generates robot programs that are rendered as Blockly block-diagrams the user can confirm, edit, or reject before execution
  • Confirmed programs are saved into a reusable function library, incrementally building a trusted set of components instead of throwaway one-off generations
  • Accepted at CIRP ICME 2026, framed around trust and intent alignment for manufacturing-floor programming by non-programmers

The Missing Touch: Spatially Distributed Tactile Feedback Brings Teleoperation Closer to Human Dexterity

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Testbed pairs a 2-DoF bilateral force-feedback telemanipulator with a 32-DoF tactile fingertip display to reproduce spatially distributed contact info from the remote gripper on the operator's hand
  • Distributed tactile feedback cut deviation between teleoperated and natural human hand trajectories by 29-79% versus setups without it
  • Higher-resolution tactile feedback also compressed the state-space of teleop motions, a property the authors link to better demonstration data for training autonomous robot policies
Read full article

Learning the Right Abstraction: Neural Reduced Dynamics for Complex Robot Control

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Neural Reduced Dynamics (NRD) trains policies inside a lightweight learned model rather than the full physics sim, then validates them back in the high-fidelity simulator, running roughly four orders of magnitude faster than the simulator it replaces
  • Tested on terrain-aware HMMWV trajectory tracking (rigid and deformable terrain) and a tracked-vehicle-plus-arm goal-reaching task
  • A single terrain-conditioned policy beat single-terrain specialists on all three terrains including zero-shot bumpy terrain; the vehicle hit 100/100 goals and the arm 97/100 with zero contact or joint-limit violations
Read full article

SCAPE: Scenario-Conditioned Simulation-Augmented Policy Evaluation

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Combines a small number of paired sim-and-real samples with large-scale simulation rollouts to predict scenario-conditioned real-world policy performance, correcting sim bias and calibrating uncertainty with conformal prediction
  • Cut scenario-level prediction error by 4.9-34.7% on autonomous-driving tasks and 14.5-27.7% on quadruped velocity tracking versus scene-conditioned-network and aggregate-statistics baselines
  • Validated with real deployment testing on a Unitree Go2 quadruped

Hybrid Feedback Sampling for Sample-Efficient Model Predictive Control

ArXiv cs.RO
  • FS-MPC realizes the optimal sampling proposal distribution via an optimized feedback policy, blending local and global search instead of relying on MPPI-style sampling alone
  • Targets high-dimensional, open-loop-unstable systems where standard sampling-based MPC needs prohibitively many rollouts to converge
  • Validated on contact-rich humanoid locomotion and dexterous manipulation, including real-world humanoid robot experiments

Amazon plans to expand Prime Air to nearly 500 cities by the end of 2026

The Robot Report
  • Expanding from 7 states today to nearly 500 cities and towns by end of 2026, roughly a sixfold increase, with FAA Part 135 certification (the highest level of FAA oversight for commercial drone delivery)
  • Drones carry items up to 5 lb that fit in a large shoebox, delivering in as fast as 30 minutes (most around 60); over 60% of frequently-purchased Amazon products qualify
  • New US metros include Chicago, Syracuse, Cleveland, Atlanta, and Boise, plus Amazon's first international site in Darlington, UK with a 7.5-mile service radius

BrainChip launches Symphony Community Akida bundle for IBM's workload management solution

Robotics & Automation News
  • The free, open-source Symphony Community Akida Bundle lets BrainChip's Akida neuromorphic processors run as a scheduled accelerator inside IBM Spectrum Symphony Community Edition
  • IBM's scheduler automatically routes each workload task to whichever processor (CPU, GPU, or Akida) runs it best, adding event-driven neuromorphic inference to existing heterogeneous compute clusters
  • Pitched for lower-latency, lower-power inference on smaller jobs than GPUs handle efficiently; Akida ships as a PCIe accelerator board

Waymo reveals Nvidia-powered compute system behind its robotaxis

Robotics & Automation News
  • Custom 5nm ASIC delivers over 1,000 TOPS of ML performance for real-time front-end processing of lidar, radar, and 13 high-resolution cameras, including temporal denoising for low-light perception, built on Nvidia accelerated computing plus silicon from AMD, Micron, Samsung, SanDisk, Socionext, and TSMC
  • Runs two independent processing 'engines' for safety: if one faults, the other seamlessly takes over; hardware is ruggedized for vibration, shock, and temperature extremes and tied into the vehicle's liquid cooling loop
  • Raw compute available to the Waymo Driver has scaled 20x over the past eight years, aimed at cutting pixels-to-actuation latency
