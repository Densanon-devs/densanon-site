SoftBank agrees to acquire Robotics and AI Institute

The Robot Report
  • RAI is the Marc Raibert-founded (2022) research spinout created alongside Hyundai's acquisition of Boston Dynamics, backed by $400M+ from Hyundai, whose perception/manipulation/control research underpins Atlas's acrobatic capabilities.
  • Deal terms are undisclosed and the transaction is under CFIUS national-security review; both companies declined to comment.
  • The acquisition pairs with SoftBank's pending $5.3B purchase of ABB Robotics and reunites SoftBank with Raibert (it owned Boston Dynamics 2017-2021); Hyundai is simultaneously buying out SoftBank's remaining 9.65% Boston Dynamics stake.

Neptune Medical earns FDA clearance for its Triton 1 robotic system

The Robot Report
  • Triton 1 is a robotic flexible-endoscopy system for colonoscopy screening, surveillance, and complex resections (EMR/ESD), replacing manual scope control.
  • In the CARE 1 clinical trial it achieved 100% cecal intubation with no adverse events and no patient repositioning or abdominal pressure, plus a 54.2% adenoma detection rate versus the roughly 35% industry benchmark.
  • NASA-TLX workload scoring showed a 67% lower operator burden compared to manual colonoscopy.

Icarus Robotics flies ISS-bound robot in microgravity for the first time

The Robot Report
  • JOY is a free-flying ISS robot with in-house 7-DoF arms and adaptive pinch grippers, built to take over astronaut cargo/logistics work, running on an NVIDIA Jetson Thor T5000 (a notable compute jump over earlier ISS robots like Astrobee).
  • The team ran four parabolic flights (Sept 9-11, 2026; 66 parabolas, about 2 cumulative minutes of weightlessness) validating manipulation, state estimation, and flight control by replaying pre-recorded teleop trajectories against terrestrial baselines.
  • JOY starts teleoperated with autonomy layered in over time; it hands over to NASA on Jan 25, 2027 and launches to the ISS in May 2027 as mission "Joyride-1."

The picks-and-shovels strategy behind the robotics boom

The Robot Report
  • Robo Inc. (Plainview, NY), led by CEO Teddy Haggerty, pivoted its Robostore business from a distribution/reseller play into a robotics education-and-deployment platform.
  • The thesis: the bigger near-term robotics opportunity is supplying the tooling, training, and integration services businesses need to deploy today's robots, not chasing a general-purpose humanoid for every home.
  • Haggerty's path into robotics ran through earlier ventures in drones, crypto infrastructure, and safety helmets, framed as a case study in riding a sector boom via supporting infrastructure rather than building the core hardware.
