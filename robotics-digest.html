Awomo-SimDataEngine: Agentic Simulation-ReadyWorld Generation

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Argues useful robot-training data needs more than visually plausible scenes: objects must support interaction and placements must stay physically valid.
  • Tasks must also admit repeatable execution; Awomo-SimDataEngine is an agentic system for generating simulation-ready worlds.

World-Calibrated Proposal-to-Action Flow for Vision-Language-Action Models

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Flow-based Vision-Language-Action policies generate action chunks by transporting samples from a task-agnostic isotropic Gaussian source.
  • The paper notes this source is conditioned on neither recent execution nor predicted future evolution, and proposes a world-calibrated proposal-to-action flow.

SoTa: Soft Tactile Skins for Dexterous Manipulation

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Tactile sensing is argued to give robot policies contact information that complements vision in dexterous manipulation.
  • Visuo-tactile robot data remains scarce because dexterous demonstrations require teleoperation; SoTa introduces soft tactile skins to address this.

Filter-Aware Fine-Tuning for Safe Humanoid Whole-Body Tracking

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Safe whole-body motion is described as essential for deploying humanoids in unstructured environments.
  • Modern humanoid control separates reference specification (planner, teleoperator, or motion generator) from execution; the paper proposes filter-aware fine-tuning for safe tracking.
