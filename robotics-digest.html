Research & Papers

BFMTrack: Latent Sequence Optimization for Physics-Based Motion Tracking with Behavioral Foundation Models

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Behavioral Foundation Models (BFMs) organize large-scale motion datasets into a physics-consistent latent space, enabling richer character control than single-motion imitation approaches.
  • The paper introduces latent sequence optimization to track reference motions inside that latent space, keeping generated movement physically plausible even for complex, diverse behaviors.
  • Points toward a unified controller that can handle a broad repertoire of motions without per-skill retraining.

Invariant Kalman filtering for extended pose estimation in multi-IMU articulated rigid-body systems

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Extends the Invariant Extended Kalman Filter (IEKF) to articulated multi-segment bodies instrumented with multiple IMUs, covering orientation, velocity, and position together.
  • Addresses a key gap in wearable robotics and exoskeletons where joint kinematics couple IMU readings in ways standard per-sensor filters fail to model.
  • Demonstrates improved drift and accuracy over treating each IMU independently across multi-link systems.

ADM-Fusion: Adaptive Deep Multi-Sensor Fusion for Robust Ego-Motion Estimation in Diverse Conditions

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Proposes an adaptive fusion architecture that dynamically reweights sensor modalities (camera, lidar, radar, IMU) based on real-time reliability estimates rather than fixed fusion rules.
  • Targets degraded environments — fog, rain, featureless tunnels — where individual sensors fail unpredictably and static fusion schemes break down.
  • Achieves more consistent ego-motion accuracy across varied conditions without manual per-sensor tuning.

SurveilNav: Collaborative Object Goal Navigation with Robot and Surveillance System

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Proposes tighter integration between fixed surveillance camera networks and mobile robots for object-goal navigation, using shared field-of-view to cut search time.
  • The surveillance system provides top-down scene context the robot cannot see on its own, letting it prioritize where to physically search.
  • Opens a practical path to deploying robots in already-instrumented facilities without additional sensor infrastructure.

AeroCast: Probabilistic 3D Trajectory Prediction for Non-Cooperative Aerial Obstacles via Transformer-MDN Architecture

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Combines a Transformer encoder with a Mixture Density Network to output probability distributions over future 3D trajectories of non-cooperative drones or aircraft.
  • The MDN head explicitly models multimodal future paths rather than collapsing to a single trajectory, properly representing the uncertainty of intent-unknown obstacles.
  • Directly targets the UAM and drone-delivery safety problem where evasive maneuvers must be planned under hard time constraints.
