Google DeepMind says Gemini Robotics 2 enables full body control

The Robot Report
  • DeepMind claims Gemini Robotics 2 lets robots reason through every movement, not just plan an end-effector trajectory.
  • Full-body control unlocks tasks that need coordinated base, torso and limb motion — reaching low, working in clutter, maintaining balance under load.
  • The generalist-model-as-controller thesis keeps advancing; the open question remains latency and reliability under real-world perturbation.

QERRA-v2 Classical: Two-Layer Moral & Physical Safety Middleware for ROS 2 & Behavior Trees

ROS Discourse
  • Open-source, fully explainable middleware that evaluates autonomous robot actions before they execute.
  • QERRA-HSR, the physical safety layer, is zero-ML pure Python: it scores hazard proximity and human presence deterministically, so its decisions are auditable.
  • The two-layer split — deterministic physical gate underneath a higher-level policy layer — is a pattern worth copying anywhere a model proposes actions that touch the real world.
