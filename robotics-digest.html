A small deterministic gate between the planner and the wheels: ROS 2 Jazzy node, simulation and CI only

ROS Discourse
  • A Simplex-style supervisor treats Nav2, LLM agents and teleop as unverified proposers. It clamps /cmd_vel against a lidar stopping envelope and trips on stale proposals (150 ms or older), sensor stalls or distress telemetry.
  • A trip latches zero velocity and needs a 1 s clear window plus an explicit reset. Decision logic is a pure function, and every tick goes into a SHA-256 hash-chained log for offline replay.
  • The author is upfront about limits: simulation only, a software zero is not Safe Torque Off, and timer jitter has not been profiled.
