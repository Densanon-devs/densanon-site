Robotics Morning Digest

Today's robotics digest leans heavily on humanoid and quadruped manipulation research: CHOREO composes thousands of pretrained skills into long action sequences on a Unitree G1, DeViGrasp benchmarks grasping under degraded vision, and OJOx captures construction demonstrations tied to changing design specs. On the industry side, Alphabet's Intrinsic open-sourced its manipulation platform under Apache 2.0, the Indy Autonomous Challenge logged its first road-course overtakes at Laguna Seca, and Boston Dynamics opened a dedicated Atlas training facility inside Hyundai's Georgia Metaplant. Research & Papers

CHOREO: Every Humanoid Skill as a Trajectory

ArXiv cs.RO
  • CHOREO converts any learned humanoid skill, regardless of whether it came from reinforcement learning, motion imitation, or generative modeling, into a unified SkillMotion representation of motion states, contacts, semantics, and boundary conditions, then composes skills via direct continuation, cubic Hermite blending, or validated bridge motions without retraining any source model.
  • Tested on a Unitree G1 in MuJoCo, the framework organized 2,950 admitted SkillMotion assets pulled from heterogeneous sources and reached 95.4% sequence success across 130 multi-action tasks, including 93.8% success on eight-action sequences.

D3DWA: Adaptive Weight and Prediction-Horizon for Dynamic Window Approach via Dueling Double Deep Q-Network

ArXiv cs.RO
  • D3DWA uses a Dueling Double Deep Q-Network to jointly pick the Dynamic Window Approach's evaluation weights and prediction horizon at every control step from the current navigation state, instead of relying on the fixed parameters standard DWA uses.
  • Across eight simulated environments, including layouts unseen during training, the adaptive policy reached every goal, and on a real robot it completed all three tested configurations, including a constrained case where a weights-only variant timed out.

DeViGrasp: Robust Visual Mobile Grasping for Quadruped Manipulators under Degraded Perception

ArXiv cs.RO
  • The paper introduces DeViGrasp-Bench, a benchmark that subjects quadruped-manipulator grasping to controlled visual degradations (occlusion, segmentation-mask dropout, depth noise, target-localization jitter) across seen and unseen objects and difficulty levels, since prior work assumed reliable perception.
  • Its companion model, DeViGrasp-Net, trains a deployable student policy from a privileged offline teacher; the student fuses dual-view segmented-depth observations with Target Hold Memory and Temporal Memory Attention, hitting 62.3% task success under degraded conditions, beating baseline VBC by 16.1 points and DQ-Net by 4.3 points, with the DQ-Net margin widening to 10.5 points on harder settings.

OJOx: Specification-Conditioned Demonstrations for Embodied AI in Construction

ArXiv cs.RO
  • OJOx captures what the authors call specification-conditioned demonstrations for construction robotics: a headset overlays design geometry onto the physical workspace and records a demonstrator's stereo passthrough view synchronized with whole-body and hand motion, so the data records not just what a worker did but the design spec that gave the action its purpose.
  • The authors report one fully instrumented session, a 33-component wall built against a specification that changes mid-task, verified against an independently registered external camera and successfully replayed onto a Unitree G1 in simulation.

Product & Industry

Intrinsic open sources key parts of its platform for easier development

The Robot Report
  • Alphabet's Intrinsic released Intrinsic Core, a ROS-compatible, Apache 2.0-licensed collection covering hardware-agnostic real-time control, 6-DoF pose estimation built on NVIDIA's FoundationPose, motion and grasp planning, Gazebo-based simulation, camera calibration, and ROS drivers, announced at ROSCon Toronto on September 22.
  • Intrinsic also unveiled the Open Machine Tending Solution, a reference design for CNC machine tending that supports FANUC and Universal Robots hardware; current scope targets manipulation tasks for ROS-familiar developers and excludes mobile robots and humanoids.

IAC completes autonomous racing event at Laguna Seca

The Robot Report
  • Unimore Racing, from the University of Modena and Reggio Emilia, beat Purdue AI Racing in the final heat of the Indy Autonomous Challenge at Laguna Seca, the first time IAC cars have overtaken each other on a road course rather than an oval.
  • Nine university teams ran identical Dallara-based AV-24s with Honda engines, Luminar sensors, and NVIDIA GPUs in one-on-one heats where the trailing car had two laps to pass; teams first had to qualify in a software-in-the-loop D-Space simulation before running on the physical track, a first for the series.

Boston Dynamics opens Metaplant Application Center to train Atlas humanoids

The Robot Report
  • Boston Dynamics, now wholly owned by Hyundai Motor Group, opened the Robotics Metaplant Application Center inside Hyundai's Metaplant America near Savannah, Georgia, to train Atlas humanoids on automotive parts logistics: sequencing components, handling delicate cosmetic surfaces, bimanual manipulation of awkward items, and extracting parts from shipping packaging.
  • The center uses teleoperation, simulated reinforcement learning, and Universal Manipulation Interface devices for data collection; Hyundai plans a roughly 10x-larger facility next year, assembly-task expansion by 2030, and manufacturing up to 30,000 robots annually by 2028 toward deploying 25,000 Atlas units across its global plants.

Stress-testing a moral execution guard beyond direct language (QERRA-v2 Classical)

ROS Discourse
  • QERRA-v2 Classical is a text-directive guard for robot behavior trees, tested in Webots on a PAL Robotics TIAGo, that screens prospective commands for disguised coercion before an action is committed; the author ran a 20-case adversarial benchmark using Cloaked Attack / Unmasked Baseline / Benign Twin triplets to isolate specific failure modes.
  • Results showed affectionate or polite phrasing reduced embedding similarity 13-16 percentage points, letting violations slip past the filter as safe, while conflict-related vocabulary sometimes triggered false-positive refusals during genuine accountability requests; the write-up attributes this to a ceiling on bi-encoder embeddings and includes reproducible benchmark logs.

r/robotics
  • A hobbyist's second-generation low-cost bipedal robot, CBR-II, is walking, though the builder notes the control policy was trained for more powerful actuators than the build actually uses, which causes it to overshoot its steps.
