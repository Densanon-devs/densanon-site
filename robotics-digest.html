Eternal.ag scales fully-autonomous harvesting robots with first customer Van Noord Growers

Robotics & Automation News
  • German agritech startup eternal.ag deployed its fully-autonomous Harvester robot at Van Noord Growers, a Dutch greenhouse operator covering 8.5 hectares of tomatoes and cucumbers.
  • This is eternal.ag's first commercial customer deployment, moving the product from demo stage to paid production use.

Kirisense wins funding to develop robotic fingertips that can sense touch and slip

Robotics & Automation News
  • UK startup Kirisense secured Henry Royce Institute funding to build tactile fingertips capable of detecting both contact force and slip — a key gap in current robot grippers.
  • Slip detection is critical for dexterous manipulation; most commercial grippers today rely on vision or simple force thresholds rather than true tactile feedback.
  • The technology targets industrial manipulation tasks where grasp reliability matters more than raw speed.

Interview with Jun Wu of GMEX Robotics: 'We provide an integrated terminal + brain closed-loop system'

Robotics & Automation News
  • GMEX Robotics argues the real bottleneck is hardware-software co-design, not just AI model capability — their approach tightly integrates the compute 'brain' with the physical 'terminal'.
  • The closed-loop system is designed to reduce latency between perception, decision, and actuation compared to loosely coupled AI-on-robot stacks.
  • GMEX positions itself against pure-software foundation model companies by emphasizing that robot intelligence cannot be fully decoupled from its specific hardware.

Fanuc-powered robotic cell automates one of furniture manufacturing's toughest jobs

Robotics & Automation News
  • A Canadian furniture manufacturer deployed a Fanuc M-710iC robotic cell to automate upholstery — historically one of the last holdouts against automation due to irregular materials and variable geometry.
  • The system was built by Fanuc Authorized Integrator Dvolu and handles a sequence of tasks that previously required skilled manual labor.
  • The deployment demonstrates that manipulation of soft, deformable materials is increasingly within reach of industrial robot cells.
