Research & Papers

PGDG: Physically Grounded Data Generation for Robust Bimanual Policy Learning from a Single Demonstration

ArXiv cs.RO
  • A single human demonstration is augmented with physics-grounded synthetic data to train contact-rich bimanual manipulation policies, dramatically cutting the collection burden.
  • The approach generates recovery supervision for off-manifold states — the core failure mode of standard behavior cloning — by simulating disturbances and their corrections.
  • Robustness to perturbation improves significantly over baselines trained on the same single demo without augmentation.

Closed-Loop Sim-to-Real Reinforcement Learning for Deformable Microfiber Shape Control

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Microscale surface interactions are notoriously hard to model, making sim-to-real transfer for micromanipulation largely impractical — this paper closes that gap with a closed-loop RL approach.
  • The system learns to control deformable microfiber shape through contact without relying on accurate physical models, using real-world feedback to iteratively refine the policy instead.
  • Results suggest the framework generalizes to other contact-based micro-manipulation tasks where surface forces dominate.

Robot Talk Episode 157 – Generating new robot designs, with Josie Hughes

Robohub

Open-Source Software Is Starting to Help Robots Think

IEEE Spectrum Robotics
  • Hugging Face, NVIDIA, and other major players are open-sourcing the AI cognition stack for robots, mirroring the earlier wave of open-source hardware that saved researchers years of work.
  • The shift makes robot reasoning — perception, planning, and language grounding — accessible to labs and startups that couldn't afford to build it from scratch.
  • The article notes this is still early, but the trajectory is clear: open-source AI will be as foundational to robotics as ROS was for motion control.

Community Highlights

Custom protocol, sub-40-ms Latency Teleoperation software

r/robotics
  • The Adamo teleoperation system achieves sub-40ms end-to-end latency for SF-to-London remote robot control by using a custom UDP-based protocol instead of WebRTC, eliminating WebRTC's buffering overhead.
  • Low-latency teleop is critical for collecting high-quality imitation learning data and for real-time human-in-the-loop correction during deployment.
  • The custom protocol trades WebRTC's built-in reliability for raw speed — a deliberate engineering choice where stale frames are worse than dropped ones.

Hand taxonomy tests with my robotic hand & wrist

r/robotics
  • A hobbyist validates their custom robotic hand against the Yale GRASP taxonomy (Feix et al., 2016), a standard 33-grasp classification used in manipulation research.
  • The hand covers the majority of power and precision grips but lacks independent distal phalanx control for inferior pinch and disk grasps — an honest capability gap rarely surfaced in community demos.
  • Testing against a published taxonomy rather than ad-hoc demos is a useful model for the maker community to evaluate real dexterity coverage.
