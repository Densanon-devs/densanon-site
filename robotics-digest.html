Research & Papers

Ctrl-F: Force as an Interface for Humanoid Loco-Manipulation

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Adds an explicit 3-D force command per designated contact body (hand or foot) to a humanoid motion tracker, so it specifies both where contact happens and how hard to push.
  • A hierarchical design pairs a unified motion-force controller with a high-level planner that issues force commands online from object state and proprioception.
  • On hardware it handled boxes up to 20 kg in pushing and kicking tasks where a force-blind baseline fails, and adapted when extra load was added mid-task.

MESA: An Evaluation Framework for Compositional, Semantic, and Spatial Generalization in Robotics

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Five evaluation suites cover in-distribution performance plus generalization over spatial configuration, object instance, object category and subtask composition; accepted to CoRL 2026.
  • Includes MESA-Gen, a task and demonstration generation pipeline improving on MimicGen, and BiMESA, described as the first large-scale bimanual generalization benchmark.
  • Across seven baselines (diffusion policies, VLM-based methods, pretrained VLAs) the best reached 52% on MESA and 49% on BiMESA; rankings broadly track RoboArena scores.

ELA: End-to-End Latent Action Policy Learning from Video Demonstrations

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Trains a latent action model, latent action policy and action decoder jointly, so latent actions are optimized for control rather than learned separately from observation transitions.
  • Lets policies exploit large amounts of action-free video with only a small set of action-labeled robot demonstrations.
  • Consistently beat prior latent-action baselines in simulated and real manipulation; it plugs into existing imitation learning methods through 4M-parameter action decoders.

Jev with Foresight: Safe Language-Guided Autonomous Driving via a Language World Model

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Jev is a typed decision model that answers predefined questions with probabilities instead of generating text; a language world model predicts nearby vehicles' motion over the next two seconds for each candidate action.
  • In simulated highway merging (500 car-episodes), foresight cut collision rate from 26.0% to 2.4% at 0.25 s per decision, with passenger intent fulfilled in 93% of free-form command trials.
  • On replayed NGSIM traffic (500 episodes) collisions fell from 48.2% to 2.8%; the world model foresaw 93% of dangerous actions.

Contact-Implicit Whole-Body Trajectory Optimization for Dynamic Humanoid Locomotion on Parameterizable Uneven Terrain

ArXiv cs.RO
  • A contact-implicit whole-body planner generates humanoid motion without predefined contact sequences, using the robot's full dynamics and kinematics.
  • Ground geometry is approximated by a smooth surface, and new contact constraints extend planning from flat floors to steps, ramps and other arbitrary smooth terrain.
  • Preliminary feasibility tests on non-coplanar terrain ran in simulation and on the real ErgoCub robot.

AGIBOT unveils GO-3 Preview and takes top spot on three embodied AI benchmarks

Robotics & Automation News
  • AGIBOT says GO-3 Preview ranks first on RoboDojo, RoboTwin and RoboColiseum, but the article gives no scores or margins; it says the model will be fully open-sourced by the end of 2026.
  • Its WRAM architecture unifies task understanding, future-state prediction and action generation, trained on human first-person video and robot trajectories.
  • Post-training pools experience across 100 physical robots plus simulation; in a convenience store the company says a Genie G2 robot adjusted its strategies without human demonstrations. These are company claims without independent measurements.

Are Drone Ports the Next Annoying Tech Neighbor?

IEEE Spectrum Robotics
  • Residents near an Amazon MK30 operation in Baton Rouge counted one drone every 48 seconds on a Saturday morning; the MK30 weighs about 36 kg and must fly low to drop packages.
  • On September 28, 15 US states and Harris County, Texas sued the FAA over eased environmental review rules for drone sites; Cobb County, Georgia denied a zoning request for a Wing facility in April.
  • Expansion plans are large: Wing aims for 270 US hubs by end of 2027, and Uber and Zipline target 1 million deliveries a day by end of 2029.

ManyForge: a new manipulation framework

ROS Discourse
  • An evolution of ManyMove, now in beta, with a ROS-free C++/Python core and ROS as an integration layer to stock ros2_control/JTC and vendor drivers.
  • A Composer GUI builds programs as behavior trees with a 3D scene, collision-checked ghost-robot manual moves and Draft + Apply; Mock, simulation and real-robot modes are meant to behave the same.
  • Uses RoboPlan as the default planner; documentation is limited so far and the post does not state a license.
