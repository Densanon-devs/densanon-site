Research & Papers

Optimize, Learn, Refine: Whole-Body Grasping and Pick-and-Throw with a Spiral Soft Robot

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Addresses whole-body grasping and pick-and-throw with a spiral soft continuum robot, starting from an initially ungrasped state.
  • Soft continuum robots can exploit distributed compliance for whole-body manipulation, but synthesizing behavior through changing contacts remains difficult.

Fiatlux: A Long-Horizon Benchmark for Humanoid Ladder Climbing and Light-Bulb Replacement

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Introduces a benchmark for humanoid ladder climbing and light-bulb replacement in a single long-horizon episode.
  • Existing benchmarks treat tabletop manipulation, flat-floor household activity, and humanoid locomotion separately; none scores vertical mobility and dexterous work on a fragile payload together.

SynIL: Leveraging Synergy for Offline Imitation Learning from Imperfect Demonstration Datasets

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Targets offline imitation learning from demonstration datasets contaminated with suboptimal or noisy demonstrations.
  • Notes that performance degrades severely on such data, and proposes leveraging synergy rather than relying on prior quality-assessment metrics alone.

TALK-Dem: Benchmarking Embodied Task Planning under Dementia-Associated Communication Patterns

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Benchmarks LLM-driven embodied task planning under communication patterns associated with dementia.
  • Argues current planners assume an ideal user whose instructions are clear, complete, and task-focused, which fails for users with cognitive impairment.
