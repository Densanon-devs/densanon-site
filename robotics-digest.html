Research & Papers

OrbiSim: World Models as Differentiable Physics Engines for Embodied Intelligence

ArXiv cs.RO
  • OrbiSim reframes world models not as latent-space imagination engines but as fully differentiable physics simulators, enabling gradient flow through contact dynamics and rigid-body constraints.
  • Unlike prior visual/latent world models, OrbiSim enforces physical laws as hard constraints rather than learned dynamics, which the authors argue closes the sim-to-real gap more reliably.
  • Differentiability through the physics layer enables gradient-based policy optimization directly through simulated contact events without separate surrogate models.
  • The architecture is designed for embodied intelligence tasks where physical plausibility of predicted futures is required, not just visual realism.

SCAR: Self-Supervised Continuous Action Representation Learning

ArXiv cs.RO
  • SCAR learns transferable action representations from visual transitions without task-specific labels, targeting the core cross-embodiment generalization problem.
  • Self-supervised training signal comes from predicting future visual states given action embeddings, forcing the representation to capture action semantics rather than robot-specific kinematics.
  • The method addresses a key bottleneck in foundation models for robotics: actions lack a universal representation analogous to tokens in language models.
  • Generalization across embodiments under limited data is the explicit target — the paper is not a sim benchmark but a cross-robot transfer result.

Support-Safe Variational Hybrid Filtering for Contact-Mode and Sparse-Law Recovery

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Proposes a variational Bayesian filter that tracks contact-mode transitions (free flight, impact, stick-slip) without permanently collapsing probability mass off feasible transitions.
  • Standard amortized filters fail in contact-rich manipulation because they assign zero probability to valid contact regime changes — support-safety prevents that degeneracy.
  • The method enables simultaneous recovery of discrete contact modes and sparse physical laws (e.g., friction coefficients) from raw observations.
  • Targeted at contact-rich manipulation pipelines where reliable state estimation across hybrid dynamic regimes is the state-estimation bottleneck.

MR-SLAM: Immersive Spatial Supervision for Multi-Robot Mapping via Mixed Reality

ArXiv cs.RO
  • MR-SLAM overlays each robot's live position and mapping state into a mixed-reality view, giving the operator a fused spatial picture without switching between per-robot dashboards.
  • Designed for multi-robot fleets doing building inspection or warehouse-aisle SLAM, where operator spatial awareness degrades as fleet size grows.
  • The MR interface provides immersive supervision rather than telemetry readouts, enabling faster operator intervention when a robot's map diverges from ground truth.
  • The mapping algorithms are standard — the contribution is the supervision interface architecture that scales oversight to heterogeneous fleets.

Haptic Rendering of Fractional-Order Viscoelasticity: Passivity and Rendering Fidelity

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Addresses haptic rendering of biological tissue that exhibits creep and stress relaxation — behaviors integer-order spring-damper models cannot accurately capture.
  • Fractional-order viscoelastic models fit soft tissue mechanics better, but rendering them on a haptic device raises passivity concerns where energy can leak and cause unstable force feedback.
  • The paper derives passivity conditions for fractional-order impedance rendering, establishing safe parameter bounds for medical training simulators.
  • Rendering fidelity analysis quantifies how closely the haptic interface can match the target fractional model within those passivity-safe constraints.

Home Robot Safety Is All About Relationships

IEEE Spectrum Robotics
  • ISO is revising its 12-year-old personal care robot safety standard, which predates modern domestic humanoids and the body of research on human-robot relationship dynamics.
  • The update shifts focus from mechanical hazard prevention toward the social and relational dimensions of safety — trust, dependence, and long-term cohabitation all introduce risks the original standard did not model.
  • Researchers now distinguish physical safety (collision, force limits) from relational safety (manipulation, privacy, emotional dependency), both of which the new standard must address.
  • The revision has direct regulatory implications for humanoid robots entering homes, where the standards framework has lagged the hardware by over a decade.

Agentic AI for Robot Teams

IEEE Spectrum Robotics
  • Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory presents a scalable agentic AI architecture for coordinating heterogeneous robot teams across autonomy, coordination, and adaptability challenges.
  • Uses agentic AI as the coordination layer rather than hand-coded task allocation, allowing the system to generalize across mission types without per-scenario reprogramming.
  • Heterogeneous fleet support is a first-class design goal — the architecture is not restricted to homogeneous swarms, targeting real-world mixed deployments.
  • The framing from APL is significant: this is defense-adjacent research applying LLM-based agents to multi-robot military and emergency-response contexts.

Einride deploys autonomous electric trucks in Ohio freight corridor pilot

Robotics & Automation News
  • Einride has deployed SAE Level 4 autonomous electric trucks in revenue-generating proof-of-concept service between EASE Logistics warehouses in Marysville, Ohio.
  • This is a real L4 deployment on a fixed freight corridor under the Ohio DOT and DriveOhio Truck Automation Pilot — not a closed test track.
  • The corridor approach reflects the current practical ceiling for L4 trucking: well-defined ODD (operational design domain) with repeatable routes and minimal edge-case variability.
  • Einride's pod-style trucks are purpose-designed for autonomous operation rather than retrofitting autonomy onto conventional cab-over hardware.

Hacker News Robotics
