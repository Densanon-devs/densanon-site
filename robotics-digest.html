Research & Papers

Warping the Workspace: Expanding Visual Access with Adjustable Reach Mapping for Humanoid Teleoperation

ArXiv cs.RO
  • First-person humanoid teleoperation normally ties access to side and rear targets to the operator's own rotation.
  • The paper proposes coupled visual-motor remapping: a panoramic azimuth warp paired with an inverse upper-limb yaw transform.
  • A working prototype implements the approach.

VeriCalib: Verifiable Target-Based LiDAR-Camera Calibration

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Target-based extrinsic calibration estimates the rigid transform between sensors from a few observations of a known target.
  • The authors note that the usual RMS residual error is a scalar that does not fully verify calibration quality, and propose a verifiable approach.
Read full article

Real-Time Ground Normal Vector Estimation for Spherical Mobile 3D Mapping using LiDAR-Inertial Odometry

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Ground plane normal estimation is key to odometry in spherical mobile mapping systems, which face rapid rotations and limited ground coverage.
  • The paper proposes two RANSAC-based approaches, including single-scan estimation.
Read full article

Human-Robot Interaction via Meta-Learning Based Mode Recognition and Adaptive Control

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Humans may guide a robot actively or follow it passively, each requiring different control objectives.
  • The work targets recognizing the interaction mode via meta-learning rather than relying on predefined switching rules, and adapting control accordingly.
