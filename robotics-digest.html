Robotics Morning Digest

Today's robotics landscape is defined by the push to close the dexterity gap: ABB and Psyonic are mining human prosthetic data to teach robotic hands, Flexiv is shipping force-controlled arms with tactile sensing, and General Intuition is pouring $320M into an unconventional bet on video game footage as robot training data.
