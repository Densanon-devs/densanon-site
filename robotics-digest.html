Research & Papers

TinyCVIO: A Constellation-Aided Visual-Inertial Odometry System for Nanodrones

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Targets nanodrones, which need real-time state estimation despite severe sensing and compute constraints.
  • Co-designs miniature sensing, visual processing, and estimation together rather than treating them as separate stages.
  • Built on a commodity dual-sensor setup, aiming to make constellation-aided VIO practical at nanodrone scale.

POIL: Point-based One-Shot Imitation Learning with Stable Dynamical Systems

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Learns manipulation skills from a single demonstration instead of large demo datasets.
  • Combines a point-based representation with stable dynamical systems to keep learned trajectories well-behaved.
  • Argues successful one-shot manipulation needs more than copying a trajectory — the underlying dynamics must generalize too.

Actively Resolving Contextual Uncertainty for Underspecified Tasks in Natural Language

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Challenges the common assumption that language-conditioned robot policies always receive fully-specified goals.
  • Foundation models let robots interpret language and reason about context, but underspecified instructions still leave gaps.
  • Proposes robots actively resolve missing task-relevant information rather than acting on ambiguous instructions as-is.

WALT: Learning World-Model-Aligned Latent Trajectories for Autonomous Driving

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Points out that accurate visual prediction from driving world models doesn't automatically translate into good trajectory planning.
  • Learns latent trajectories aligned with the world model's own predictive representations to close that gap.
  • Targets the bottleneck between rich environmental prediction and actual driving decisions.

VkVIO: Cross-platform GPU Acceleration for Visual-Inertial Odometry with Vulkan

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Uses the Vulkan API to GPU-accelerate visual-inertial odometry and VI-SLAM across platforms, for robotics and XR alike.
  • Aims at efficiency, since VIO/VI-SLAM is a proven but computationally demanding approach to state estimation.
  • A cross-platform GPU design could make real-time VIO more portable across different hardware.

Small, medium or large, a robotic fish maintains its swimming ability

Robohub
  • EPFL CREATE Lab's scalable robot ScaFi maintains swimming performance across small, medium, and large sizes.
  • Motivated by the limits of propeller-driven underwater vehicles: spinning blades snag on vegetation, stir up sediment, and startle wildlife.
  • Fish-like propulsion is being explored as a less invasive way to monitor aquatic environments at multiple robot scales.
