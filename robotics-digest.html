Research & Papers

AI discovers a hidden heart signal that could predict sudden death

ScienceDaily
  • UC Berkeley researchers trained an AI model on more than 440,000 Swedish EKGs linked to death certificates, using ordinary EKG images already collected in hospitals.
  • Conventional screening based on how much blood the heart pumps per beat flagged a group with a 4.6% annual sudden cardiac death rate, versus 7% for the AI-flagged group.
  • It was validated on San Diego and Taipei datasets, and the AI also flagged a broader group at elevated risk that conventional methods would mostly call low risk.

Embodied AI unveils AIR humanoid robot designed to learn factory tasks on the job

Robotics & Automation News
  • Swiss company Embodied AI unveiled AIR at Humanoid Europe 2026: a tendon-driven humanoid with motors in the torso, light compliant arms and a wheeled base, designed to ISO/TS 15066:2016 with CE marking planned for 2027.
  • Each job starts under human operator control while every run is recorded; the company says AIR typically learns the task on its own within about a month.
  • The company reports 98% reliability on a cable-handling test against a 90% goal, in a test where it says five funded startups did not meet targets.

MOCAP4ROS2 2.0.0 released: one interface for OptiTrack, Vicon, Qualisys and Gazebo, new tools and website

Open Robotics Discourse
  • One branch now builds for ROS 2 Humble, Jazzy, Kilted and Lyrical (plus Rolling), with drivers for OptiTrack, Vicon (DataStream SDK 1.13), Qualisys QTM, a Gazebo plugin and a simulated system.
  • New tools include a ros2 mocap4r2 CLI with a doctor subcommand, a terminal UI, robot ground-truth tools with online ATE/RPE and TUM export, and human-motion tools with C3D export.
  • Migrating from 1.x replaces mocap4r2_msgs with mocap4r2_interfaces; drivers now publish TF themselves via a publish_tf parameter.
