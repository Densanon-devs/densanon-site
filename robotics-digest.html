Today's robotics digest is anchored by a strong run of arXiv research on 'Physical AI' -- a Real2Sim2Real VLA manipulation pipeline built on AMD ROCm, a world-cognition model for human-robot interaction, and a data-scaling-law study for high-precision manipulation. Real2Sim2Real for Vision-Language-Action Manipulation: An AMD ROCm-Based Pipeline

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Presents a Real2Sim2Real pipeline for training/evaluating vision-language-action (VLA) manipulation policies, built on AMD's ROCm stack rather than the default CUDA/Nvidia path

The Curse of Precision: A Data Scaling Law for High-Precision Robotic Manipulation

ArXiv cs.RO
  • Systematically studies how data scaling laws differ between open-world generalization tasks and closed-world, high-precision tasks like robotic assembly
  • Finds the data-to-precision relationship for closed-world tasks is largely unexplored and behaves differently than open-world imitation-learning scaling
  • Conceptually relevant to any project reasoning about how much training/fine-tuning data is needed for precision-critical behaviors (e.g., ALM extraction accuracy, ultralight-coder tool-call precision)

False-green mobility on firm soil — Dual check on your URDF (PASS or FAIL both count)

ROS Discourse
