Can language models drive a humanoid robot?

Artificial Analysis
  • AA-Robotics-Sim has coding agents write Python to drive a simulated Unitree G1 through 10 household and warehouse tasks in Isaac Sim, after 5 practice episodes each.
  • GPT-6.2 Astra leads at 71.0, then GPT-6.2 Sol Max (62.6) and Claude Fable 5.2 (57.5); every model aced navigation.
  • Grasping is the wall: 6 dishes racked in 100 episodes and zero laundry loaded, and the robot moves only 1-4% of the time.

Robbyant open-sources AI models to improve robotic navigation and spatial memory

Robotics & Automation News
  • LingBot-Map 2.0 corrects its spatial memory without reprocessing images, cutting trajectory error on a 5.2 km route from 31.12 m to 4.72 m.
  • LingBot-Navi keeps persistent spatial memory across missions and takes natural-language commands, hitting 68.6% on a GOAT-Bench subset.
  • Code, weights and a 511-scene real-world dataset are public.

Introducing DWARL: A fast Reinforcement Learning based local controller for Nav2

ROS Discourse
  • DWARL trains a PPO policy that maps sensor data to a local control action and runs it as a Nav2 controller plugin.
  • The TurtleBot demo converges in about 20 minutes, suiting compute-limited robots.
  • Open source for differential-drive robots; the authors flag wobbly motion from current rewards and invite contributions.

Omnibot Reborn: Giving a 1984 Robot Butler the Brain It Was Promised

Hacker News Robotics
  • A 1984 Tomy Omnibot got a Raspberry Pi 5 and an IMX500 camera running YOLO11 nano on-chip, driving the original motors with audio tones.
  • A cloud LLM driver took about 15 s per decision and always said 'right'; about 50 lines of rules replaced it.
  • The LLM now only narrates what the robot sees; the code is MIT-licensed.
