HakemBench: A Turkish Benchmark of Typed Decisions

ArXiv cs.CL
  • Turkish benchmark of typed decisions: the model reads a text, a question and fixed options, and returns a probability for every option.

Finding the Move Is Not Winning the Game: XiangqiBench for Closed-Loop Evaluation of LLM Agents

ArXiv cs.CL
  • Argues static evaluations credit naming the right move but not carrying a plan to a verified outcome against a responding opponent.

Counterexample Generation via Per-Theorem Symbolic Verifiers: When Imitation Hurts and Reinforcement Repairs

ArXiv cs.CL
  • Finds LLMs often solve a theorem forward yet fail to disprove a closely related false one, a falsification gap.
  • Reports supervised fine-tuning does not close the gap and can worsen it; the title points to reinforcement learning as the repair.
