AutoPILOT: A Visual Agent Harness for Real-Time Gameplay

ArXiv cs.AI
  • Multimodal models play games well when the game waits for them but often fail when it keeps running.

Language Model Harnesses can be Compositional Generalizers

ArXiv cs.CL
  • Standard agent harnesses accumulate context that the neural network must generalize to, making long-horizon training expensive and slow.

ArXiv cs.CL
  • Judging whether a response is harmful involves contextual and normative choices, yet red-teaming studies often hand that judgment to automated evaluators.

ArXiv cs.CL

torchtsetlin: Fast, Interpretable Tsetlin Machines for PyTorch

ArXiv cs.AI
  • The Tsetlin machine learns propositional rules with teams of automata driven by reward and penalty rather than gradients, so models are interpretable by construction and cheap to evaluate.
