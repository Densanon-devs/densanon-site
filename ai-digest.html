Research & Papers

The Ignition Index: Measuring Global Workspace Dynamics in Language Models

ArXiv cs.AI
  • Introduces a sigmoid-fit metric over per-layer linear-probe accuracy that measures whether a transformer's internal representation shifts are abrupt ("ignition") or gradual, validated across 11 models in 5 architecture families with 9.6x selectivity over shuffled-label controls.
  • Feedforward transformers showed 89% stronger ignition signatures than State Space Models; Mamba showed near-linear (no-ignition) profiles.
  • In Pythia-410M, ignition strength jumped 67% at training step 256, preceding induction-head formation - a possible early signal of emerging capability.

Woodpecker Distillation: Weak Models Diagnose Reasoning Bugs in Strong Models

ArXiv cs.AI
  • Argues many LLM reasoning failures are localized bugs in specific intermediate steps rather than global incompetence, and are fixable with targeted patches.
  • Weak "probe" models generate corrective patches for a strong model's reasoning errors; instead of fine-tuning directly on the patches, the method contrasts successful vs. unsuccessful interventions to build a corrective teacher distribution that gets distilled into the strong model.
  • Consistently beats direct-imitation baselines on math reasoning benchmarks - directly fine-tuning on the raw weak-model patches does not transfer as well.

SkillTrace: Multi-Trace Provenance Auditing for LLM-Agent Skill Reuse

ArXiv cs.AI
  • Proposes provenance auditing for reused LLM-agent "skills" (mixed packages of instructions, code, tools, workflows) since ordinary code-clone detectors miss cross-modality reuse.
  • Extracts three independent provenance traces - Expression, Implementation, and Operational (the last captured as a "Skill Operational Graph" of activation/procedure/resource flows) - using an LLM only at ingestion, then deterministic comparison at audit time.
  • Hits AUROC 0.938 / F1 0.898 on an 820-case reuse benchmark, and was run against 36,446 real-world skills in a wild audit.

Universal Pathologies, Conditional Consequences: A Triple-Robustness Analysis of RAG for Multi-Hop Traceability

ArXiv cs.CL
  • Large-scale study (4,440 main runs, 1,200 faithfulness assessments) holding retrieval architecture fixed while varying embedder, corpus, and judge finds GraphRAG has a structural citation-precision problem: 11-15 citation IDs per answer at only 0.12-0.23 precision, despite 0.68-0.87 retrieval recall.
  • Faithfulness is corpus-dependent, not architecture-dependent: GraphRAG faithfulness fell from 74% to 40% across hops on typed-edge data but rose from 42% to 58% on Wikipedia-chain data.
  • Single-LLM-judge evaluation is fragile - GPT-5.4 self-agreement (kappa) was just 0.137 across embedder swaps, with 41% of verdicts flipping; a lightweight learned router hit 0.86 macro-F1 on hop-type classification using only dense embeddings.

Mean-Field Dynamics of Chain-of-Thought Reasoning in Large Language Models

ArXiv cs.CL
  • Models LLM chain-of-thought as a "guided discovery process on a clue graph" and derives a one-dimensional mean-field equation for the fraction of clues discovered over the course of reasoning.
  • Identifies critical "clue tokens" via normalized surprisal (how much a student model is surprised by a teacher model's next token) rather than architecture-specific heuristics.
  • The predicted discovery curves reproduced consistently across many reasoning chains within the same dataset, matching the theoretical equation.

RIG-RoPE: Relation- and Instance-Gated Rotary Positional Encoding with Duration-Aware Temporal Coordinates

ArXiv cs.CL
  • Proposes a fix for multimodal RoPE (M-RoPE): today's height/width rotations get applied to token pairs with no well-defined spatial relationship, causing cross-modal interference.
  • Restricts spatial rotation to tokens within the same visual instance and marginalizes undefined cross-instance displacement instead of defaulting it to zero; adds duration-aware temporal coordinates (unit duration for text, log-scaled for images, extended for video).
  • Adds no learnable parameters and drops into existing attention kernels with minimal overhead - presented as a conceptual framework, not yet backed by empirical benchmark results.
