A quiet Sunday for AI: Simon Willison's Claude-built Battledrones RTS gets a four-player online mode.

Tools & Practical

battledrones v14: Compass Rose, a four player map

Simon Willison
  • Battledrones, a vibe-coded mobile web RTS, adds four-player online play with Compass Rose, an ice-biome map with four-way rotational symmetry. Earlier maps were two-player only.
  • Claude Opus 5.5 built the map and reworked the WebSocket multiplayer code from a single prompt. Players join by share link or QR code, and AI fills any empty slots.
  • The release also adds purple and green teams, and the demo replay shows a four-way AI fight under the 'Goliath Armada' mutator.
