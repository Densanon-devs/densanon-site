Research & Papers

Know What You Don't Know: Agentic Data Synthesis by Evolving Generation Space for Closed-Loop Learning

ArXiv cs.AI
  • Argues that LLM improvement increasingly depends on synthetic experience for interactive agents.
  • Good synthetic data needs trajectories that are executable and verifiable, and that also give real learning value.
  • Proposes evolving the generation space in a closed loop so synthesis targets what the model does not yet know.

SpireBench: Evaluating Long-Horizon Strategic Play of Agentic Models with Slay the Spire

ArXiv cs.AI
  • Notes that most agentic benchmarks are short and episodic, leaving long-horizon strategic reasoning under-studied.
  • Uses Slay the Spire as the testbed for long-horizon play.
  • Aims to separate stored knowledge, reasoning and long-horizon planning in agent performance.

EliteTable: Self-Improving Program Search for Executable Control Policies

ArXiv cs.AI
  • An LLM-guided program-search algorithm that keeps a fixed-capacity table of high-scoring solutions.
  • The table guides new proposals, edits of existing solutions, and remixes of several solutions.
  • Targets executable control policies.

One Model to Loop Them All: Elastic Latent Recurrence for Language Models

ArXiv cs.CL
  • Latent recurrent LMs reuse a shared block of layers, so one checkpoint can trade inference compute for accuracy by changing loop count.
  • Existing models of this type typically fall well short of non-recurrent models at a single loop.
  • The paper proposes an elastic approach to close that gap.

Contextual Correction of Parallel Drafts for Speculative Decoding

ArXiv cs.CL
  • Speculative decoding speeds up inference by having a lightweight drafter propose tokens that the target model verifies in parallel.
  • Diffusion-based drafters make this especially attractive because they draft in parallel.
  • The work adds contextual correction to improve those parallel drafts.
Read full article

AutoIndexer: Flexible-Order Decoding by Training Causal Models on Chains of Edits

ArXiv cs.CL
  • Autoregressive decoding handles non-causally ordered data (cross-references, future dependencies) poorly because output cannot be revised.
  • Diffusion LLMs only partly address this.
  • Trains causal models on chains of edits to get flexible-order decoding.
Read full article

Equal-Budget Monte Carlo Tree Search versus Randomized-Restart List Scheduling for DAG Workflows: A Controlled Equal-Budget Computational Study

ArXiv cs.AI
  • Points out that MCTS is often proposed for DAG workflow scheduling without giving a simpler baseline the same search effort.
  • Runs a controlled equal-budget comparison against randomized-restart list scheduling.
  • A useful reminder to test fancy search methods against simple baselines.
