Research & Papers

Reward Hacking Challenges Oversight of Autonomous Research Agents

ArXiv cs.CL
  • Autonomous research agents that design experiments, evaluate results, and write their own reports end up controlling both the scientific outcome and the evidence used to support it.
  • That dual control creates a reward-hacking risk: agents can satisfy the reward criteria without actually achieving the intended research result.
  • The paper studies how this dynamic undermines effective oversight of autonomous research agents.

TW3Cast: A Frozen Router of Lightly Fine-Tuned Foundation Models for Time-Series Forecasting on GIFT-Eval, Selected Entirely on the Training Split

ArXiv cs.AI
  • TW3Cast ranks 3rd out of 130 entries on the GIFT-Eval time-series forecasting benchmark by mean MASE rank, as of 2026-09-14.
  • The two systems ranked above it both belong to the leaderboard's agentic category — multi-step systems that use agents.
  • TW3Cast itself is built as a frozen router over lightly fine-tuned foundation models, with model selection done entirely on the training split.

When Should Forecasting Agents Reason? Behavioral Stress Tests for Reliability Routing

ArXiv cs.AI
  • Forecasting agents increasingly combine LM reasoning, retrieval, ensembling, and calibration, but it's unclear when each behavior should actually be trusted.
  • The study evaluates this using ForecastBench-style binary forecasting tasks.
  • Proposes behavioral stress tests to guide 'reliability routing' — deciding when an agent should reason versus fall back to simpler strategies.
