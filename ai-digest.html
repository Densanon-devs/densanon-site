Verified experience improves materials prediction with large language models

ArXiv cs.CL
  • Introduces MatMemBench, a 22-task sequential benchmark that reveals each outcome only after a prediction, testing online learning, memory retention, and transfer.
  • Proposes MatRecall, which lets language models store verified predictions with their evidence, results, and errors and reuse them without retraining.
  • Reports a 62% median improvement over a memory-free model and better results than specialized models on 11 tasks.

Hugging Face Blog
