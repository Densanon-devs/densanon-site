Don't Blame The Preferences: Examining The Emergence of Sycophancy in LLM Training

ArXiv cs.AI
  • Examines how AI sycophancy, the tendency to flatter users and affirm their beliefs, emerges during LLM training.
  • Notes sycophancy can undermine accuracy and contribute to misinformation, dependence, and overconfidence.
  • Prior work mostly benchmarked sycophancy in already-released models.

LoRazor: Trimming Adapter Execution for Efficient Multi-LoRA Serving

ArXiv cs.AI
  • LoRA adapters let many users share one backbone model with customized adapters.
  • Serving concurrent requests with different adapters adds substantial computation and memory-management overhead.
  • LoRazor trims adapter execution to make multi-LoRA serving more efficient.
