Learning When to Reason for Text-to-SQL via SFT and DPO

ArXiv cs.CL
  • Addresses the inference-time overhead of applying Chain-of-Thought reasoning uniformly to all Text-to-SQL queries, even simple ones that don't need it
  • Uses SFT + DPO training to teach a model when to invoke expensive reasoning versus answering directly
  • Directly relevant to adaptive-reasoning strategies for tool-calling/agent systems where not every query justifies full CoT overhead

Between Suppression and Collapse: Evaluating Narrative Unlearning with LENS

ArXiv cs.CL
  • Introduces LENS, a framework for evaluating whether machine-unlearning techniques actually suppress disinformation-aligned narrative reproduction versus causing broader capability collapse
  • Identifies a middle-ground failure mode between 'unlearning doesn't work' and 'unlearning breaks the model'
  • Relevant to any product doing safety fine-tuning or content-moderation training where suppressing a specific behavior without collateral damage is the goal
