AI Morning Digest

Research today centers on LLM agents: how well they hand off work between context windows, how they evolve at test time, and how they learn computer use from video. On the tools side, Hugging Face and OpenRouter announced a partnership around open-weight models, LLM 0.37.3 shipped a fix for a SQLite change, and Claude Code v2.1.326 landed.
