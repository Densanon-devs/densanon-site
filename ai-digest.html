Research & Papers

Early Stopping in Large Reasoning Models: A Survey and Empirical Audit of Efficiency and Reliability

ArXiv cs.AI
  • Large reasoning models gain accuracy from longer reasoning traces, but some of that computation may be unnecessary.
  • Early-stopping methods aim to halt once enough reasoning is available to answer.
  • The paper surveys these methods and audits both their efficiency and reliability.

MEMENTO: Learning Context Management for Long Context Processing Agents

ArXiv cs.AI
  • LLM agents working over long inputs must decide what to retain in a fixed-size context window.
  • A common approach is compaction, where the model replaces its history with a self-generated note.
  • The paper argues off-the-shelf agents handle this poorly and proposes learning context management.
Read full article

Reimagining Text-to-SQL: A Survey of Large Language Model Architectures and Strategies

ArXiv cs.AI
  • Text-to-SQL systems translate natural-language questions into executable SQL, opening relational data to non-experts.
  • The survey traces the progression from handcrafted rules and templates to neural encoder-decoder methods and LLM-based approaches.

From Fragmentation to Fusion: A Survey and Roadmap for Omnimodal Embeddings

ArXiv cs.CL
  • Embedding models underpin search, recommendation and most modern AI systems.
  • Research on embeddings remains fragmented by modality, with separate communities working largely in isolation.
  • The paper offers a survey and a roadmap toward unified omnimodal embeddings.
