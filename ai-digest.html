Research & Papers

EntropyMoE: Entropy-Aware Sparse Expert Routing for Tokenizer-Free LLMs

ArXiv cs.AI
  • Proposes entropy-aware sparse expert (MoE) routing for tokenizer-free, byte-level LLMs that group bytes into dynamically sized patches.
  • Targets a gap in current byte-patch architectures, which still apply the same dense feed-forward computation regardless of patch complexity.
  • Extends MoE-style sparse routing, previously mostly a token-based technique, to tokenizer-free models.

ADIAS: Automated Design of Interactive Agentic Systems

ArXiv cs.AI
  • Introduces ADIAS, a framework for automated design of interactive agentic systems via iterative revision, evaluation, and feedback summarization.
  • Argues existing automated agent-design methods are 'candidate-centric' — cross-round experience is organized around individual candidate agents rather than shared, transferable learnings.
  • Frames that candidate-centric organization as the main limitation left unaddressed by prior repair/improvement approaches.
Read full article

Do AI Personas Grow? Analyzing and Benchmarking Personality Evolution in LLM Agents After Life Events

ArXiv cs.CL
  • Benchmarks whether personality-conditioned LLM agents (PC-Agents) should evolve their persona after simulated 'life events,' rather than staying static.
  • Notes PC-Agents are increasingly deployed for emotional support, social simulation, and role-playing.
  • Motivated by the need for lifelong agents that remain coherent over extended, long-running interactions.
