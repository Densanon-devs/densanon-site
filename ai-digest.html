Embeddings Are Overpriced? A Frozen 16 MB Vocabulary for Small and Ternary Language Models

ArXiv cs.CL
  • Replaces Gemma 3 270M's 168M-parameter learned embedding table with a frozen 16 MB table of 512-bit codes, derived by PCA projection and sign binarisation.
  • The frozen-table model matches or beats the learned table on LAMBADA, HellaSwag and PIQA; only ARC-Easy favours the learned one.
  • With a ternary BitNet b1.58 body the whole model is 46 MB and generates 615-740 tokens/s on a laptop CPU in 62 MB of memory.

Where Prefill Expert Reduction Hurts MoE Models: A Few Prompt Tokens Carry Most of the Short-Answer F1 Loss

ArXiv cs.CL
  • Halving routed experts during prefill in MoE models mostly hurts answer form: the model finds the answer but stops answering briefly.
  • The damage concentrates at prompt ends, e.g. the two chat-template tokens in Qwen3-30B-A3B; keeping about 4% of tokens at full experts there holds F1 within one point.
  • Time to first token falls about 14% on 11K-token prompts but not at 2K tokens.

RealCLI: Toward a Unified Evaluation of Human-Agent Collaboration for CLI Agents

ArXiv cs.CL
  • Introduces RealCLI, a paired benchmark (EXEC vs COLLAB) with a five-dimension framework, 22 sub-dimensions and 51 interaction scenario templates.
  • The best frontier-agent configuration scores only 49.2% on average and 37.6% on the joint collaboration score.
  • Agents struggle most with steerability, trust calibration and safety boundary management.

AuditKIT: A Unified Evaluation Spine for Benchmarking, Judging, and Comparing Large Language Models and Agentic Systems

ArXiv cs.CL
  • AuditKIT is a source-available evaluation library that records accuracy, judge scores, runtime, tool use and retrieval quality in one result format.
  • Unobservable evidence is marked unscored rather than counted as failure; agent evals separate parallel from sequential tool calls.
  • Seven case studies show how answer extraction, runtime behavior and metric choice change evaluation outcomes.

Apertus 1.5 Release Notes

Hugging Face Blog
  • Apertus 1.5 extends the 8B and 70B models with image and audio understanding, optional thinking mode, a 262,144-token context (4x), and better tool use, under Apache 2.0.
  • Continued pretraining added 4T tokens (8B) and 2T tokens (70B); multimodality is early-fusion via Emu3.5 image and WavTokenizer audio tokens, extending only the vocabulary.
  • Fully open: weights, data and training details; models understand images and audio but generate only text.
