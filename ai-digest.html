Chain-of-Thought Controllability Can Reduce Chain-of-Thought Monitorability

ArXiv cs.AI
  • Chain-of-thought monitoring is a promising way to detect hidden goals, off-task behavior, and other agent failures.
  • The paper stress-tests the risk that models could deliberately shape their reasoning to be opaque to monitors.
  • Its title reports that controllability of chain-of-thought can reduce monitorability.

Language Models Can Rewrite Their Own Weights

ArXiv cs.AI
  • Models are normally trained once and frozen; improving one again takes another training run, and prompts or scaffolds vanish when a task ends.
  • The paper builds on a prior result showing a frontier model can edit the weights of a much smaller model.
  • Title claim: language models can rewrite their own weights.

RAW: Refusal Aversion in the Wild, A Causal Measurement Method for Deployed LLMs

ArXiv cs.CL
  • Deployed LLMs handle hundreds of millions of conversations a day and refuse a non-trivial fraction of requests.
  • The user-side cost of refusal (whether users disengage, and by how much) is unmeasured on any production model.
  • Introduces RAW, a causal measurement method for refusal aversion in deployed LLMs.
