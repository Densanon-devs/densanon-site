The Coin Flip Judge? Reliability and Bias in LLM-as-a-Judge Evaluation

ArXiv cs.CL
  • Researchers ran repeated identical evaluations across 29 tasks and 10 categories, finding significant run-to-run variance in LLM judges that undermines the trustworthiness of public leaderboards.
  • Unreliable judge scores propagate noise into RLHF pipelines — any team using LLM-as-a-Judge to train reward models is compounding this instability.
  • The study calls for standardized reliability thresholds before LLM judge rankings can be treated as ground truth in model development.

Orchestra-o1: Omnimodal Agent Orchestration

ArXiv cs.AI

  • The omnimodal scope means the orchestrator routes tasks to agents handling text, images, and other input types within a unified pipeline.

Why AI hasn't replaced software engineers, and won't

Simon Willison
  • Arvind Narayanan and Sayash Kapoor argue there is now enough evidence to reject the narrative that AI will automate away software engineering, even though the field is uniquely AI-susceptible.
  • Their analysis separates AI augmenting individual developer output from AI reducing total demand for developers — finding evidence for the former but not the latter.
  • A substantive counterpoint to the widespread assumption that coding assistants are a direct precursor to engineer displacement.
