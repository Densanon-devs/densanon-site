The Hitchhiker's Guide to Agentic AI: From Foundations to Systems

ArXiv cs.AI
  • A full-stack practitioner's reference covering autonomous AI system design from first principles through production deployment — the most comprehensive single treatment of agentic AI architecture to date.
  • Central thesis: great agentic systems are fundamentally about orchestration, not raw model capability — tool use, memory, planning, and error recovery matter more than model size alone.
  • Organized as a book-length treatment, making it a durable reference rather than a narrow empirical paper.

Perfect Detection, Failed Control: The Geometry of Knowing vs. Steering in Language Models

ArXiv cs.CL
  • Finds that detecting a behavior in model activations and steering that behavior are geometrically different problems — knowing where something lives does not mean you can modify it.
  • Challenges a core assumption of mechanistic interpretability: that identification implies controllability.
  • Has direct implications for AI safety work relying on activation patching and representation engineering.

Small edits, large models: How Wikipedia advocacy shapes LLM values

ArXiv cs.CL
  • Demonstrates that coordinated Wikipedia editing by a small volunteer group measurably shifts how major LLMs discuss animal welfare — because Wikipedia is weighted heavily in most training datasets.
  • Quantifies the influence pathway from Wikipedia edits to LLM output, revealing a concrete and underappreciated training data manipulation vector.
  • Raises urgent questions about training data provenance auditing and the outsized influence of well-organized minority editors on AI values at scale.

Diagnosing and Mitigating Compounding Failures in Agentic Persuasion via Taxonomic Strategy Retrieval

ArXiv cs.AI
  • Identifies and categorizes the compounding error problem in multi-step agentic systems: early mistakes contaminate later reasoning in ways that Multi-Agent Debate cannot fully fix.
  • Proposes Taxonomic Strategy Retrieval as a mitigation — retrieving relevant error-recovery strategies at each step rather than letting errors propagate unchecked.
  • Particularly relevant for open-ended persuasion and subjective-domain agents where deterministic debate approaches break down.

Hugging Face Blog
  • A new ASR benchmark designed around real-world audio conditions — accents, noise, domain-specific vocabulary — rather than clean read-speech corpora that existing leaderboards over-optimize for.
  • Exposes significant gaps between leaderboard-topping models and real deployment performance, particularly for named entities and low-resource language variants.

Which tokens does a hybrid model predict better?

Hugging Face Blog
  • AllenAI analyzes where hybrid models (combining attention and state-space layers) outperform pure-transformer baselines at the token level.
  • Hybrid models show the largest gains on long-range dependency tokens and tokens requiring positional memory — exactly the categories SSM architectures were designed to excel at.
  • Provides empirical grounding for when to reach for a hybrid architecture vs. a standard transformer in practice.
