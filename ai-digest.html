Research & Papers

BlockLock: Reproducible LLM Inference with Fixed Batch Geometry

ArXiv cs.AI
  • Keeps shared batched prefill and pins only the numerical path of the protected request, so its output stays bitwise identical when companion requests in the batch change.
  • Tested on five dense models (Qwen3, Llama-3.1, Gemma-3) across A6000, A100 and H100 GPUs; both modes preserved target sequences and Top-20 logit bits.
  • Versus vLLM batch-invariant kernels at equal delivered work, BlockLock-Rows cut latency 53.5-55.0% and raised throughput 115.2-122.2%, with no custom kernels needed.

Resource-Efficient Reinforcement Learning Post-Training through Elastic Expert Parallelism

ArXiv cs.AI
  • In MoE RL rollouts, workers that finish early stay occupied serving experts for longer responses; the paper says about one-third of occupied worker time in the baseline is spent this way.
  • FlexFloor releases workers progressively while keeping the expert computation ongoing responses need, guided by historical response lengths and memory limits.
  • Against the verl baseline the authors report 65.40% more response tokens per unit of occupied compute and 52.74% higher generation throughput, with little change in rollout reward.

The Two-Ledger Model: Transaction-Grain Cost Accounting for Multi-Agent AI Systems Across Clouds, Models, and Systems of Record

ArXiv cs.AI
  • Billing data reaches finance only at settlement grain, so it cannot answer what a given order or claim cost in AI; the paper proposes linking a settlement ledger with an invocation-level attribution ledger built on OpenTelemetry GenAI telemetry.
  • In simulation with synthetic data, cost coverage stayed above 99% even with no trace propagation, and the reconciliation residual recovered planted unmetered spend within 0.24 percentage points.
  • Mid-flight gating cut p99 transaction cost by up to 61% with a 2-4% false-pause rate; results are simulated, not from production.

KDASO: Knowledge-Data Dual-Driven Automated Skill Optimization for Liability Adjudication Tasks

ArXiv cs.AI
  • Targets stale skill libraries, the gap between skill descriptions and execution, and context-budget overflow by continuously evolving the library with induction, trajectory-based refinement and budgeted routing.
  • Evaluated on ride-hailing adjudication, legal liability and logical reasoning judgment; the authors report significant gains over baselines and an 8.96% improvement when transferring skills to concept-similar scenarios.
  • The abstract names no baselines or absolute scores.

New NVIDIA Cosmos 3 Open Models for Interactive World Simulation

Hugging Face Blog
  • Four open-weight checkpoints post-trained from Cosmos 3 Nano: Transfer-Auto for multi-view driving data, Sim-Auto for streaming camera and LiDAR, Sim-Transfer for simulator-to-photoreal video, and Sim-Bimanual for two-arm robots.
