AI Morning Digest

Agent reliability leads the research today: papers ask when a reviewer should be allowed to overrule an agent, how host telemetry improves tool choice, and when tiered inference actually pays off. On the practical side, two small open models target on-device speech and document field extraction, and Datasette Agent gains a side panel for building apps from chat.

Research & Papers

Who May Overrule the Agent? Evidence-Gated Authority in LLM Review Institutions

ArXiv cs.AI
  • Compares broad reviewer override authority with an evidence gate that allows replacement only after the reviewer records a binding-constraint violation and a compliant repair.
  • Tested with two local open-weight models on 96-task spatial-policy and portfolio-transfer benchmarks; the gate sharply reduces corruption of correct proposals while keeping most repairs.
  • With a retain/replace/escalate choice, Qwen stopped corrupting correct proposals but Mistral still replaced all 48, so interface design is not a model-independent safeguard.

Agent Harness Awareness: Turning Verified Telemetry into Operational Control for Tool-Using Agents

ArXiv cs.AI
  • Proposes giving the model a small, fresh, host-verified snapshot of tool health before consequential actions, while the host keeps enforcement and final dispatch authority.
  • In a 216-decision ablation across three frontier models, verified tool-health state cut mean policy regret by 96.9% and raised single-dispatch completion from 69.4% to 95.8%.
  • A 27-decision live replication with injected faults completed 9/9 model-by-fault cases versus 5/9 for a neutral control.

Challenge-Aware Knowledge Recruitment with Meta-Agent Supervision for Medical Multi-Agent Reasoning

ArXiv cs.AI
  • An external CHALLENGER reviews the evolving reasoning trace and triggers targeted retrieval or mid-discussion specialist recruitment when it finds gaps.
  • On the 862-question MedAgentsBench hard subset, accuracy improves on 8 of 9 datasets; upfront retrieval helps little compared with retrieval during reasoning.
  • Challenger-triggered recruitment lifted GPT-4.1-mini on MedQA from 46% to 54%, but naively combining retrieval and recruitment degraded performance.

A Feasibility Condition for Confidence-Gated Tiered LLM Inference

ArXiv cs.AI
  • Derives a closed-form condition for when any confidence threshold can make a tiered cascade beat sending every query to the datacenter model.
  • Gives a signal-free floor and a perfect-signal ceiling, and the condition inverts with the local-to-escalation cost ratio: where local compute is billed, cost binds; where it is sunk, feasibility holds.
  • The paper is theoretical: it reports no experiments and only proposes a co-designed architecture for future testing.

Uncertainty-Aware Resource Orchestration for Agentic AI Systems

ArXiv cs.AI
  • Agentic pipelines have compute, bandwidth and latency demands that only emerge at runtime, so schedulers that need a known pipeline graph do not fit.
  • Conformal Reservation for Orchestration (CRO) turns a sliding window of past execution traces into dispatch-time allocations with distribution-free coverage guarantees.
  • In simulation, CRO was the only evaluated method to meet target coverage at every risk level, and it roughly halved the resource waste of the Chebyshev-bound baseline.

A Closer Look at Phonon TTS. Launching High-Quality On-Device Speech That Tops the Benchmarks.

Hugging Face Blog
  • Two on-device models of about 65 MB and 75 MB; the English 31M model (30.8M parameters) runs with no server or network connection.
  • On the Open TTS Leaderboard CPU streaming benchmark, Phonon 31m EN had the lowest p95 time to first audio at 28 ms (40m: 38 ms; Pocket TTS: 165 ms).
  • The 31m model is English-only under Apache 2.0; the 40m model covers five languages but is licensed CC BY-NC (non-commercial).

Dharma Field Extractor: Any Document In, Structured Fields in JSON Out

Hugging Face Blog
  • A 2B-parameter model built on DharmaOCR-LITE v1.1 with a LoRA adapter trained on 43,384 examples, supporting open and schema-directed extraction.
  • Scored 0.834 on a 1,638-case benchmark from 546 documents, versus 0.816 for Claude Opus 5 and 0.741 for GPT-5.6 Luna (the vendor's own benchmark).
  • The post claims roughly 6x to 147x lower cost than frontier APIs when self-hosted on one L40S at $2.24 per hour; it states no license.
