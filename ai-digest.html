Kākāpō Party

Simon Willison
  • Willison gave Claude Opus 5.5 three kākāpō photos and one prompt asking for an animated pixel-art HTML5 canvas party of at least 20 kākāpō with confetti. The finished slide celebrates the record 2026 breeding season.
  • To turn the page into keynote video, Claude Code wrote a short Playwright script: Chromium at 1280x720, 10 clicks across corners, edges and centre over 15 seconds to set off confetti, recorded straight to a video file.
  • A handy pattern: have an agent build an interactive web artifact, then script a headless browser to record it as video.
