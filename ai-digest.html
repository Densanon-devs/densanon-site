AI Morning Digest

Research today focuses on the reliability of LLM agents and small models: memory filtering, hallucination detectors on quantized on-device models, and chain-of-thought monitorability. On the tools side, Simon Willison shipped a burst of Datasette releases, including a security backport and new plugins built with heavy AI assistance.

Research & Papers

When Should LLM Agents Forget? Memory Filtering Under Query-Critical Resolver Errors

ArXiv
  • Hard-filtering memory systems delete facts classified as stale, which can reduce downstream accuracy.
  • The harm appears when resolver errors delete query-critical facts.
  • Across three models, a controlled sensitivity intervention was used to show the effect.

Does the Ranking Hold? A Practitioner-Facing Audit of Hallucination Detectors on Small, Quantized, On-Device LLMs

ArXiv
  • Hallucination detection is mostly evaluated on 7B-70B research models with abundant sampling budgets.
  • This audit asks whether detector rankings hold on small, quantized, on-device models such as Phi-3-mini.
  • Aimed at practitioners who ship small models on-device.

ArXiv
  • Chain-of-thought is a valuable tool for overseeing AI models, but some architectural shifts could reduce its monitorability.
