Quoting Greg Brockman

Simon Willison
  • At OpenAI many staff wire ChatGPT into Slack, and people dislike being pinged by a coworker's agent — even for work they would happily do if asked directly.
  • The objection is social rather than technical: the request loses the relationship signal that made it acceptable.
  • Practical lesson for anyone shipping agents into shared workspaces — agent-initiated outreach to third parties needs different norms than agent-assisted work.
