r/LocalLLaMA
  • mudler's APEX-I-Compact quant of Gemma 4 26B-A4B hits 38 tokens/sec on a 16 GB RX 9060 XT at 90K context via llama.cpp Vulkan — strong throughput for a single mid-range GPU.
  • Community reports no perceptible quality degradation versus the reference quant, making this a compelling option for local inference on constrained VRAM budgets.
