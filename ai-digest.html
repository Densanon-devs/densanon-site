Cleaning an Agent's History Hurts When the World Still Holds the Error

ArXiv cs.AI
  • Tool-using agents accumulate histories of their own mistakes, and systems that compact, repair or hand off that history decide what it says about those errors.
  • The paper argues that cleaning the history can hurt when the error's effects still persist in the world.

Abstention Is Not Threshold Compliance: Stated Scoring Rules in Open Language Models

ArXiv cs.CL
  • Tests the proposal (Kalai et al.) of stating the scoring rule with an explicit threshold t, so a model answers only when its probability of being correct exceeds t.
  • Probes each requirement of compliance on shared items across 12 open-model checkpoints; the title concludes abstention is not threshold compliance.

ArXiv cs.AI

An LLM Is All You Need: Data-Efficient Adaptation to Full-Duplex, Word-Level Spoken Dialogue

ArXiv cs.CL
  • Full-duplex spoken dialogue (listening while speaking, backchanneling, being interrupted) is usually obtained by training on hundreds of thousands to millions of hours of speech.
  • That is costly; the paper presents a data-efficient adaptation of an LLM to full-duplex, word-level dialogue.
