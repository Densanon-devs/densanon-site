Private by design

There are no DensAssistant servers and no analytics. Capture, transcription, indexing, and recall all run on your own machine with small local models. Audio is recorded only when you choose. Your memory is one file on your disk — encrypted at rest, export it or erase it anytime. The only network calls are the ones you turn on (your own Google account, your own cloud model key).
