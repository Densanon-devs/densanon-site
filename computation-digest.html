Phoronix
  • Meta kernel engineer Rik van Riel's newest memory-management patches yield up to a 12.8x speedup on the gup_test benchmark.
  • The work batches lookups inside follow_page_mask(), the kernel routine that translates virtual addresses to their corresponding struct page.
