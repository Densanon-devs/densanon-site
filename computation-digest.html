China strategically slows exports of critical materials used in semiconductor fabrication to Taiwan — germanium and quartz exports to the region also threaten optical and robotics supply chain

Tom's Hardware
  • China has slowed exports of germanium, quartz-based materials, and rare-earth magnets specifically bound for Taiwan, adding a new front to the ongoing chip supply chain standoff.

Tom's Hardware

Hot Chips 2026: Micron warns HBM wafer penalty is widening with every generation — AI memory uses 3x more silicon than DDR5, company says memory wall is 'getting worse' as prices rise

Tom's Hardware
  • Micron Fellow Raghu Sreeramaneni told Hot Chips 2026 that HBM now consumes roughly 3x more silicon area than equivalent DDR5 capacity, and the gap is widening with each generation.
  • Micron frames this as the "memory wall" getting worse, not better -- more silicon per bit means AI memory pricing has structural upward pressure baked in regardless of demand swings.
  • For anyone speccing AI training/inference hardware, this is a direct explanation for why HBM-equipped accelerators keep getting relatively more expensive even as process nodes shrink.

Hot Chips 2026: Nvidia breaks down 88-core Vera CPU — spatial multithreading benchmarked, 1.2 TB/s SOCAMM2 memory, agentic workloads detailed, and more

Tom's Hardware
  • Nvidia detailed its 88-core Vera CPU at Hot Chips 2026, built for agentic AI data center workloads rather than general-purpose compute.

New AF_ALG Restrictions With Linux 7.3 Due To The Security & Maintenance Nightmare

Phoronix
  • Linux 7.3 further restricts AF_ALG, the userspace interface to the kernel's internal crypto API, continuing a rollback that started with deprecating it outright in Linux 7.2.
  • Maintainers are citing AF_ALG as a "security & maintenance nightmare" -- prior cycles already dropped zero-copy support and hardware crypto offload through the interface.
  • Anything relying on AF_ALG for userspace crypto acceleration (some VPN and disk-encryption tooling) should have a migration plan; the kernel team's direction here is clearly toward removal, not stabilization.
