Bank of England pours cold water on AI productivity boom

The Register
  • The Bank's November Monetary Policy Report estimates AI has raised UK productivity by about 0.5% over three years, or 0.1 to 0.2 percentage points of extra annual growth since early 2024.
  • AI-related activity drove just under a fifth of UK GDP growth since the start of 2026, mostly from building and deploying AI.
  • A cited Gartner survey found 3% of organizations met their AI targets and 38% were over budget, yet 85% planned to spend more next year.

UK ministers seek powers to investigate when AI agents are let off the leash

The Register
  • An amendment to the Cyber Security and Resilience Bill would let ministers set rules on agentic AI: safeguards against loss of control, notifying affected organizations, preserving evidence, and government-led incident reviews.
  • The amendment adds no duties by itself; future regulations need consultation and parliamentary approval. A Pinsent Masons partner says powers could extend to inspectors, searches and seizing documents.
  • Energy suppliers, managed service providers and cloud providers deploying agents could be covered; foundation model developers are not automatically in scope. The bill is not yet law.

ORNL, IBM, and AMD Launch New Co-Design Platform for Quantum Error Correction

HPCwire
  • The first deployment pairs IBM's 120-qubit Nighthawk processor with ORNL's Umbra HPC system, built on AMD Instinct MI355X GPUs and 5th Gen EPYC CPUs.
  • It supports the DOE Quantum Genesis Mission goal of 100 logical qubits running 100,000 hard operations by late 2028; IBM has demonstrated up to 70 logical qubits and 438 hard operations so far.
  • Findings could inform IBM's planned fault-tolerant Starling system.

DMuon: Scaling Matrix Optimizers in Data Parallelism with Topology-Aware Matrix Ownership and Communication

ArXiv CS (Architecture + Distributed + Performance)
  • Muon-style optimizers update whole matrices, which conflicts with sharded data-parallel training; DMuon gives each matrix a single cost-balanced owner for FSDP and HSDP.
  • Tested on up to 256 H100 GPUs, it reports a median 1.05x throughput versus FSDP2 with AdamW and 2.1x versus gather-then-replicate Muon (authors' abstract).
