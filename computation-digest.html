Intel ANV Vulkan Driver Now Enables Descriptor Heaps By Default

Phoronix
  • VK_EXT_descriptor_heap support in Intel's open-source ANV Vulkan driver is now on by default after ~2 months of post-experimental hardening.
  • Descriptor heaps are a DirectX 12-style resource management model — enabling them closes a GPU driver parity gap on Intel iGPUs and Arc.
  • Improves compatibility for workloads ported via DXVK and vkd3d-proton translation layers on Intel hardware.
