AMD unveils Threadripper Halo Station, an AI workstation packing 96 cores and dual liquid-cooled MI350P accelerators

Tom's Hardware
  • AMD's Threadripper Halo Station pairs a 96-core Zen 5 Threadripper CPU with dual liquid-cooled MI350P accelerators (the chassis supports up to four MI350P cards) and 2TB of DDR5 memory.
  • AMD is billing it as 'the most powerful workstation in the world,' claiming it can run trillion-parameter models locally.
  • Worth watching as a benchmark for the ceiling of local/on-prem inference hardware relevant to any local-LLM cost/capability tradeoff discussion (vs. cloud API costs) across our local-AI projects.
