Nvidia Updates Rubin GPU Specs as First Production Hardware Arrives

Hacker News Computation
  • Nvidia lowered Rubin per-GPU HBM4 bandwidth from 22 TB/s to 19.2 TB/s (per-pin rate 10.75 to 9.375 Gbps); capacity stays at 288GB.
  • NVLink bandwidth per GPU fell from 3.6 to 3.0 TB/s while scale-out networking rose from 0.4 to 0.45 TB/s.
  • HGX Rubin NVL8 took the biggest cut: HBM4 capacity 2.3 to 2.0 TB and bandwidth 176 to 130 TB/s, attributed to using 7 of 8 HBM4 stacks per GPU.

INSIGHT-HPC: An Interpretable System Intelligence for Global HPC Telemetry

ArXiv CS (Architecture + Distributed + Performance)
  • INSIGHT-HPC is a multi-plane anomaly-detection system that fuses statistical signals, workload context and physics-based consistency checks into de-duplicated, explained fault episodes.
  • It ingests telemetry from BMCs, job schedulers and facility power distribution units across mixed CPU/GPU clusters.
  • Reported results: precision 0.913, recall 0.817, F1 0.862 and AUROC 0.896, better than the evaluated baselines.

EpiLoRA: Fusing Many LoRA Adapters into Low-Precision GEMMs for Serving and RL

ArXiv CS (Architecture + Distributed + Performance)
  • As base models move to FP8 and 4-bit, per-tenant LoRA adapter kernels take a growing share of each decode step.
  • EpiLoRA, a vLLM plugin, fuses the adapter up-projection into the low-precision GEMM output stage, cutting time per token by up to 24% versus vLLM's fastest configuration on Hopper GPUs.
  • A LoRA RL trainer working directly on FP8 weights lets a 27B model train on a single 48 GB GPU.

China’s Photonic Quantum Computer Among WIC Award Winners

The Quantum Insider
  • USTC's Jiuzhang 4.0 photonic quantum computer won a 2026 World Internet Conference Leading Science and Technology Award, one of 20 honored from over 430 submissions.
  • It used 1,024 squeezed-light beams in an 8,176-mode circuit and detected up to 3,050 photons in a run, versus 255 for Jiuzhang 3.0.
  • The speed claims for boson sampling (over 10^42 years for El Capitan) have been challenged, and the task has no known commercial use.
