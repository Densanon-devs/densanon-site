OpenCL 3.1 Released To Bolster AI & HPC Workloads

Phoronix
  • The Khronos Group shipped OpenCL 3.1 six years after OpenCL 3.0 debuted in provisional form, with the spec explicitly targeting AI inference and HPC compute workloads.
  • OpenCL remains the primary vendor-neutral heterogeneous compute standard as an alternative to CUDA and ROCm — this update keeps it relevant for cross-vendor HPC pipelines that cannot lock into NVIDIA or AMD toolchains.
