Lumentum CEO warns of impending bottleneck on critical material used for silicon photonics

Tom's Hardware
  • Lumentum CEO Michael Hurlston said at the RAISE Summit that the indium phosphide supply squeeze is on track to be worse than the recent memory-chip shortage.
  • Indium phosphide is a key material for silicon photonics and co-packaged optics, both increasingly used in AI datacenter networking.
  • Current fab and material output already lags roughly 30% below customer demand.

OPI Abstraction v0.1 Published With Aiming To Standardize DPU/IPU Ecosystems

Phoronix
  • The Linux Foundation's Open Programmable Infrastructure (OPI) project published its first coordinated release, OPI Abstraction v0.1.
  • The goal is a standardized abstraction layer across DPU (data processing unit) and IPU (infrastructure processing unit) hardware from different vendors.
  • Aims to reduce vendor lock-in for datacenter offload hardware, mirroring how other Linux Foundation projects standardized adjacent infrastructure layers.

Streaming QR codes at 60 FPS achieves nearly 190 KB/s data rate in phone-to-phone tests

Tom's Hardware
  • A proof-of-concept transfers data between phones by streaming QR codes at 60 FPS through the camera, reaching close to 190 KB/s.
  • The method runs entirely in the browser, needing no dedicated app, network connection, device pairing, or any permission beyond camera access.
  • It trades speed for zero-infrastructure simplicity, useful when Bluetooth/Wi-Fi pairing isn't available or desired.
