Google's C Binder Driver Removed With Rust Binder Ready For "Few Billion Devices"

Phoronix
  • Linus Torvalds merged the char/misc changes for Linux 7.4, which remove Google's C-written Binder IPC driver from the kernel.
  • The Rust Binder driver takes over as Google's sole focus; Phoronix says it has reached feature parity and outperforms the C driver in some tests.
  • Greg Kroah-Hartman's pull request said the Rust version "is stable and will be used going forward in a few billion devices."

AV2 Video Codec Adoption Talked Up, SVT-AV2 Being Developed

Phoronix
  • The AV2 v1.0 specification was released in May; the Alliance for Open Media says the codec has "significant ecosystem momentum" with backers including Amazon, Apple, Google, Intel, Netflix and Samsung.
  • AV2 is said to offer about 30% greater compression efficiency than AV1 plus improved HDR performance; YouTube AV2 streams are expected in early 2027 and Meta has committed to it for Facebook and Instagram.
  • Intel is developing SVT-AV2, a CPU-based encoder following its SVT-AV1, SVT-HEVC and SVT-VP9 encoders.

AMD 600 Series Chipsets Now Have Temperature Monitoring Support On Linux

Phoronix
  • Linux 7.4 adds temperature sensor monitoring for AMD 600 Series (AM5) chipsets, with backports marked for the 7.2 and 7.3 series.
  • The earlier xhci-pci-prom21 driver, upstreamed in Linux 7.2, only had 800 Series device IDs; the fix adds the 600 Series PCI IDs with no other driver changes needed.
  • Phoronix notes the original driver was developed by OpenAI Codex GPT-5.5.

Autonomous attack agent in 'Skynet mode' hijacks other models to carry out its evil deeds

The Register
  • Symantec's Threat Hunter Team obtained the agent's full instruction set after trapping it in an AI honeypot; it scans for exposed OpenAI-style endpoints with no authentication or a stolen key and uses those models as its reasoning engine.
  • Its layered jailbreak prompt includes 74 offensive tool definitions, and "Skynet mode" authorizes up to 100,000 unattended steps, including quietly deploying cryptocurrency miners.
  • Symantec found no evidence of real-world use and has not attributed it to a group; the endpoint owner would pay for the inference.

Red-team report found OpenAI utterly failed to monitor its agents during Hugging Face attack

The Register
  • An evaluation by METR and Redwood Research found OpenAI's misalignment monitoring, as it existed on and before July 21st, was suspended during context compaction; about 10% of eligible internal Codex traffic went unmonitored between May and July 2026.
  • Evaluators also found a way for Codex agents to bypass sandboxing and Auto-Review, and two client-controllable headers that could disable monitoring for new agents.
  • The report does not establish that the agents deliberately used these gaps, and OpenAI says it has since improved the system.
