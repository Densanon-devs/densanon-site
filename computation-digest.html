ChainDrop worm crawls into npm supply chain, evades standard defenses

The Register
  • ChainDrop, a variant of the Shai-Hulud worm family, has poisoned at least 444 npm packages.
  • It propagates through malicious tarballs and hooks into developer-tool workflows, specifically evading standard supply-chain defenses.
