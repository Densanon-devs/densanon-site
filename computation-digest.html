AMD Instinct MI350P: PCIe Add-In Card For High Performance Open-Source AI/Compute

Phoronix
  • MI350P is a PCIe 5.0 add-in card — no NVLink, no liquid cooling required — enabling Instinct MI350 compute in standard air-cooled rack servers.
  • Carries 144 GB HBM3E and claims ~40% higher FP16/FP8 theoretical throughput vs. Nvidia H200 NVL at the same PCIe form factor.
  • Positioned as a drop-in upgrade path for existing server fleets ahead of the MI400 series later in 2026.
  • ROCm open-source stack is the primary software target, giving data centers a non-CUDA inference option with mainstream server hardware.

Motherboard Sales 'Collapse' by More Than 25% as Chipmakers Prioritize AI Chips

Tom's Hardware
  • Combined sales of Asus, Gigabyte, MSI, and ASRock are forecast to fall ~28% in 2026 — roughly 11.7 million fewer units industry-wide.
  • Root cause: fab capacity (TSMC especially) is being reallocated to AI accelerator silicon, shrinking supply and raising costs for consumer PC components.
  • Asus alone is projected to ship 5 million fewer boards, a structural shift rather than a demand cycle dip.
  • Signals a sustained squeeze on enthusiast and workstation PC builds for the foreseeable future.

SR-IOV Support Appears To Be Coming For Next-Gen Ryzen AI NPUs

Phoronix
  • AMD has upstreamed AIE4 NPU support targeting Linux 7.2; new patches add SR-IOV virtual function support for those NPUs.
  • SR-IOV would allow NPU resources to be partitioned and shared across VMs — enabling AI workloads in virtualized and containerized environments on client hardware.
  • This is a first for on-chip NPUs; previously SR-IOV was limited to discrete GPUs and network cards.
  • Likely to matter for enterprise laptops running local inference in multi-tenant or VDI deployments.

New GCC Back-End Proposed For WebAssembly

Phoronix
  • A new proposal revives a decade-old effort to add a WebAssembly code-generation back-end directly to GCC, vs. the LLVM/Clang-dominant toolchain.
  • GCC WASM would let projects that already depend on GCC — including Linux kernel tooling and many embedded toolchains — target WASM without switching compilers.
  • Opens the door to GCC-specific optimizations (LTO, profile-guided opts) in WASM output, potentially improving performance of server-side WASM runtimes.
  • Prior attempt failed to merge roughly 10 years ago; success this round would broaden WASM's reach into systems software ecosystems that don't use LLVM.

KDE Plasma 6.7 To Provide A Much Better Experience For CPU-Based Rendering

Phoronix
  • KDE dev Xaver Hugl switched QtWidgets CPU-rendered surfaces from Wayland shared memory (wl_shm) to UDMABUF, eliminating redundant buffer copies.
  • Fixes a known performance gap for apps still using CPU rendering under Wayland — relevant for any Plasma desktop with legacy or non-GPU-accelerated widgets.
  • UDMABUF allows zero-copy DMA transfers between CPU-rendered buffers and the compositor, reducing latency and CPU overhead.
