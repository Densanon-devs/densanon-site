Huawei develops 122TB SSD with new packaging tech to sidestep US sanctions on 3D NAND chips — Chinese firm develops proprietary tech to cram more NAND dies in a smaller footprint

Tom's Hardware
  • Huawei's die-on-board (DoB) packaging mounts NAND dies directly on PCB, bypassing the need for high-layer-count 3D NAND that relies on US-origin technology.
  • The result is a 122TB SSD — a headline density figure demonstrating how sanctions pressure is driving Chinese firms toward novel packaging engineering.
  • This approach trades per-die efficiency for density via sheer die count, a meaningful architectural divergence from Western NAND roadmaps.
