Sebastian Hassinger (The New Quantum Era): why neutral atoms lead the qubit race for now

The Quantum Insider
  • Hassinger points to Vladan Vuletic's team demonstrating 48 logical qubits as evidence neutral atoms currently lead on scale and error correction.
  • He says the platform's biggest weakness is speed: laser-based atom preparation and movement makes gates roughly 1,000x slower than superconducting qubits.
  • His forecast: superconducting qubits take the mid-term lead, with spin qubits and photonics eventually winning out via CMOS-style fabrication at scale.

Apple buried Copland 30 years ago. Now the failed OS boots in a browser

The Register
  • Developer Michael Steil got Apple's abandoned Copland OS booting in a browser using an experimental fork of the DingusPPC Power Mac emulator.
  • He wrote 11 AI-assisted patches to get DingusPPC to boot Copland D11E4, the final developer build distributed in June 1996.
  • Copland was designed as System 7's PowerPC successor around a microkernel, protected memory, and preemptive multitasking; Apple abandoned it in 1996 and acquired NeXT instead.
