China's ByteDance gained access to over 2,000 Nvidia B200 chips through Norway data center

Tom's Hardware
  • UK neocloud Nscale sold GPU capacity to Spring (SG) Pte Ltd, a Singapore-based ByteDance subsidiary, giving the TikTok parent access to more than 2,000 Nvidia B200 chips.
  • Nscale's U.S. IPO filings never named ByteDance directly, referring to Spring only as a 'significant customer' -- even though Spring reportedly accounted for 73% of the UK neocloud's 2025 revenue.
  • The arrangement shows how offshore data centers and layered subsidiary structures can move restricted Nvidia hardware to Chinese firms around export controls.

Alibaba unveils Zhenwu V900 AI accelerator, claims it's 'the most powerful AI chip in China'

Tom's Hardware
  • Alibaba's chip division T-Head unveiled the Zhenwu V900, which it bills as China's most powerful AI accelerator, with 216GB of memory.
  • Alibaba claims roughly triple the performance of its predecessor, the M890.

  • The changes also touch other graphics IP blocks, suggesting bring-up work for a next-generation architecture is underway.

Meta's new transoceanic undersea cable boasts a whopping petabit of bandwidth

Tom's Hardware
  • Meta announced Petal, a new subsea cable connecting the USA and France across roughly 4,300 miles.
  • The link will carry about a petabit of raw bandwidth, which Meta says makes it twice as fast as its previous transatlantic cable.
  • It's another sign hyperscalers are building their own undersea infrastructure to keep up with AI-driven data demand.

China launches 'Supercomputing-1' AI satellite as orbital data centers gather momentum

Tom's Hardware
  • China launched 'Supercomputing-1,' a satellite built to run AI compute in orbit.
  • The goal is onboard processing of Earth-observation data, cutting turnaround from hours to minutes instead of downlinking raw data for ground processing.
