LG Display introduces new OLED deposition technique that uses lithography instead of metal masks - "FLiPP" photolithography delivers 1.6x brightness and 2.4x longer lifespan

Tom's Hardware
  • FLiPP replaces traditional metal-mask OLED deposition, which wastes material, is costly, and can sag under its own weight, with photolithography using photomasks and photoresist layers to define RGB subpixels.
  • The process delivers 1.6x brightness and 2.4x longer panel lifespan versus conventional metal-mask deposition.
  • It also opens the door to higher pixel density and lower manufacturing waste for future OLED panels.

Rust 1.98 Adds Algebraic Floating-Point Methods Akin To "-ffast-math"

Phoronix
  • Rust 1.98 adds algebraic floating-point methods that behave like C/C++'s "-ffast-math", trading strict IEEE 754 semantics for speed.
