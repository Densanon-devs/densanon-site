Tom's Hardware

Linux 7.2 To Revert Back To The FIFO DRM Scheduler Policy Due To "Fair" Regressions

Phoronix
  • Linux 7.2 had switched the Direct Rendering Manager (DRM) scheduler's default from FIFO to a new "fair" policy, intended to improve fairness for AMDGPU and other DRM drivers.
  • The "fair" policy caused measurable regressions, so the default is being reverted back to FIFO before the change ships broadly.
  • An example of a kernel graphics-scheduling change getting caught and rolled back during its own merge cycle rather than shipping with known regressions.
