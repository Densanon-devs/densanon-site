Unkernel and Trynix just transported computing to 2035

The Register
  • Unkernel boots a real Linux kernel on a Zig-written Arm64 runtime compiled to Wasm; unkernel.dev runs Debian 13 with a Wayland GUI in a tab.
  • Trynix runs NixOS on top, bringing Nix packages, OpenGL and ALSA audio to the browser; Doom and Firefox both run.
  • A follow-up, tabc, uses browser tabs as a container runtime so macOS can run Linux containers without a VM.

Smartphone shipments face another bleak year before modest 2028 comeback

The Register
  • Counterpoint forecasts shipments down 14% in 2026 and another 6% in 2027, with a 6% rebound in 2028.
  • The cause is DRAM and NAND output shifting to AI servers; IDC puts memory costs up about 350% year on year.
