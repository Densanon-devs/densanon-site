Computation Morning Digest

Memory is the story today. A startup is betting it can walk around the HBM shortage entirely — Arm cores plus up to 128TB of commodity LPDDR6 instead of expensive high-bandwidth memory — while Microsoft has started shrinking Windows 11's footprint because the DRAM squeeze is pushing 8GB laptops back onto shelves. On the silicon side, Zen 6 is rumored to chase per-core thermal and power tuning to fix microstutter and 1% lows, and the kernel just patched some Zen 5 chips that were misreporting themselves as Zen 6. Elsewhere: the first open-source firmware lands on AM5, Linux 7.3 picks up new Adreno GPUs, and Steam on Linux nudged back over 4%.
