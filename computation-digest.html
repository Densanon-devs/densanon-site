Intel Posts Initial GCC Compiler Patches For AI Compute Extensions "ACE"

Phoronix
  • Intel and AMD co-developed ACE (AI Compute Extensions) to standardize matrix multiplication support across x86, replacing the fragmented AMX/VNNI landscape with a cross-vendor specification.
