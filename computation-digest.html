Physics-Based Validation: The Foundation Of Trustworthy Agentic EDA

Semiconductor Engineering
  • The article argues an agentic workflow counts as physics-validated only if a deterministic signoff engine confirms each step's output before the next step runs. The model proposes and the engine decides.
  • Example: Claude Code drives a nine-stage 3D-IC electro-thermal flow across seven Siemens EDA tools through the Fuse MCP aggregator. Each run takes 100-200 turns over more than 12 hours. The 20x speedup is a vendor claim.

The EU Cyber Resilience Act Reaches Chip And IP Suppliers

Semiconductor Engineering
  • CRA reporting obligations started in September 2026. Full conformity, documentation and CE marking are required from December 11, 2027, for any supplier selling into the EU, wherever it is based.
  • Actively exploited vulnerabilities go through three reports (early warning, notification, final) on the Single Reporting Platform, and manufacturers must do due diligence on third-party components.
